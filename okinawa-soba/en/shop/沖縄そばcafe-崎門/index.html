Okinawa Soba Cafe Sachijo

沖縄そばcafe 崎門
Moromisato, Okinawa CityOld Okinawan houseRating 9/15

Opened in February 2025. An Okinawa soba cafe in a stylishly renovated old house with a cement-tile roof. Its signature is the "Sachijo Soba," with a classic pork-bone broth soup and medium-thick round noodles, topped with fish cake, pig's feet (tebichi), bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku). The cafe-like interior is popular with locals.

This time I visited "Okinawa Soba Cafe Sachijo (Sachijō)" in Moromisato, Okinawa City 😊 I went to eat here in May 2025 😄

The exterior alone has a great atmosphere. It's a building like an old house with a cement-tile roof, with a solid presence. At first glance it looks like a traditional Okinawan home, but the sign says "Okinawa Soba Cafe," giving it a curious charm where tradition and stylishness coexist ✨ I'm told it's a new shop that only opened in February 2025, so my expectations are rising.

Once inside, in contrast to the old-house atmosphere of the exterior, the interior has been newly and cleanly renovated, and a calm, cafe-like space opens up 😌 I visited at lunchtime, and it was bustling with local customers, so I felt this is a popular shop.

Looking at the menu, this time I decided to order the large "Sachijo Soba," named after the shop 😋

Here comes the "Sachijo Soba, large"! First, my usual sip of the soup 😊 It's a soup with a classic pork-bone broth, with a reassuring flavor of the quintessential Okinawa soba.

Next, the noodles. The noodles are medium-thick round noodles with a soft texture. They cling well to the soup and are easy to eat 🍜 The toppings are plentiful: fish cake, pig's feet (tebichi), bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) 😆 The meat has a moderately soaked-in flavor, which is quite nice. However, the braised pork belly and bone-in pork ribs had some parts that still had a bit of sinewy texture.

Rating (9/15): The flavor was "ordinary," a bowl where you can comfortably enjoy classic Okinawa soba 😊 Above all, the atmosphere of the stylishly renovated old house with a cement-tile roof is lovely, and it's understandable why it's bustling with locals. When you come to the Moromisato area of Okinawa City, why not give it a try? 🍜
