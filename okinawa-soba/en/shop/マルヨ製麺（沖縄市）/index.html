Maruyo Seimen (Okinawa City)

マルヨ製麺（沖縄市）
Takahara, Okinawa CityRating 9.5/15

A shop run directly by a noodle factory near Kensō (Okinawa Prefectural Comprehensive Sports Park). The chewy in-house fresh noodles made with Okinawa-grown wheat are high-level, and the top pick is the "Yokubari Soba," topped with a whole-rib bone-in pork rib (hon-soki), soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku).

I ate here in September 2023 😁

This shop is relatively close to Kensō (Okinawa Prefectural Comprehensive Sports Park), but when I googled it, it seems to have shops in various places (;^ω^) This time, as usual without any research, I happened to find this place and went in.

Looking at the menu sign in front of the shop entrance, the "Yokubari Soba" is introduced as the "shop's top pick."

It's described as "a luxurious dish topped with a bone-in pork rib (hon-soki) using one whole pork rib, soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku)." So my order is decided: this "shop's top pick, Yokubari Soba" 😊

It was lunchtime, but probably because it was a weekday, the shop was empty. Entering from the entrance, there's a ticket machine (cash only) near the entrance, and I bought the recommended "Yokubari Soba (medium)" and ordered it from a staff member.

On the table was a laminated sheet explaining what seem to be this shop's selling points, the "fresh noodles" and the "broth."

The explanation says, "Fresh noodles: chewy texture, smooth slurping, made with Okinawa-grown wheat" and "Broth: light yet rich and savory, a luxurious broth of seafood, chicken, and pork, superb with the in-house fresh noodles." Just reading the explanation makes it sound delicious 😋

After a while, the "Yokubari Soba" came.

First, the soup 🤗 According to the explanation it's a blended broth of seafood, chicken, and pork, and since "seafood" came first, I assumed on my own that the bonito base would be strong and took a sip.

But the taste was somehow different from what I imagined? I took another sip to check. Again, it didn't seem like a bonito base? If anything, it felt like a chicken and pork base.

It's a tasty soup, but personally I would have liked a stronger seafood feel (;^ω^) But it's still ordinarily tasty.

Next, the fresh noodles. They're medium-thick straight noodles, and as described, they have a chewy texture, moderate springiness, and are smooth. Delicious noodles 😊

Is that something made from Okinawa-grown wheat kneaded into the noodles? You can see something worked into them.

Rating (9.5/15): It's ordinarily tasty. I thought the fresh noodles in particular are high-level. I thought they're similar to and as high-level as "Kinchichi Soba" that I ate in Onna Village and "Suke Soba" that I ate in Ginowan City the other day. The soup is delicious and goes with the noodles, but personally I thought a stronger seafood style would be better. The toppings are tender but lightly flavored, so personally I'd prefer them to be more flavor-soaked. Why not try the fresh noodles made with Okinawa-grown wheat at least once?
