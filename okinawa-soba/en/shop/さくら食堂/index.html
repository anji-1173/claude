Sakura Shokudo

さくら食堂
Gusuku, NagoAs good as the top picksRating 12/15

A community-rooted diner on the 2nd floor of the Nago Municipal Market. The famous "stamina bean sprout soba" is spicy with garlic and doubanjiang, and hugely filling. The yushi-dofu soba from my previous visit was superb too.

― 2nd visit (November 2024) ―

This article was created with AI (Claude 3.7 Sonnet), with some additions of my own lol🍜✨ I ate here in November 2024😄

I'm introducing "Sakura Shokudo" in the municipal market in Gusuku, Nago😊 It's been quite a long time since I last ate here😆

Since it's a shop inside the market, you get the feeling it's a place loved by the locals.

When you enter, there's an old-fashioned diner atmosphere.

Last time I ate the "yushi-dofu soba" and introduced it as being every bit as good as my recommended shops, and this time it's the "stamina bean sprout soba" I'd wanted to eat when I came next😄

Looking at the menu, the words "stamina bean sprout soba," the house specialty, jumped out at me. This is the dish I'd been thinking "next time" about ever since I ate here last time, so I had no choice but to order it🔥

After a while the "stamina bean sprout soba" appeared! What first catches the eye is the amount of bean sprouts and chopped meat heaped on top of the noodles😳 That's hugely filling!

First, as usual, a gulp of the broth. Is it a combined bonito and pork broth? Or maybe it's agu broth. It's a delicious broth with plenty of umami😋

Next, the noodles. They're medium-thin and straight with a softish texture, but they suit this dish! Just plain tasty.

And what's distinctive is the toppings. The heap of bean sprouts and chopped meat is seasoned with garlic and doubanjiang, and the spicy flavor is irresistible! Eating it makes your body heat up and you start sweating💦 This is truly the stamina type! A powerful bowl, just as the name says.

I ordered the large size, but the volume was more than I imagined, and I almost gave up partway through😅 Next time I think a small size will be plenty. Even so, it'll surely be quite satisfying.

Rating (12/15): It's a shop in a community-rooted location, the municipal market in Gusuku, Nago, but the "stamina bean sprout soba" is a distinctive dish in a different league from ordinary Okinawa soba. Eating it while sweating from the spiciness seems addictive. I ate at this shop for the first time in a long while, and as expected, I recommend it. The volume is huge too, so I recommend going hungry! It's worth trying at least once😊

― 1st visit (July 2020) ―

It was introduced in the "Nago City Soba Map," so I tried eating here in July 2020😀

I ordered the "yushi-dofu" that was introduced in the soba map.

The agu broth, the pride of local Nago, has a strong flavor, with flat noodles. The toppings are generous too: wakame, sea lettuce (āsa), braised pork belly, and yushi-dofu (soft Okinawan tofu)😀 Delicious.

The menu introduced the "stamina bean sprout soba" as Sakura Shokudo's specialty, so I'd like to try that next🤤

Rating (12/15): Generous and delicious. This one is also recommended, so please give it a try.

PS: There's a certain amount of free parking in the municipal parking lot, so be careful not to park at a nearby coin parking lot without checking for open spaces.
