En Soba

縁そば
Gusukuma, Urasoe City (inside Makeman)Rating 10/15

Moved from Gakuen-dori in Iso to inside Makeman Urasoe. The signature En Soba has a broth with the umami of pork bone, kelp, and bonito flakes and chewy whole-wheat noodles. A noodle shop with mazesoba, ramen, and even a fried chicken set.

― 2nd Visit (March 2025) ―

I went to “Mendokoro En Soba” inside Makeman in Gusukuma, Urasoe City 😊 I went to eat in March 2025 😄

What I had this time was the “En Soba, large,” which has the same name as the shop. I had eaten here before, when it was on Gakuen-dori in Iso, Urasoe City, so this is my first visit since then. This was my first time since it moved here, so I headed over looking forward to how it would be 🚗✨ Since it's inside Makeman, there's almost no worry about parking, which is really helpful. It's easy to stop by while shopping, and the location seems easy for families and locals to use.

In front of the shop is a big yellow “En Soba” noren, which was quite eye-catching 🌼 As it's a restaurant inside a home improvement store, at first I imagined something closer to a food court, but it was actually a proper standalone shop, with a calm interior. The wood-grain floor and tables give warmth, and the seating seemed relatively roomy. It looks easy to use at lunchtime or after shopping, and I like the atmosphere of casually eating Okinawa soba 🍜

Looking at the menu, there was quite a variety just in Okinawa soba. En soba, tebichi soba, braised pork belly soba, soft-bone pork ribs soba, meat and bean sprouts soba, small soba, and more were lined up, and there are also seafood mazesoba, Taiwan mazesoba, ramen dishes, and even a fried chicken set 😳 Rather than an Okinawa soba shop, it's more of a noodle place where you can eat a wide range. The fried chicken set also looks good value, and seems good for those who want to eat heartily. This time I ordered the signature-looking “En Soba, large” without hesitation.

The bowl that arrived after a while looks like a classic Okinawa soba just from appearance ✨ On a brownish broth sit medium-thin noodles, braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), fish cake (kamaboko), shredded egg, and green onions, a look that is not too flashy but well put together. The bowl is also subdued, matching the broth's color, and looked delicious.

Starting with the broth, it seems to be made from pork bone, kelp, and bonito flakes, with firm umami. I can feel the mellowness of the pork bone, but overall the bonito flavor comes slightly to the front 🐟 I thought it's still a delicious broth as ever 😋

The noodles are medium-thin, slightly wavy noodles kneaded with whole-wheat flour. When lifted with chopsticks they look smooth, and when eaten they're chewy 🍜✨ They're slurpy, easy-to-eat noodles. Since they contain whole wheat, they have a slightly different presence from ordinary noodles 😊

Since the toppings include braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs, it looks satisfying as well. The braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs are tender too 🥩 My honest impression is that both are tender but the sinewy texture bothered me a little. Because the impressions of the broth and noodles are so good, if the meat toppings had been finished a bit more meltingly, the overall satisfaction would have risen further. The flavor isn't bad, so it was truly just a little short, I felt 🤔

The fish cake, shredded egg, and green onions are included too, giving the proper reassurance of Okinawa soba. Especially the shredded egg gives a touch of brightness to the look 🌿 The aroma of the green onions suits the broth, balancing the whole. Rather than a flashy impact, it's a bowl that lets you take a breather in the middle of shopping. Including its location inside Makeman, I think it's an Okinawa soba shop easy to use in daily life. Even with a family, there are many menu choices, so those who want Okinawa soba, those who want mazesoba, and those who want to add fried chicken could each be satisfied 👍

Rating (10/15): This time's “En Soba, large” was an easy-to-eat bowl with a well-balanced broth and slurpy, chewy noodles 🍜 While both pork bone and bonito umami can be felt, the broth with a slight bonito lean has stability. The noodles with whole wheat have character and went well with the broth. However, it was a pity that the braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs toppings had a little sinewy texture. If that were improved, it would become a highly satisfying bowl. Even so, I'm glad I can eat Okinawa soba of this level while shopping at Makeman 😊 It's easy to enter, the menu is extensive, and it was a shop where I want to try other menu items again.

Noodles: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Soup: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Toppings: ★★★☆☆（3）

― 1st Visit (October 2023) ―

I ate here in October 2023. This shop is a participating shop in the “13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29),” and I went as the 4th of 34 shops 😊

It seems this shop's lease expires at the end of December 2023, but the relocation site hasn't been decided. Because of that, and the stamp rally running until December, I decided to go early 😁

The stamp rally booklet featured the “En Soba,” which has the same name as the shop, so I had planned to order the “En Soba,” and the menu also introduces it as the owner's recommendation.

This shop has not only Okinawa soba but also mazesoba and ramen. Previously I thought the Okinawa soba at the ramen shop “Ajisen Ramen” on the Okinawa Expressway (Nakagusuku SA) was of quite a high level, so I can expect this shop's Okinawa soba too 🤗

First the broth. The moment I drank it: “Delicious” 😊 The menu says “Our proud broth, carefully simmered with selected pork bone, kelp, and bonito flakes,” and it's a gentle, delicious broth with a bonito stock lean.

Next, the noodles. The explanation said “Please enjoy the chewy, slurpy texture of the noodles kneaded with whole-wheat flour,” and indeed, they were chewy thin straight noodles, and these were delicious too 🤤

Rating (10/15): There might be a rule that “Okinawa soba shops that also serve ramen are high level” 🤗 The bonito-based broth and chewy whole-wheat noodles were high level and delicious. However, the braised pork belly was a little sinewy, which was a pity~. The relocation site after the end of December 2023 hasn't been decided, but I think it's delicious Okinawa soba, so I hope they somehow find a new location and keep operating.

Noodles: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Soup: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Toppings: ★★★☆☆（3）
