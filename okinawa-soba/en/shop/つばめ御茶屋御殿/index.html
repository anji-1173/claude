Tsubame Uchaya Udun

つばめ御茶屋御殿
Asato, NahaRating 9/15

A charming shop named after "Uchaya Udun," a villa of the Ryukyu royal family. In an elegant interior decorated with artworks such as Ryukyu lacquerware and pottery, you can enjoy soki soba made with Kamehama Soba's thin straight noodles.

I went to eat here in November 2024 😄

This time I made my way to "Tsubame Uchaya Udun" in Asato, Naha ✨ The "Uchaya Udun" in the shop's name apparently refers to a villa built for the royal family's guests in the era of the Ryukyu Kingdom 🏯 With a name that sounds so prestigious, I visited wondering what kind of Okinawa soba was waiting for me 😋

The entrance is a charming wooden gate, as if you're stepping into an old Okinawan mansion 🌿 If it weren't for the red banner reading "Okinawa Soba," the atmosphere is so calm you'd never guess it was a soba shop ✨

When you step inside, a space with the warmth of wood opens up, and there's tatami seating too, so it seems like a place where you can spend a relaxed time 😊 The walls are decorated with artworks such as Ryukyu lacquerware and pottery, giving it a truly elegant atmosphere 🎨 It's a space where not only tourists but locals too can enjoy a meal in peace.

Opening the menu, there's a simple yet carefully selected lineup of Okinawa soba 🍜 From it, this time I ordered the "Soki Soba" 🎶 At ¥1,200 the price is set a bit high, but if they're particular about their craft, it's an understandable price range 💰

After waiting a while, an elegant-looking soki soba was brought to the red lacquered table 🍲 The broth is a stock combining pork bone, bonito flakes, and kelp, and taking a sip, it's finished with a balance where neither the bonito nor the pork bone asserts itself too much ✨ However, personally I might have liked one of them to assert itself a little more 🤔

The noodles are Kamehama Soba's thin straight noodles, which are rare for Okinawa soba 🍜 These have a unique texture, going down smooth and slippery, and when you bite them you can enjoy a moderate springiness 😆 They give a clean, refreshing impression.

And then, the main pork spare ribs (soki). They look big and seem thoroughly simmered, but when I took a bite, the seasoning was on the light side, and the slightly dry texture bothered me 🤔 Since I'd been expecting soki that's tender and falls apart, it was a bit of a letdown 💦 With less fat, some people might find them easy to eat, but personally I wanted them a bit juicier 😅

Overall, the shop's atmosphere and the quality of the noodles are high, but my impression was that the soki's preparation was a bit of a shame 😌 You can enjoy a leisurely meal in the calm space inside, so it might be the perfect shop to drop by between sightseeing stops 🏖️

Now, my rating for Tsubame Uchaya Udun's soki soba this time is as follows ✨

Rating (9/15): Overall, I'd call it a "decently tasty Okinawa soba" 😋 If it had a few more distinctive points, it would probably be an even more memorable bowl ✨ I'm looking forward to my next Okinawa soba tour too ♪🥢
