Jagaru Soba

謝苅そば
Yoshihara, Chatan TownRating 10/15

A popular shop on the heights of Chatan where you can see the sea in the distance from the counter. A light bonito-broth soup with a choice of boiled or fresh noodles, and large, tender soki. Lots of autographs from Kings players too.

I ate here in August 2022 😊

Since I went at lunchtime, there was a line of customers. The parking lot in front of the shop was full, so I parked at the “Jagaru Park” parking lot across the street and walked a little to enter 😊

This time I ordered the “Jagaru Soba,” which shares the shop's name.

A light bonito-broth soup, with noodles you can choose between “boiled noodles” and “fresh noodles,” and I chose fresh noodles. The soki is tender and large. Because the shop is on the heights of Chatan Town, from the counter seats you get a view of the sea far away (around Mihama) 😊

Also, inside the shop it's full of autographs of players of the B.League (basketball) Ryukyu Golden Kings and of entertainers 😁 It was a line again today, so it's a popular shop.

Rating (10/15): Both the fresh noodles and the broth are just plain tasty, but unfortunately the time from ordering to serving was by far the slowest of all the shops I've eaten at so far (T_T) Other shops also had lines, but food came out relatively quickly after ordering, so Jagaru Soba's slowness stood out, which I thought was a pity.

Noodles: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Soup: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
