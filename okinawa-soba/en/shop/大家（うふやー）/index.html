Ufuya

大家（うふやー）
Nakayama, NagoOld Okinawan houseRating 9/15

An old-house soba restaurant nestled at the end of a path lined with red torii gates in the vast "Ufuya no Sato" in the mountains of Nago. Seats overlooking a garden and soft-bone pork rib soba with homemade thick noodles. Hugely popular with tourists, with a record-level wait. There's also the famous dessert "Ufuya Ball."

I went to eat here in March 2024 😄

The photo above is the view from where I sat after being shown inside; it's garden-like 😄　It's got a ton of atmosphere 😆

However, there were a crazy number of tourists before I was shown inside, and I waited quite a while. I experienced the No.1 wait of all the Okinawa soba shops so far. (Just under an hour?)

At this shop, it seems you order by smartphone. They make full use of technology, which is nice. I think it's good and something other shops don't have 👍

This time I ordered the "Soft-Bone Soki Soba" from the menu. I was curious about the cream puffs too, so I also ordered the "Ufuya Ball" for dessert 😁

The soft-bone soki soba arrived 😁　First, I taste the soup. It feels like a blend of bonito and pork, a gentle flavor with a bit of bonito coming through. Solidly tasty.

Next, the noodles. The noodles seem to be homemade within the Sato, a thick straight type with a chewy feel. These are solidly tasty too 😋

After savoring the Okinawa soba, I have the dessert, the "Ufuya Ball." It has a name, but it's a cream puff, right lol

This "Ufuya Ball" seemed to be promoted quite a bit inside the shop, so I thought it might have something special about it, but sorry, I thought it was like an ordinary store-bought cream puff (^0^;)　Solidly tasty.

I came to this Ufuya to eat Okinawa soba, but the grounds seem to be quite large, with various facilities (sorry, I didn't research it well, so please google for details 🙏).

The sign says "Ufuya no Sato," so the grounds feel like a single village, a "sato."

Rating (9/15): I think it's solidly tasty, but it seems to be very popular with tourists, and I waited quite a while (^0^;)　I thought "Suu~gii~jii," where I ate in February 2024, was my all-time No.1 wait, but here I waited even longer (^0^;)

So I thought it's best to go with plenty of time to spare, or make a reservation if you can (I'm going from vague memory, but I think only group reservations are possible, and the menu becomes group-only).

The building and the path leading to it are quite Instagrammable, so why not experience it at least once 😆
