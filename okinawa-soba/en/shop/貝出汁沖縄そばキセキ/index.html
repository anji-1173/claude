Kaidashi Okinawa Soba Kiseki

貝出汁沖縄そばキセキ
Nakama, Urasoe (also Chatan branch)UniqueRating 9/15

A stylish, popular shop that uses a LINE queuing system. A new-style bowl of creamy pork-bone white broth combined with shellfish broth, topped with fried burdock and black wood-ear mushrooms. There is also a Chatan branch in American Village that serves a rich shrimp broth.

― Urasoe Branch (November 2023) ―

I ate here in November 2023. This shop is a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (Oct 1, 2023 – Feb 29, 2024)," and I went as the 15th shop out of 34 😊

This shop has its own parking lot, and there are also several parking spaces right across from it (in the photo), but lots of customers were lined up near the entrance. It was lunchtime, but it was a weekday, so you can tell it's a hugely popular shop.

However, since people were sitting on the benches outside, I couldn't tell whether they were customers resting after eating or people waiting, so I went inside and asked a staff member.

I was told to add the shop as a friend on LINE using the QR code near the entrance and wait for my turn that way.

Registering your place in line through LINE feels really cutting-edge, which is nice 🤗 I've been to about 100 Okinawa soba shops so far (as of November 2023), but this is the first time I've seen a system like this. Making good use of technology, nice.

I registered by following the LINE steps. I was set as the 5th customer in line lol

When my turn came, I got a notification on LINE. After receiving it and going inside, a staff member explained the various menu items in front of the ticket machine. It seems they do this because it's a new shop.

The interior is new and stylish too, and there was even a young woman dining alone. I noticed a mix of customers, such as office workers from the area and young women.

In the middle of the staff member's explanation, I said, "I'll order the Okinawa soba introduced in the stamp rally booklet," and decided to order the "Kaidashi Okinawa Soba (single item)."

However, while the queue registration uses LINE and all that technology, the ticket machine is, amazingly, cash only (;^ω^) Since they've gone this far, I wish they'd add cashless payment here too. It's a new shop, so I found this part disappointing.

In the stamp rally booklet you can apparently choose from "shellfish broth, shrimp broth, and niboshi (dried sardine) broth," but I went with the "shellfish broth," which matches the shop's name and seems to be their pick lol

After a little wait, the "Kaidashi Okinawa Soba" I ordered arrived. It looks Instagram-worthy, and it should definitely appeal to female customers.

First, as always, I tasted the soup 😊 I had assumed the white color of this soup came from the shellfish broth, but since the explanation panel says "a perfect balance of creamy pork-bone white broth and shellfish broth...," I think the cloudy white comes from the pork-bone white broth. It reminded me of the cloudy white soup at "Nakaza-ya" in Naha that I ate at before.

When I drank the soup, I had imagined a soup with a strongly pronounced shellfish broth, but the shellfish broth wasn't that noticeable, and the pork-bone white broth base was strong. Since the dish is named "shellfish broth," I felt the shellfish broth could stand out more.

There was quite a bit of shellfish broth aroma coming from inside the shop, so it's a little disappointing (;^ω^)

Next, the noodles. The medium-thick noodles are slightly wavy, and the explanation says "fresh noodles are used, not boiled noodles." However, I didn't really feel the slipperiness and chewy springiness I've experienced from fresh noodles at other shops; they had a rather soft texture.

The toppings feel new and different too 😁 First, the long, thin topping that had caught my eye from the stamp rally booklet photo (lol). I had assumed it was "shellfish?", but according to the explanation panel it's "fried burdock" (;^ω^) When I ate it, it was indeed burdock lol

The black wood-ear mushrooms are also a topping you don't find at other shops. The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) was tender and tasty in the ordinary way, but although the explanation says it's "seared after the order comes in," I couldn't really detect much of that aroma here either.

The flavor of the seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) I had at "Okinawa Pork Soba," which I ate at during the stamp rally the other day and introduced in my "Holds Its Own Against the Recommendations" corner, is still vivid in my memory, so I'm sorry to say this one felt like it lacked impact 🙇

After finishing, I also received a "one-topping service coupon" via LINE notification, and since this shop is apparently a sister shop of the nationwide Hokkaido/Sapporo soup curry specialty restaurant "Rojiura Curry SAMURAI.," they gave me a discount coupon for soup curry.

Rating (9/15): Well, it's perfectly tasty, but since the shop name says "shellfish broth," I couldn't help thinking the soup would be better with more shellfish broth than pork bone. The atmosphere inside is very stylish and it's a popular shop, but since I came with high expectations, it was a bit disappointing. It was also a pity that the ticket machine doesn't support cashless payment. Also, the expiration date is the end of November 2023, but you can still get the discount coupon for the Hokkaido/Sapporo soup curry specialty restaurant "Rojiura Curry SAMURAI." right now, so why not give it a try? 😊

Noodles: ★★★☆☆（3）
Soup: ★★★☆☆（3）
Toppings: ★★★☆☆（3）

― Chatan Branch (October 2024) ―

This article was created with AI (ChatGPT 4o) and partly supplemented by me 😁

I ate here in October 2024. This shop is a participant in the "14th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (Oct 1, 2024 – Feb 28, 2025)," and I went as the 1st shop out of 33 😊

It's my first time at "Kaidashi Okinawa Soba Kiseki." since I ate at the Urasoe branch during last year's stamp rally 😆 I didn't know they had opened a second shop in American Village in Mihama, Chatan Town.

"Kaidashi Okinawa Soba Kiseki. Urasoe Branch" was the most popular shop in last year's stamp rally. This Chatan branch is just as attractive, with an equally refined atmosphere ✨ With its stylish exterior that suits a tourist destination, it seems likely to be loved by tourists as well as locals.

What I ordered this time was the "Rich Shrimp Broth Okinawa Soba" introduced in the stamp rally booklet, with extra shrimp topping as the staff member recommended, plus an egg and seared braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) (^0^;).

It looks vivid and luxurious right from the start. The colorful toppings and the rich-looking soup blend beautifully in the bowl. I can't help but get my hopes up.

First, a sip of the soup. True to its name, the rich shrimp flavor spreads through my whole mouth 🦐 This fragrant umami is a type you can't easily find in conventional Okinawa soba. I think it's an irresistible bowl for shrimp lovers. However, if you're expecting the flavors typical of Okinawa soba, such as pork bone and bonito flakes, you may be a little surprised. This soup specializes in shrimp, so it comes across as a completely creative-style Okinawa soba.

The noodles were medium-thin, gently wavy fresh noodles, and their softness was distinctive 😊 In some parts I felt they were a little low on chew to stand up to the richness of the soup.

The toppings were impressive too ✨ The presence of the fried burdock is unique again. Its texture and fragrant flavor served as an accent and gave a slightly healthy impression. It's quite an interesting idea.

Also, adding extra shrimp topping further heightened the unity with the soup. Besides its luxurious look, you can enjoy an indulgent feeling that shrimp lovers will love. Overall the balance of toppings was good, and I got the impression that they are proposing a new form of Okinawa soba 🌈

The interior was very stylish, with an atmosphere that seems popular with women and couples 💕 The Urasoe branch was refined too, but the space here at the Chatan branch is just as lovely.

Rating (9/15): So, my overall impression is that it was ordinarily tasty, and the "Rich Shrimp Broth Okinawa Soba" was a very unique and delicious bowl, but for me it settled into an "average" impression 😌 The rich shrimp flavor is distinctive and offers a fresh experience, but it may feel slightly lacking for those seeking the charm of traditional Okinawa soba. Even so, I think this creative soba is worth trying at least once. I'd especially recommend it to shrimp lovers.

Noodles: ★★★☆☆（3）
Soup: ★★★☆☆（3）
Toppings: ★★★☆☆（3）
