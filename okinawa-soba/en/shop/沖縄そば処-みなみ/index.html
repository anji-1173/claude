Okinawa Soba-dokoro Minami

沖縄そば処 みなみ
Uechi, Okinawa City (near Okinawa-Minami IC)Rating 9/15

A new shop that opened in November 2022 in a renovated detached house. A combined broth where the pork bone slightly wins, and the meal ticket machine that accepts cashless payment is convenient. Close to Okinawa Arena and the expressway IC, so it's handy on the way back from events too.

I ate here in August 2023 😊

First, when you go into the shop, the meal ticket machine in the photo is installed right in front. The inside seems newly renovated, and when I googled it later, it seems it had just opened in November 2022.

The meal ticket machine supports cashless payment, so I could pay quickly. Convenient 😊　There are Okinawa soba shops that support cashless payment, but there are still many old-fashioned “cash only” shops, so I hope they progress soon lol

This time I ordered the “soki soba (large),” located in the upper row on the right side of the meal ticket machine and which seemed to be the recommendation.

To enjoy the soba's aroma, I checked the flavor 😊　From the smell, I thought the soup was “bonito broth?” and took a sip.

However, it wasn't bonito; if anything, I tasted a bit of a pork-bone-based flavor. I think it's a combined broth of pork and bonito, but maybe the pork bone slightly wins?

The aftertaste after drinking the soup had something like an acidity; just very slightly, but I could also feel a sour sensation. It's just plain delicious, but other shops don't have an aftertaste like this.

The noodles are a thin, wavy type. The texture is on the soft side 😊

The soki is tender too and just plain delicious, but the taste feels a little restrained. The meat's texture is sinewy in places too, and after eating I needed a toothpick to clean between my teeth 😊

Rating (9/15): It's just plain delicious, but the soup's aftertaste bothered me somehow. It seemed to be bustling with local customers. It supports cashless payment (this time I paid with QUICPay), and unusually for an Okinawa soba shop, I could get a receipt issued from the meal ticket machine, so I'd like to turn it into money too using the method in my other article (“[Points hacking] How to turn receipts into money (small change) (super steady type)”) 😄　This shop is close to the expressway IC, the venue for the basketball World Cup being held this month (August 2023) (Okinawa Arena), and the ground where a professional baseball team (Hiroshima Carp) holds its winter camp (also used for the high school baseball prefectural qualifiers), so how about stopping by once when you attend a sports event 😄
