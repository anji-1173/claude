Okinawa Subayaa Taiyo

おきなわすばやぁ太陽
Maehara, Uruma City (Uru Marche)Rating 9/15

An Okinawa soba shop in the food court of Uruma City's farmers' market facility "Uru Marche". It's fun that you can choose your noodles, such as curly noodles, fresh sea lettuce (āsa) noodles and fresh mozuku noodles, and they also have goat soba and nationwide shipping. You can casually enjoy a bowl at the open outdoor seats.

I ate here in October 2024 😄

This time I visited "Okinawa Subayaa Taiyo" in the food court of "Uru Marche" in Uruma City!

🌺Various shops are lined up in the food court, and they all look delicious, so it's hard to decide 😋.

What I chose from among them was, of course, the "Soft-bone Soki Soba" at Okinawa soba stamp rally participant "Okinawa Subayaa Taiyo". Normally I always order the Okinawa soba introduced in the stamp rally booklet, but for this shop it was "goat soba" that was introduced, and after eating goat soba before at "Mukashi Mukashi" in Nago City, the impression of the gamy smell afterward stayed with me strongly, so somehow I couldn't choose it (^0^;)　I gave up on it this time (^0^;)　While I was eating it, I thought it had no quirkiness (^0^;)　It's the after-meal part that bothers me 😄

On top of that, for the noodles I tried choosing "fresh mozuku noodles" 🎶. I'm looking forward to the unusual noodle texture!

While waiting for the soba at the roofed outdoor seats, a pleasant breeze blew in and I was in the best mood ☀️. Eating in an open space like this on a nice day feels really good, doesn't it. It really boosts the vacation mood too ⛱️.

And now, the long-awaited soba arrives ✨. The bowl is full of jiggly soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki)! The broth has a clear color, and the fresh mozuku noodles look delicious too 🍜. My heart is full of anticipation: time to eat!

First, a sip of the broth. The pork-bone-based stock comes through solidly, with a deep flavor 😊. However, overall the saltiness is a bit on the strong side, so opinions may be divided. But it might be perfect for people who like strong flavors!

Next I have the "fresh mozuku noodles". They have a chewy, springy texture that's satisfying to eat ✨! However, to be honest, the "mozuku feel" is a bit restrained, and the flavor is on the faint side? 🤔　That said, there's mozuku in it as a topping, so eating them together, you can properly enjoy the mozuku. The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) 🍖 are tender and melt-in-your-mouth too 😳

Rating (9/15): My overall impression is "fairly plain tasty", but with the openness of eating outdoors and the liveliness of the whole food court added in, I had a fun time 😊. Also, Uru Marche has lots of other gourmet food too, so next time I'd like to try other shops as well 🍔🍣.

"Okinawa Subayaa Taiyo" was a shop you can drop into casually and enjoy Okinawa soba in a relaxed way 🍜. When you head out to Uru Marche, please do pay it a visit! 🌈
