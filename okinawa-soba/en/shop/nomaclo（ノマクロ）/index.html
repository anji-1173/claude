nomaclo (Nomakuro)

nomaclo（ノマクロ）
Onaga, Nishihara TownRating 9/15

An Okinawa soba shop like a select shop, with gentle white walls and wooden textures. The everything-on-top Nomakuro Soba with a creamy, cloudy white pork bone broth comes with freshly fried tempura. You can also enjoy hand-drip coffee and oden.

*This article was created with AI (ChatGPT 5 Thinking) and partly added to by me 😆

I ate here in January 2025 😄🌺 I went to "Nomaclo (Nomakuro)" in Onaga, Nishihara Town.

First of all, the thread-like logo on the noren (shop curtain) is cute, and it suits the exterior with its gentle white walls and wooden textures perfectly. It's an Okinawa soba shop, but beyond the glass door there's also the atmosphere of a select shop, and it feels like everything is pulled together in the world of "Nomaclo = Nomakuro".

When I sat down, the table guide said "Water is self-service" and "Please return your dishes to the return counter", a pleasant smartness like a cafe. There were also the business hours and an Instagram QR code, giving off a modern feel. Looking over the menu, besides Okinawa soba there were coffee, non-alcoholic drinks, craft beer, and the words "takeout available". On top of that there's "Nomakuro Oden" and "tebichi (pig's feet)" too, which got me a little excited. This time I ordered the "Nomakuro Soba (everything on top)", which shares the shop's name.

The bowl that arrived on a round tray first of all looks great in photos with its polka-dot pattern. The broth is a cloudy white pork bone stock type. Taking a sip, it's creamy. The savoriness of the bones spreads gently. It's on the rich track that reminds me of "Tsukikuruchi" in Uruma City, with depth too, and I felt it was carefully simmered.

The noodles are medium-thick and loosely curly. Lifted with chopsticks they shine glossily, and in your mouth they have a pleasant chewy spring that pushes back against your teeth. Thanks to the curls they coat themselves in broth, and when you slurp, the sweetness of the pork bone arrives softly at the same time. They're boiled on the slightly soft side, and combined with the creaminess of the broth, they create a "gentle sense of unity".

As the name says, the toppings are everything on top, and lively to look at too. The pig's feet (tebichi) are melt-in-your-mouth, and the jiggly bounce of the collagen is fun. The fat is well rendered, so you can eat it easily without feeling heavy. The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) are prepared lightly too, the gelatinous parts around the bone falling apart, and the more you chew the more the flavor seeps out. And what made me even happier was the white of the yushi-dofu (soft Okinawan tofu). Its silky softness that crumbles fluffily pairs perfectly with the milkiness of the broth, pulling the whole impression together into something much milder. The kelp and green onions add aromatic accents, and I also liked how the aftertaste doesn't get dull.

And on a small side plate, there was a little bit of freshly fried tempura. Two kinds, okra and chikuwa, with a thin batter that's light and crispy when you bite into it. Delicious 😋 Drinking a sip of broth, biting the tempura, then going back to the soba refreshes the flavor. The contrast in temperature is fun too, and it makes you feel like you got a little bonus. The contrast between the round tray and the small black plate looks great too 📷

The shop is new and clean, and the color scheme of the bright wood-grain tables with black spoons and dishes is stylish. Along the walls, carefully selected goods are lined up, with cups, fabric items and plants casually arranged, and I felt it would go over well with groups of women or on a date. There's a hand-drip coffee menu too, so it's nice that you can enjoy a cup after your meal.

Including the sense for presentation and tableware, the added value of the tempura, and the comfort of the shop, my satisfaction with the total experience is solidly high.

Rating (9/15): Next time it might be nice to also get the oden set or coffee and take my time. So, the taste lands at a calm "fairly tasty", but how about trying this shop at least once 🙂
