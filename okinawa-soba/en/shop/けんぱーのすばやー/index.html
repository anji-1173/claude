Kenpa no Subaya

けんぱーのすばやー
Omoromachi, NahaRating 9/15

A popular shop near Naha Shintoshin Park. Its feature is the clear golden (kugani) broth finished over 2 days with a golden ratio of pork, bonito and salt. There are autographs from Norika Fujiwara and Yoko Gushiken too. The serving speed feels great as well.

― 2nd visit (August 2023) ―

I went to eat here in August 2023 😊　This is about a year after the previous time 😁

I actually wanted to go to an Okinawa soba shop I'd never eaten at, and I was driving from the north toward the south looking for one, but in the end the time for an errand was approaching, so I decided to go into this one, which was plain tasty when I ate it last time lol

It was lunchtime and bustling with local customers and tourists, and I didn't have much time, so for a moment I thought, "Will this take quite a while? Should I leave?", but thinking "I might just make it?", I ordered the "Nankotsu Suba (regular size)" at the meal ticket machine.

After that, I secured a counter seat along the wall.

When I ate here last time I didn't really notice (my powers of observation are way too lacking (^_^;)), but I realized the walls are plastered with tons of autographs from celebrities and famous people from Okinawa Prefecture (^_^;)

Looking at the autographs one by one, what suddenly caught my eye, although the autograph is from a while back, was none other than Norika Fujiwara's autograph 😊

*I only noticed while writing this blog now, but the autograph posted below Norika Fujiwara's was from Yoko Gushiken, the former world boxing champion I respect, a star of the people of Okinawa Prefecture known by the nickname "Kanmuri Washi (Crested Serpent Eagle)" (^_^;)

Several people had ordered before me, so I'd braced myself that it would take a fair amount of time, but in about the time it took to gaze at the autographs on the wall for a bit, the "Nankotsu Suba (regular size)" I ordered came out.

This sense of speed, where you order quickly and it comes out quickly, is great ✌　My rating went up 😁

This is about other shops, but in the past I've experienced Okinawa soba shops that took quite a long time after ordering, even though they were popular shops, so I got a very good impression 😊

Like last time, this clear broth and the thin noodles feel so "Kenpa no Subaya", nice. First, a sip of the standard broth; as always, there's no doubt it's a delicious broth with the bonito stock coming through 😊

The noodles are plain tasty too. After savoring the plain bonito-stock broth, I added Kenpa's kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori) that was on the table to change up the flavor.

This is plain tasty too 😋　Next, the soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) topping. They're tender and plain tasty in flavor, but not super melt-in-your-mouth, and there were sinewy soft-bone ribs in there too, so they had chewiness, and the sinewy meat got stuck between my teeth, so I needed a toothpick's help too (^_^;)

Rating (10/15): Plain tasty 😊　As expected, with the recent rise in prices, it seems the price has gone up compared to a year ago. The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) felt a bit sinewy, and I needed a toothpick to get the meat stuck in my teeth lol　I think it's plain tasty, so how about it when you come to Naha Shintoshin for sightseeing and such?

Noodles: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Soup: ★★★☆☆（3）
Toppings: ★★★✬☆（3.5）

― 1st visit (May 2022) ―

I went to eat here in May 2022 😊

On the official website it says: [The concept of our soba making is "harmony". To bring out a harmonious flavor, we repeated failure over and over again, yet without giving up and without compromise, we finally arrived at the unshakable golden ratio of pork, bonito and salt.

The golden (kugani) broth, finished over 2 days, has depth within its clarity; with the faintly fragrant bonito flavor and an exquisite level of salt, it is a bowl of soul made by summoning every ounce of our strength.]

They're really particular about the broth 😊

I ordered the "Seared Nankotsu Green Onion Suba".

The clear, transparent broth that you don't see much at other shops has the gentle taste of bonito stock. After drinking about half the broth, I enjoy changing up the flavor with homemade chili oil 😊　The flavor change is a nice accent lol　The noodles are also ramen-like, a thin noodle type, plain tasty.

Rating (9/15): Plain tasty 😊　How about it when you come to Shintoshin for sightseeing and such?

Noodles: ★★★☆☆（3）
