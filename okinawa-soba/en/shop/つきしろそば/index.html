Tsukishiro Soba

つきしろそば
Maeda, UrasoeRating 10.5/15

A shop of real ability, with a salty broth of vegetable stock and pork bone at recommended-shop level. Its specialty is the black laver seaweed soba piled high with black bara-nori, and changing up the flavor with shikuwasa pepper is delicious too. The Kimutaku figurine at the counter is a hidden highlight.

I ate here in November 2023. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (Oct 1, 2023 to Feb 29, 2024)," and I went here as my 18th of 34 shops 😊

When I went inside, it was quite busy with local customers. At the ticket machine near the entrance inside, I bought the "Black Laver Seaweed Soba" featured in the stamp rally.

I sat at the counter, and right in front of me was the Kimutaku ornament from the photo above 😁 I knew Kimutaku is a longboarder, but he's holding it super close to the nose (the tip), huh. That would drag the board (surfboard), so this style only works at beach-break spots on the mainland lol. I surf too (shortboard), but in Okinawa the spots are reefs (dead coral), so if you carried it that way your board would get wrecked (;^ω^)

I was observing all sorts of things around me between ordering and the food coming out, and after a while the "Black Laver Seaweed Soba" arrived 🤗 It's piled insanely high with black laver seaweed and looks delicious 😁

First, as always, a sip of the broth alone. I wanted to taste just the broth, and since there was so much black laver, I was careful not to mix it in (;^ω^)

As described in the stamp rally booklet, the broth with its "vegetable stock and pork bone" base is really tasty 😋 I thought it was a high level. It has a solid saltiness too and is delicious.

Next I mixed the broth with the laver and took a sip 🤗 On top of the delicious broth, you can taste the flavor of the seaweed too, making it even tastier 😋 As expected, I thought the broth was a high-level taste even compared to other shops.

Next, the noodles. They're thin, straight noodles. Slurping them, they're soft, ordinary noodles lol. These are decently tasty.

The black laver topping is generous, which is good. The egg is a thick rolled omelet and pretty tasty. However, the braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) was on the small side and a bit sinewy. Since I thought the broth was quite high level, I wished the noodles and braised pork belly had been better.

Rating (10.5/15): I thought the salty broth made with vegetable stock and pork bone was delicious, at recommended-shop level. Changing up the flavor with the shikuwasa pepper was a nice accent and delicious too. However, I thought the noodles were just average, and while the laver and thick rolled omelet toppings were delicious, the braised pork belly still had a bit of sinewy texture, so I felt it was one step short. Still, I think the broth is a high level, so why not give it a try at least once? 😁
