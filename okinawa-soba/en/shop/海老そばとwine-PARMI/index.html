Ebi Soba to wine PARMI

海老そばとwine PARMI
Omoromachi, NahaUniqueRating 9/15

Shrimp soba by day and a wine bar by night, a double act. Its signature is the limited 5-servings-a-day "Ebi Zanmai -Kiwami-" with a rich, creamy soup made from a pork bone base and 20 shrimp. Luxurious toppings including two head-on shrimp and chashu.

I ate here in November 2024😆 This time I visited "Ebi Soba to wine PARMI" in Omoromachi, Naha City.

As the name says, this shop serves soba featuring shrimp, and it's a soba shop with a unique style where you can also enjoy wine, especially at night.

The inside has a calm atmosphere, a space like a stylish bar, with wine bottles lined up on the counter. Being able to enjoy wine at an Okinawa soba shop is quite a rare concept🍷✨.

What I had this time was the limited 5-servings-a-day "Ebi Zanmai -Kiwami-" for ¥1,600😄. As the name suggests, it was a bowl that lets you enjoy shrimp to the fullest. The soup is made from a pork bone base with broth drawn from 20 shrimp, giving it a very creamy, rich finish🦐. It's packed with the umami of shrimp, and I could also taste a spicy accent🔥. The soup is frothy😆

The noodles are medium-thick, gently wavy fresh noodles🍜. They have a chewy texture, and with every bite you can faintly taste the flavor of the wheat and a hint of salt. They cling well to the soup, picking up the rich shrimp soup nicely. Unlike traditional Okinawa soba noodles, they're closer to a ramen style.

The toppings were very luxurious too✨. Two large head-on shrimp, a seasoned egg, four slices of chashu, plus two pieces of melt-in-your-mouth braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), and small shrimp scattered over it, a volume truly worthy of the name "Ebi Zanmai (shrimp galore)"🦐🍖. However, what was disappointing was the seasoned egg and the chashu🥚. They were cold, as if just taken out of the fridge, and the gap with the temperature of the soup bothered me a bit❄️. If they'd taken the extra step of warming them, it might have been a more unified bowl.

Rating (9/15): I got the impression the menu was a little similar to that of "Kaidashi Okinawa Soba Kiseki.", which I visited the other day during my stamp rally rounds🤔. Both are seafood-style Okinawa soba, characterized by a creamy soup. However, personally I felt Kiseki's soup was better put together and more polished. PARMI's shrimp soba is delicious too, but the cold toppings ended up being a bit of a letdown. I'm sorry, but I ended up thinking "Kaidashi Okinawa Soba Kiseki." was tastier🙏
