Suu~gii~jii

すぅ～ぎぃ～じぃ
Tomari, Naha (moved to Onna Village)ClosedRating 9/15

A popular shop in Tomari, Naha, where I experienced my longest-ever line (it closed at the end of February 2024 and moved to Yamada, Onna Village, as "Sakihama Seimen"). Its house-made thick noodles boiled after you order and its distinctive A set with wasabi for changing up the flavor were its specialties.

I ate here in February 2024😄

*However, this shop was moving to Yamada, Onna Village, at the end of February 2024 due to the building's deterioration, so I believe it's no longer operating here. I'm glad I got to eat it in Naha one last time😁

This time I went at lunchtime on a weekday, and quite a few people were lined up outside (^0^;).

I thought, this is a popular shop (^0^;)

The reason for the line was posted outside. It seems they boil the noodles after taking your order. So that's why people line up; that makes me get my hopes up for the taste😄

How long did I wait in line? I don't remember the exact time, but it's my longest wait ever (^0^;)

Finally my turn came and I went inside. The "relocation notice" I introduced earlier was posted. After moving to Yamada, Onna Village, it seems the name will also change to "Sakihama Seimen," so I'm glad I got to visit it one last time as "Suu~gii~jii"😁

When I finally got inside and was shown to my seat, they recommended the "A set," which includes the "Suu~gii~jii soba," from the menu, so I went ahead and ordered the "A set."

The menu had an explanation of the types of noodle firmness, but I wasn't particularly asked how I wanted my noodles (^0^;)

There were lots of customers, so I couldn't take many photos inside, but it has a tatami-room feel. A sanshin was also placed there, and one nearby customer tried plucking the strings(?) a little.

It finally came (^0^;) First, I taste the broth. It's a combined bonito and pork bone broth, with the pork bone slightly prominent. It's tasty, but kind of ordinary😆

Next, I taste the noodles. The house-made noodles are thick and straight. The menu had an explanation of noodle firmness, and I don't know whether these were "regular noodles" or "firm noodles," but while they felt a little firm, I didn't think they were as firm as at "Ishigufu," which I ate in Nago and Urasoe.

The seared braised pork belly had a sinewy texture. Also, to change up the flavor I put wasabi in the broth and tried it; it had a refreshing quality, and I thought it went surprisingly well with Okinawa soba broth. It made me want to try it when I eat Okinawa soba at home too😁

Rating (9/15): This shop closes at the end of February 2024, so you'll be eating it as "Sakihama Seimen" at its new location in Yamada, Onna Village.

I think the menu will be about the same at the new location; it's plainly tasty, but considering I waited the longest ever, it wasn't all that, I thought it was perfectly ordinary. Changing up the flavor with wasabi is something no other shop has, so if you have a chance to eat in Onna Village, why not give it a try?😄
