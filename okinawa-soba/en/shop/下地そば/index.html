Shimoji Soba

下地そば
Maji, NahaOld Okinawan houseRating 9/15

An old-house diner just like grandma's house from the old days. The ordering system, where you place a magnet on a whiteboard, is fun. The signature Shimoji soba is an everything-on-top bowl with braised pork belly, soft-bone pork ribs, yushi-dofu, and mozuku.

I ate here in January 2024. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29)", and I went as my 33rd of 34 shops 😊 I've steadily eaten my way through the stamp rally and finally made it this far 😆

When you go inside, it feels like grandma's house from the old days 😆 So nostalgic lol

It was my first time at this shop, so I didn't know the system, but there was no ticket machine, and I had planned to order the "Shimoji soba" featured in the stamp rally booklet anyway, so I said "Shimoji soba, please", and was told, "Put a magnet on number 4 on the whiteboard", so I put a magnet on number 4. The number 4 order on the whiteboard in the photo is mine 😆

It seems the soba menu items are numbered. Maybe the numbers were written on the photo menu posted on the wall, but I didn't notice (^0^;)

They seem to use fresh noodles, and maybe because they take time to boil, I had the impression it took a little while to arrive.

As it was also written on the menu on the wall, the "Shimoji soba" is a so-called "everything-on-top" with braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), yushi-dofu (soft Okinawan tofu), and mozuku.

The "everything-on-top" menu item reminded me of "Futami Soba" in Nago City, which I introduced in "Holds its own against the recommended ones" 😆

First I taste the soup. Maybe the yushi-dofu broth is mixed in, so the broth base was hard to make out, but it tasted light with a slight pork bone base — rather thin, though.

Next I try the noodles. A thin noodle type, and being fresh noodles, they do have a chewy quality. Then I tried the toppings: the braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs were on the small side, and their flavor was also thin and light. Including the yushi-dofu and mozuku, it's a light style overall.

Rating (9/15): Ordinary. The building is an old house like an Okinawan grandma's house from the old days, and the atmosphere is very Okinawan, so how about it when you come to this area sightseeing?
