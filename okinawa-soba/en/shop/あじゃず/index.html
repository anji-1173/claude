Ajazu

Akebono, NahaRating 9/15

A stylish popular shop with jazz playing inside (is the name from Aja × jazz?). The entrance covered in greenery is the landmark, and the signature dish is the soki soba with three large pieces of soki on a light pork bone soup.

*I also had AI (Claude 3.5 sonnet) make this article, and I've edited and updated part of it myself lol

On my Okinawa soba tour, this time I'll introduce “Ajazu” in Akebono, Naha City! 😄 I ate here in September 2024 😄

I went to “Ajazu” in Akebono, Naha City. It's located heading toward the port from the Aja intersection on National Route 58.

The shop's exterior is really stylish! The sign reading “Okinawa Soba Ajazu” on the white wall catches the eye. There are lots of green plants around the entrance, full of tropical vibes 😊

When I entered the on-site parking lot, it was actually full (^0^;)

It was lunchtime, but I thought it must be a popular shop.

However, since it's Okinawa soba, I figured turnover would be quick and waited a bit on the premises. Then customers who had finished eating came out, and I somehow managed to park.

And when you go inside, jazz is playing as background music. I wonder if this is where the shop's name comes from? Maybe it's a pun on the place name “Aja” and “jazz.” It had a somewhat different atmosphere from other Okinawa soba shops, and felt a bit stylish.

Looking at the menu, besides the standard Okinawa soba, of course, there's also yushi-dofu (soft Okinawan tofu) soba and jushi (Okinawan seasoned rice). This time I went for the classic and ordered the “soki soba.”

While I waited, I looked around the shop, and maybe because it was lunchtime, it was really bustling. There were lots of locals too, and I felt that it's a popular shop.

Now, at last, the soki soba arrives! 🍜

First, the soup. Mmm, this is the standard pork bone base. It's light and easy to drink. It's just plain delicious, but I don't think it has a particularly standout personality.

Next, when I slurp the noodles, they're thin straight noodles and fairly soft. Personally, I thought they could have a little more chewiness / bite, but this might be where preferences differ.

Looking at the topping, the soki, there are three large pieces of soki on top! It seems to be one bone-in pork rib (hon-soki) and two soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). The seasoning is on the light side, so you can enjoy the flavor of the meat itself. However, there are some sinewy parts too.

Looking at it overall, “Ajazu's” soki soba doesn't do anything particularly unusual, but it's a stable taste.

But honestly, maybe because I saw how packed it was at lunchtime and expected too much, the taste was about “Yeah, just plain delicious.” It wasn't like I was especially moved! 😅

But Okinawa soba eaten in a shop with a nice atmosphere where jazz plays feels a little special just for that, doesn't it. If you've come to Naha sightseeing and want to enjoy Okinawa soba in a somewhat stylish atmosphere, I think “Ajazu” would be a good option.

Rating (9/15): Just plain delicious, but nothing particularly stands out; that's my impression.
