I ate here in October 2024 😄

This time I went to "Waa-ya" on the grounds of Roadside Station Yui Yui Kunigami in Okuma, Kunigami Village. Kunigami Village is a nature-rich area at the northern tip of Okinawa's main island, surrounded by the Yanbaru forest and beautiful seas, and it's popular as a sightseeing spot too 🌳🌊

Roadside Station Yui Yui Kunigami is full of local specialties and tourist information, and it's a perfect spot for a break on a drive. "Waa-ya", on its grounds, is a popular shop not only with locals but with tourists too 😄

When you go inside, the atmosphere feels somehow nostalgic. The tropical-style checkered tablecloths give it warmth. Looking at the menu, there were several Okinawan local dishes, starting with Okinawa soba. This time I decided to order the classic "soki soba" 🍜

After waiting a while, the "soki soba" was brought out. Medium-thick straight noodles in a clear amber soup, with the soki meat carefully arranged on top. First, as usual, I taste the soup.

Taking a sip of the soup, the pork bone broth had a slight kick, and the saltiness felt somewhat strong.

Next I have the noodles. Medium-thin straight noodles with a somewhat soft texture. They cling well to the soup, but personally I felt they could have a bit more bite.

The soki meat had a light seasoning and was simply tasty, but some parts still had quite a sinewy texture and were hard to eat. From what I heard before, this shop used to use inobuta (wild boar–pig hybrid) meat, but apparently it became hard to source and now it's regular pork. I wanted to try inobuta soki soba for the first time, so that was a little disappointing 😢

There were also several groups of what looked like tourists in the shop, and I could see them enjoying Okinawan food culture. When you make the trip to Kunigami Village, it's a spot where you can enjoy this Okinawa soba along with the nature of Yanbaru 🚗

Rating (9/15): The soup is pork-bone based with a salty kick and simply tasty. The noodles are on the soft side and easy to eat, but the soki meat was a bit sinewy, so it felt like it needed just a little more. If you stop by while sightseeing in Kunigami Village, I think it's worth trying once as a local flavor 😊
