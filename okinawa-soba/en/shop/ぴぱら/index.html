Pipara

ぴぱら
Nakadomari, Onna Village (Onna no Eki)Rating 9/15

An Okinawa soba shop in the food court of "Onna no Eki Nakayukui Market," bustling with tourists. The braised pork belly soba, with thin noodles in a broth of rich pork bone and plenty of bonito stock, is popular. The photo spot with a view of Iejima Tatchu and browsing the local-produce souvenirs are fun too.

*I had AI (Claude Opus 4.1) help write this article too, and I've added and updated parts of it myself lol. I ate here in December 2024 😄

This time I'd like to introduce "Pipara," an Okinawa soba shop inside "Onna no Eki Nakayukui Market" in Nakadomari, Onna Village. This is a tourist facility whose concept is "a village farmers' market that connects people with people," and it's a popular spot that's always bustling with lots of tourists 🌺

When you pull into the Onna no Eki parking lot, the first thing that leaps into view is a set of signs with cute bitter melon (goya) and pineapple characters drawn on them. Greetings in the Okinawan dialect like "Matan, mensore" (come again, welcome) are written on them too, which gets tourists excited 😊 The weather was nice that day too, with blue skies spreading out, perfect for sightseeing ☀️

Inside the facility, it has exactly the atmosphere of a roadside station, with local farm produce, seafood, souvenirs, and more lined up in rows. Among them, our destination this time, "Okinawa Soba Pipara," had its shop in the food court area. Actually, there were other shops like a seafood bowl shop too, and everything looked so delicious I didn't know where to look 😋

When you stand in front of Pipara's ticket machine, there's a long row of menu items with photos. Among them, I decided to order the "Braised Pork Belly Soba," which was introduced as a "popular item."

Outside Onna no Eki, the beautiful Okinawan sea spreads out 🌊 On days with good weather, you can even see Iejima's Tatchu (Gusuku-yama) in the distance. There's also a photo spot 📸, and from the photo spot at the top of the slope you can take great scenic shots 😄

After a while my number was called, and I picked up the "Braised Pork Belly Soba" at the counter. In the bowl are neatly arranged braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), thin noodles, green onions, and a broth with a bit of extra oil floating on top. First, as always, let's taste the broth 😊

Taking a sip of the broth, you can feel the richness of the pork bone, but the bonito stock comes through too. However, my impression was that it was a little on the oily side. Still, it's decently tasty in its own way. After walking around in the Okinawan heat, this richer broth actually feels like it soaks right into your body.

Next, the noodles. They're thin noodles with a loose wave, and the texture is on the soft side. They cling well to the broth and slip down easily. They feel like gentle noodles with no quirks that even tourists will find easy to eat 🍜

And as for the braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) topping, it had a sweet seasoning that was well soaked in. However, some parts still had a bit of sinewy texture, so it was finished with more chewiness than melting tenderness. The sweet-savory seasoning feels like classic Okinawa soba, and I think it's a flavor that even tourists eating Okinawa soba for the first time will find easy to like.

After eating, as I strolled around the facility, there were lots of fresh seafood and local vegetables for sale, so you can enjoy picking out souvenirs too 🎁

When you step outside the facility, the contrast between the blue sea and sky is truly beautiful, and a quintessentially Okinawan view spreads out before you. You can see the sea from the parking lot too, so being able to soak up the Okinawa vibe from the moment you get out of the car is a nice plus ✨

"Onna no Eki" feels like an ordinary roadside station, but I think it's a good spot for tourists to casually experience Okinawan food and culture. Besides Okinawa soba, you can enjoy all kinds of Okinawan food like seafood bowls and taco rice, so it's the perfect place to drop by during a break on a drive 😄

Rating (9/15): Pipara's Okinawa soba this time isn't especially distinctive, but it has the steady deliciousness you'd expect from a tourist spot, and it felt decently tasty. When you visit Onna Village for sightseeing, why not stop by once as a nice spot to enjoy Okinawa soba while gazing at the beautiful sea 🌴
