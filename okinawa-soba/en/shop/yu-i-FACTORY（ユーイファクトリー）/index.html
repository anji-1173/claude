yu-i FACTORY (Yui Factory)

yu-i FACTORY（ユーイファクトリー）
Arakawa, Haebaru TownUniqueRating 8.5/15

An ultra-rare shop open only once a month. The "Okinawa 82 (Habu) Soba", with a broth of bonito, pork, chicken and kelp plus habu snake stock, topped with simmered habu, can only be tasted here. Next door there's also a habu leather goods shop (*as of July 2025 it appears to be suspended; please check).

*This article was also made with the help of AI (Claude 4 Sonnet), and I added to and updated part of it myself lol

*As of July 2025, it's difficult for this shop to procure habu for the soba, and it seems they aren't doing their once-a-month opening, so I recommend checking in advance whether they're open 😆

I ate here in December 2024 😄

This time I'm introducing a shop where you can have an especially rare experience, even among my Okinawa soba tours 😄 I learned about it in the newspaper: "yu-i FACTORY" in Arakawa, Haebaru Town. This shop is an ultra-rare Okinawa soba shop that's open only once a month, an Okinawa soba shop that uses that "habu" snake as a topping 😄

The moment I saw the exterior, it was such a unique building that I thought, "What kind of facility is this?" 😅 It has a stylish construction that looks like a renovated container house, and at first glance it's a modern atmosphere you'd never take for an Okinawa soba shop.

When you enter the shop, there's "habu soba" 😆 The ultimate oddball soba you can only taste in Okinawa. As someone who goes around eating Okinawa soba, this is something I absolutely have to take on 👍

What I ordered this time was the "Okinawa 82 Soba (Habu Soba) Set" for ¥1,300. I also ordered the limited-quantity ¥300 "Jushi with Habu Stock" along with it. A combination of Okinawan representatives ✖️3 — Okinawa soba, jushi and habu — there can't be a more Okinawan menu than this 😂

First, as usual, I taste the broth. It's quite a unique broth said to combine bonito, pork, chicken, kelp, and habu stock. Taking a sip… honestly, none of the stocks stood out, and if I hadn't been told it was habu I wouldn't have known 😅 But maybe that actually makes it easier to drink.

Next I have the noodles. They're a medium-thick straight type, with a soft-ish texture. My impression is that they're plain tasty Okinawa soba noodles 😊

And then the simmered habu, which you could call the star this time! When I timidly picked it up with my chopsticks, it had a ton of small bones and less meat than I expected 😅 There was no smell or quirkiness at all, and surprisingly it had a light flavor. But eating it while picking out the small bones was a bit of a struggle 💦

The rafute was simmered until tender, but it felt like a little sinewy texture remained. Even so, it was a reassuring flavor as a standard Okinawa soba topping 😄

And I also taste the limited-quantity "Jushi with Habu Stock". This one was lightly seasoned too, and finished so that you wouldn't know it had habu stock in it unless you were told. But the unique lingering aftertaste left in my mouth after eating (earthy?) may perhaps be a characteristic of habu 🤔

Finishing my meal and looking around, a habu leather goods shop was also open in the prefab building next door 😆 The wallets combining indigo dye and habu leather were really cool, and appealing as a very Okinawan souvenir too ✨

Being open only once a month is a rare business style. Okinawa soba using habu, an ingredient unique to Okinawa, was a precious experience you absolutely can't have anywhere else 😊

To be honest, judging by taste alone it felt like ordinary Okinawa soba, but considering this rarity and the value of the experience, I think it's a shop worth trying at least once. It's definitely worth visiting by aiming for the once-a-month opening 👍

Rating (8.5/15): The taste itself is ordinary, but it's a shop where you can have a precious experience available only here, even within Okinawa, using the unusual ingredient habu. Including the rarity of opening only once a month, I think it's worth trying at least once if you love Okinawa soba 😄 However, while eating it didn't seem to have any quirkiness, after finishing there was something — not the gamy smell like after eating goat soba, but earthy? — a feeling different from usual, so be careful (^0^;)
