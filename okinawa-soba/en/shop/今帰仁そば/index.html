Nakijin Soba

今帰仁そば
Shoshi, Nakijin VillageOld Okinawan houseRating 9/15

A tile-roofed old-house soba shop near Nakijin Castle Ruins and Churaumi Aquarium. Agu pork and bonito blended broth with homemade noodles, and you can choose from four toppings. The three homemade condiments made with Yanbaru ingredients are fun too, and it's a photogenic spot.

I ate here in April 2025 😊 This time, on an Okinawa soba tour of the northern area of the main island, I went to "Nakijin Soba" in Shoshi, Nakijin Village 🌺 Nakijin Village is an area close to the World Heritage site Nakijin Castle Ruins and Churaumi Aquarium, and this shop is in a spot that's easy to drop by while sightseeing 😄

Arriving at the shop, the first thing that jumped out at me was a very Okinawan tile-roofed old house. The noren (shop curtain) with "Nanokuni-mura Style House" and "Nakijin Soba" written in large letters, plus the yellow "Open" sign, really build up the atmosphere ✨ This is exactly what an Okinawan old-house soba shop looks like 😊 Just the exterior gets you excited 🎵

Before you pass under the noren at the entrance, there's a rugged Ryukyu limestone wall, bougainvillea flowers, and trees like banyans planted, giving it a super Okinawan feel 🌴 It had become a photogenic spot that makes tourists want to take pictures 📸

When you go inside, this too is a stylish design 😲 Stylish rattan chairs and tables are arranged, and it's an open space with natural light coming in through the sudare (bamboo blinds). It keeps the charm of an old house while feeling new 🌸

Inside there's also a raised tatami area, which has an atmosphere like an old-fashioned Okinawan grandma's house 😊 Orion Beer posters and Nakijin Village watermelon posters are up, so you can feel the local vibe too 🍉

Looking at the menu, the signature "Nakijin soba" is ¥950. Homemade noodles with Agu pork and bonito broth, and it's set up so you can choose from four toppings 😄 You choose from "melt-in-your-mouth nankotsu soki", "seared salt-flavored sanmai-niku", "mix", and "yushi-dofu (limited quantity)", and this time I greedily ordered the mix (plus ¥50) 🍜 There's also kids' soba (¥750), a Nakijin soba set (¥1,350), jushi (¥300), homemade yushi-dofu (¥400), and more.

There are three kinds of condiments on the table — "nama-shichimi (spicy miso made with Yanbaru shikuwasa and island chili peppers)", "nimi togarashi (Yanbaru shikuwasa and island chili peppers)", and "kōrēgūsu" — all homemade condiments made with Yanbaru ingredients, which gets you excited 🌶️ They seem to sell them as souvenirs too, and you can sense their dedication ✨

After a short wait, the Nakijin soba (mix) was brought out 🍜 In a bowl that looks like Uruma ware, it looks very photogenic, with something white and creamy like yushi-dofu, julienned ginger, soft-bone pork ribs, seared braised pork belly, and green onion arranged in good balance 😊

The customary sip of soup. It's supposed to be an Agu pork and bonito blended broth, but hmm, honestly it's a light style where neither really comes through 🤔 Not in a bad way — it's a very gentle flavor with a refined finish — but personally I thought it'd be fine if the richness of the Agu pork or the bonito aroma hit a bit harder. Maybe they've finished it to have broad appeal for tourists 😅

Next I have the noodles 🍜 Homemade medium-thick, gently wavy noodles, and these are pretty good 😋 They have solid chewiness, and the noodles are simply tasty 👍

The braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs toppings are simmered until melt-in-your-mouth and tender, but my impression was that for the price of ¥1,000, they were a bit on the small side 😅 And some parts of the soft-bone pork ribs still had a bit of a sinewy texture, so I'd have been happier if they were just a little more fall-apart tender 🥩

Rating (9/15): The charming old-house exterior and the stylishly renovated interior are outstanding, and it seems like a shop that will be popular with tourists 😊 But as for the flavor, honestly, it's "ordinary" 🤔 With a light soup where neither the Agu pork nor the bonito comes through, and toppings on the small side for ¥1,000, I'd say it needs a little more in terms of value for money 🍜 For those who want to enjoy the atmosphere of an old house on the way back from sightseeing in Nakijin Village or from Churaumi Aquarium, I think it's worth stopping by 😄
