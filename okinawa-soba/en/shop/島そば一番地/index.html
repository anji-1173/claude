Shima Soba Ichibanchi

島そば一番地
Hikawa, Naha City (Nōren Plaza 2F)Rating 9/15

The Naha branch of a Yaeyama soba specialty shop whose main store is on Ishigaki Island. Shima soba with thinly sliced fish cake and braised pork belly, round thin noodles, and a faintly pork-bone broth, served with a mini miso rice ball. Changing the flavor with pipaz spice is the Yaeyama way too.

I ate here in February 2024 😄

This soba shop is a Yaeyama soba specialty shop, and it seems to run two shops: the main store on Ishigaki Island, and this Naha branch (Hikawa, Naha City) on the Okinawa main island.

From the menu I decided to order the “shima soba,” which shares the shop's name.

Perhaps as a feature of Yaeyama soba, there's a spice called “pipaz,” and it seems to have various effects 😄 I'd like to try it partway through to change the flavor 😆

The shima soba has arrived 😄 Not only pickles but also a mini rice ball (miso) comes with it, which is a good deal 😆

I previously had Yaeyama soba for the first time at “Miyara Soba (Urasoe branch)” in Urasoe City, and the feature is that the fish cake and braised pork belly are thinly sliced.

First, a sip of the broth. A broth with a faint pork-bone base, just plain tasty.

Next, the noodles. They are thin straight noodles, on the soft side. The braised pork belly and fish cake are just plain tasty too.

Partway through I added “pipaz” to change the flavor 😆 But it tasted like ordinary pepper? and wasn't the spice I'd imagined (^0^;)

Rating (9/15): It's just plain tasty. If you want to try Yaeyama soba, why not give this shop a try once? I used a paid coin parking lot nearby without knowing, but apparently the Nōren Plaza parking lot is free for one hour 😄
