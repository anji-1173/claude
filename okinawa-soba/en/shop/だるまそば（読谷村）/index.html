Daruma Soba (Yomitan Village)

I ate here in January 2025 😄 This time I visited "Daruma Soba" in Sobe, Yomitan Village. Actually, this shop is the second branch of "Daruma Soba" in Yamazato, Okinawa City. It's combined with an izakaya called "Sumiya," so I headed right over to see what kind of Okinawa soba they serve! 😄

The interior has a warm, wood-based design, and it's a tatami-seating style where you take off your shoes and step up. 🏮 It has a casual izakaya-like feel, and its charm is that you can enjoy Okinawa soba while relaxing at your leisure.

The menu is extensive, so you can enjoy not just Okinawa soba but also lunch sets and all kinds of Okinawan dishes. Okinawa soba made by an izakaya, so my expectations rose a bit! ✨

This time, from the menu, I ordered the "Soft-Bone Soba (Medium)."

The broth has a rich flavor with plenty of bonito broth coming through. As you'd expect from an izakaya making it, you can really taste the depth. Its defining feature is a rich broth for which the word "hearty" is a perfect fit. It's a little different from the broth at a typical Okinawa soba shop; if anything, it's a somewhat distinctive flavor that feels packed with the izakaya's particular care. Maybe this is the reason the taste differs between the second and first shops.

The noodles are medium-thick wavy noodles, but they have a surprisingly soft texture 🍝

The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) 🍖 are well seasoned through and have a tender texture. However, they're not completely fall-apart tender; a bit of sinewy texture remains. The broth also has an oily quality, so I think it'll be irresistible for people who like rich, hearty flavors 🎉

Rating (10.5/15): Looking at it overall, I really feel this shop serves Okinawa soba with a good balance of noodles, broth, and toppings. They say it tastes different from the first shop in Yamazato, Okinawa City, and personally I found this second branch in Sobe, Yomitan Village, tastier. It's a bowl I can truly recommend to anyone who likes hearty flavors. Its unique depth and richness are something only an izakaya could deliver. If you ever head toward Yomitan Village, please do stop by at least once 😋
