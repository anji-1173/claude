SHIKAKU

Kumoji, NahaRating 9/15

A luxurious Okinawa soba shop on the 1st floor of the Southwest Grand Hotel. Its feature is the kettle (teapot) style where the noodles and soup come separately, and the dashi chazuke, made by pouring the kettle's broth over the tuna nakaochi rice, is also recommended.

This article was written with AI (Gemini Pro) and partially edited by me 😁

I went to eat here in February 2025 😄

This time I visited “SHIKAKU,” located on the 1st floor of the “Southwest Grand Hotel” in Kumoji, Naha City. The aura is different from the exterior alone. Since it's on the 1st floor of a hotel, there's a shisa sitting against a stylish exposed-concrete wall, and even this shisa looks somehow sleek, like modern art ✨

Standing in front of the shop, a sense of luxury is in the air. But as an Okinawa soba lover, I summoned my courage and charged in!

When I went inside, the lighting was turned down quite low, and it had an atmosphere like a bar at dinnertime or an upscale teppanyaki restaurant. It's moody even though it's daytime, a space you could use for a date. And above all, what surprised me was the staff's service! When showing me to my seat, they actually pulled out my chair and escorted me, just like at a hotel restaurant…! Having my chair pulled out when I came to eat Okinawa soba may be a first in my life (lol). It makes you sit up a little straighter 😳

Now, when I sat down and opened the menu, it was another simple and stylish design. The “SHIKAKU soba” regular is ¥1,400 and the large is ¥1,600. I ordered the “SHIKAKU soba (regular)” and the side dish the staff recommended, “tuna nakaochi rice (¥700)” 🍜

After a while, this set was brought out.

The style is unique! The bowl with the noodles and toppings and a silver kettle with the soup came out separately. This is the same style as the long-established “Yae Shokudo” in Nago City! I never thought I'd encounter this kettle style at a hotel in Naha. But it's not a homey kettle like Yae Shokudo's; it has the look of a sleek stainless-steel teapot, and I feel the stylishness here too ✨

This ritual of pouring the soup yourself from the kettle is exciting. Every time you pour, the nice aroma of bonito broth wafts up. The good thing about this style is that the soup stays piping hot.

First, I try a sip of the soup. Yeah, the bonito broth comes through.

Next, I have the noodles. The noodles are a thin type with a slight wave. When I slurp them, they have a smooth, slippery texture, and they're boiled fairly soft. As someone who likes noodles with strong firmness, I'd like a little more chewiness / bite 🤔

The toppings are braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and fish cake (kamaboko), plus green onions and bonito flakes. The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) is simmered tender, and the seasoning is neither too strong nor too light, just right. The bonito flakes sprinkled on top further bring out the aroma of the soup, and the flavor is really good 👍

And then the side dish, the “tuna nakaochi rice”! It comes with wasabi and looks delicious, but I poured the broth from the kettle holding the soba soup over the tuna rice and ate it like dashi chazuke.

This was exactly right! 🐟✨ The sweetness of the tuna's fat combines with the piping-hot bonito broth, and the half-rare tuna melts in your mouth. The wasabi adds an accent, and you can just shovel it down. I really want everyone to try it too!

Rating (9/15): My overall impression is that the taste is just plain delicious. However, there's no denying that it's “hotel pricing~.” With ¥1,400 for Okinawa soba, and over ¥2,000 with rice too, I think it's a shop for a sightseeing memory or for when you want a meal in a nice atmosphere, rather than an everyday lunch.

The atmosphere inside is supremely luxurious, and the staff's service is wonderful, so it has value as an “experience.” However, if it competes purely on “the taste of Okinawa soba,” compared with other shops, my honest feeling is that it ends up with a slightly harsher rating in terms of cost performance 😅

But the contrast of pouring the soup yourself from a kettle in this luxurious space was fun, and it's the perfect shop for a conversation topic! Thank you for the meal 🙏
