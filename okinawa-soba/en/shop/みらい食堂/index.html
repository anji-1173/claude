Mirai Shokudo

みらい食堂
Kanna, Ginoza Village (Michi-no-Eki Ginoza)ClosedRating 9/15

A diner that used to be inside Michi-no-Eki Ginoza (* now closed). Its Okinawa soba featured the signature “jagamen,” noodles kneaded with potatoes from Ginoza Village, and the Ginoza soba topped with pig's feet, braised pork belly, pork spare ribs, and egg omelet was hearty. I'm keeping this article as a record.

I ate here in January 2021.

Note: Unfortunately this shop is now closed. I'm writing it down as a record.

I ordered the “Ginoza soba, large.” The noodles are medium-thin, made from potatoes. The broth is bonito stock and delicious 😋 There seemed to be no difference from ordinary noodles ^^; The toppings were hearty too, with pig's feet (tebichi), braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), pork spare ribs (soki), and egg omelet.

Rating (9/15): It was just plain tasty, but it's a pity it has closed. Ginoza Village promotes itself as a potato village, and the road lighting fixtures in the area are designed as “potatoes,” so please take a look when you pass nearby 😁 It seems to be a shop called “Shokudo Manjiro?” now, so maybe they have Okinawa soba using “jagamen.”
