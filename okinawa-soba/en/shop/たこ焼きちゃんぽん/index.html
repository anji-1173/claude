Takoyaki Champon

たこ焼きちゃんぽん
Chuo, Okinawa City (Ichibangai)Rating 9/15

A shop with outstanding value for money in the characterful Chuo Ichibangai area of Okinawa City. Vegetable soba at an astonishing ¥400, with a gentle pork bone broth and plenty of vegetables. A welcome find in this era of rising prices.

I ate here in February 2024 😄

This is the menu posted inside the shop, and everything is at reasonable prices you won't find at other shops 😄 With so many products and shops raising prices due to soaring costs and wage increases, can they really make a profit at these prices? Maybe they have another source of income lol

From the menu I decided to order the super-reasonable "Vegetable Soba." ¥400, so cheap 😄

The vegetable soba arrived. It has a decent amount of volume 😄 First I taste the broth.

It's a gentle broth with a bit of a pork bone base coming through. Decently tasty.

The noodles are medium-thick, straight, and soft. For toppings, egg and pork luncheon meat (Spam) are cut into thin strips and added as accents. There's quite a lot of vegetables, and the seasoning is on the light side. I'm always eating Okinawa soba that's nothing but carbs and meat, so getting some vegetables in seems to make my nutritional balance a little better 😆

Rating (9/15): A great-value Okinawa soba in the characterful Chuo Ichibangai area of Okinawa City. The taste is decently good too, so why not give it a try at least once 😄
