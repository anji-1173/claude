Mutsumibashi Kadoya

むつみ橋 かどや
Makishi, Naha (Kokusai-dori)ClosedRating 9/15

A long-established shop that lasted 72 years near the Mutsumibashi intersection on Kokusai-dori (closed June 20, 2024). Its specialty was the ¥600 pork loin soba with a gentle pork-bone-based soup. A memorable shop where I savored a last bowl before it closed, along with a very long line.

*I also had AI (Claude 3.5 sonnet) make this article, and I've edited and updated part of it myself lol

I ate here in June 2024. This seems to be a long-established shop, but I learned it would close on June 20, 2024, so I came for the last chance! 😄　It's sad that an Okinawa soba shop loved by locals is closing.

Maybe because it's closing, there was a very long line. As expected of a long-established shop that's continued to be loved! But turnover was good, and I was able to eat sooner than I expected.

I'm glad I was able to eat at Kadoya before it closed 😄

This time, from the menu, I decided to order the “pork loin soba.”

First, the soup. It's characterized by a gentle pork-bone-based taste, not too rich, a light flavor. This is the long-standing taste that customers have loved for so long 😄

The noodles are a medium-thick straight type. They have a soft texture and cling well to the soup. They taste just plain delicious.

The main topping, the pork loin, has a light flavor. It has little fat and a refreshing texture.

“Mutsumibashi Kadoya's” Okinawa soba is truly the most classic of the classic. There's no flashiness, but with one bite it feels like “Ah, this is Okinawa soba!”

Long-time love — it makes sense that it's widely loved by both tourists and locals.

Since its closing has been decided, I'm really glad I was able to eat it one last time. There's no doubt that this shop, bringing down the curtain on more than 70 years of history, has adorned an important page in the world of Okinawa soba.

On top of that, I even got a closing commemorative card lol　Maybe it's a bit of a rare item? I found myself thinking, I wonder if it would sell for a high price on Mercari (lol)

Rating (9/15): It tasted just plain delicious, but I'm glad I was able to eat at Kadoya before it closed. Thank you very much.
