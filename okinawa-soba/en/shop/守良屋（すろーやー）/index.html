Suroya

守良屋（すろーやー）
Kyoda, NagoRating 9.5/15

A popular handmade Okinawa soba shop tucked away deep in the Kyoda village. Committed to using no chemical seasonings, with glossy homemade wavy flat noodles and a light, gentle bonito broth. A natural style with windows wide open and only electric fans.

Driving along National Route 58 in Kyoda, Nago, I'd been curious about a handmade sign reading "Handmade Okinawa Soba Suroya," and I finally went to eat here in July 2023 😊

There was no menu; the soba menu was posted on the wall of the building.

Since you access it from the national highway via a narrow road through the village, it's a quiet, tucked-away place, but inside it was packed with locals and tourists (・・)

As usual, I went in without any research, but I think it's a popular shop.

I ordered the "Suroya Soba," which has the same name as the shop. Because I hadn't done any research, I didn't know how to read the kanji, and when ordering I ended up saying "Moriyoshi-ya soba, please," but my order went through just fine (^_^;)　When I googled it after eating, I learned it's read "Suroya" (^_^;)

On the wall inside was a notice saying "No chemical seasonings used." I got the impression they're quite particular about making things with natural ingredients.

First, a sip of the soup. A gentle flavor with a light bonito broth coming through; solidly tasty 😋　The noodles are homemade, handmade wavy flat noodles. They look glossy and delicious. When you eat them they have a bit of firmness, and these are solidly tasty too 😋

The toppings are soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) and bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), both jiggly and tender 😄　The seasoning is gentle and light, which felt different from the type at other shops where the flavor has thoroughly soaked in.

Rating (9.5/15): It's a shop particular about its ingredients, with "no chemical seasonings used" 😄　How about it for those who like healthy Okinawa soba?

When I ate here it was a super-hot lunchtime right after the rainy season had ended, but maybe because their concept is enjoying food naturally in an old house, the shop's windows were wide open with only electric fans, and no air conditioning was on (^_^;)　Everyone was eating while sweating, which in a sense was healthy too 😁

Noodles: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Soup: ★★★☆☆（3）
Toppings: ★★★☆☆（3）
