Shirano

シラノ
Takahara, Okinawa CityRating 9/15

A stylish, Western-style "Okinawa Soba and Pan Shop" run by an owner with a French cooking background. Starting with an appetizer of garlic toast, its soki soba in bonito soup is distinctive, letting you change the flavor with citrus condiments made from shikuwasa and tankan.

I ate here in March 2024 😄 I learned about this shop from the newspaper lol

What country's architecture does this look like? It's Western-style 😄 Different from the Okinawa soba shops I usually go to 😄

According to the newspaper article, it had only just opened in November 2023. The owner seems to be a chef with a French cooking background. That's why the building is Western-style like this — because of the French background 😁

When I went inside, since it was lunchtime, there were quite a lot of customers. The interior is Western-style, but the moment I entered there was a lovely smell of bonito broth 🤤

It seems to be based on a bonito and pork bone broth, but from the smell drifting through the shop, I thought the bonito was dominant lol

I forgot to take a photo of the menu, but I ordered the "soki soba". Among the other items there was also "clam-broth Okinawa soba", which I thought looked delicious, but I was told it was "sold out" (^0^;) There was also a menu item called "spice curry soba", which also looked delicious and I hesitated, but I thought I should order the classic here after all, so I went with the "soki soba" 😁

Before the Okinawa soba, garlic toast came out with "Here's your appetizer". Other shops don't have this style — just as the sign says, it really is an "Okinawa Soba and Pan Shop" 😁

The crispy toasted bread has a good garlic kick and is simply tasty.

First I taste the soup. It's mainly bonito, combined with pork bone broth, and the soup seems to take cues from French technique. It's a soup that fills the shop with a lovely aroma, so I took a sip with high expectations.

As expected, the bonito comes through, and it's a simply tasty soup.

Next I have the noodles. They're homemade aged noodles, apparently rested for five days after being made before they're finished. Thin noodles, and I thought these were also just ordinary.

While enjoying the soup, noodles, and soki, in the second half I changed the flavor with condiments made from shikuwasa and tankan (Okinawan citrus), something other shops don't have.

A citrusy freshness gets added to the bonito broth soup, and I thought it was a new flavor, but they go well together — delicious.

Rating (9/15): A shop with a stylish Western-style exterior and interior. How about trying Okinawa soba made by an owner with a French cooking background at least once 😁 The citrus condiments go well with the bonito-forward soup, so why not try them? The location is peaceful and quiet, so you can enjoy your meal at a relaxed pace.
