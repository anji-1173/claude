Yume no Ya

夢ノ弥
Furujima, NahaRating 10/15

Okinawa soba, limited to 30 servings a day, made by a shop specializing in rich chicken, pork bone, and seafood tsukemen. The clean pork bone broth with no off-odor and the meltingly tender braised pork belly are delicious; just one step short of recommended level. Cashless payment is a plus too.

I ate here in April 2024😄 Every time I drove along National Route 330 there was a conspicuous sign, so I'd been curious for a long time, and I finally went😆

It's "Okinawa soba" made by a rich tsukemen specialty shop😁 Doesn't that alone sound delicious? lol And it's limited to 30 servings a day! My expectations are high.

When you go inside, the meal ticket machine is right in front of you. It's a tsukemen specialty shop, but my only target was the "Okinawa soba," so I ordered the "Chicken, Pork Bone & Seafood Okinawa Soba." And it accepts cashless, which is wonderful👍 I paid with Rakuten Pay, which earns Rakuten points😆

There was also a description of the soba. My expectations are high😄

The Okinawa soba made by a tsukemen specialty shop arrived😁 First, the broth.

It's a broth with plenty of pork bone, with no off-odor and a clean feel. I thought it was different from other shops' pork bone broths. Delicious😄

Next, the noodles. They're thin and straight. They feel soft and are just plain tasty. The braised pork belly topping is meltingly tender with no sinewy texture, and delicious; I wanted to eat a bit more😆

Rating (10/15): The broth is a pork-bone-based broth you won't find at other shops, and the braised pork belly topping was delicious too. Since it's Okinawa soba made by a tsukemen specialty shop, I feel I may have set the bar too high; it's delicious, but I thought it's just one step short of recommended level.
