Maruhira Shokudo

まるひら食堂
Uka, Kunigami VillageRating 9/15

A diner in a former community store in Uka, Kunigami Village, at the northernmost tip of Okinawa's main island. Featured in the newspaper as "the northernmost diner on Okinawa's west coast." Braised pork belly soba in a gentle pork bone broth, and the specialty "Genkotsu Set Meal" with its super-huge fried chicken.

The shop I'm introducing this time is "Maruhira Shokudo" in Uka, Kunigami Village, at the northernmost tip of Okinawa's main island. This shop is actually a place with history that has long been loved by locals as the former Uka Community Store 😁 I went to eat there in December 2024; it's been featured in the newspaper too and is known as "the northernmost diner on Okinawa's west coast."

Since it's in Kunigami Village at the very north of Okinawa's main island, it's not a place you can easily come to, but that's exactly what makes it feel special ✨ When you step inside, there's the atmosphere of an old-fashioned diner. Since it was lunchtime, surprisingly there were quite a lot of customers 😲 Maybe it's not just locals but lots of people visiting on sightseeing trips too.

Looking at the menu posted on the wall, there's a wide selection of set meals besides Okinawa soba. I'm especially curious about the dish called "Genkotsu Set Meal" 😆 It apparently comes with super-huge fried chicken, and it seems to be a famous dish too. This time the goal was my Okinawa soba tour, so I decided to order the "Braised Pork Belly Soba, Medium" from the menu. At ¥750 the price is reasonable too, and though you can't tell from the menu, it's a nice plus that pickles come complimentary 😊

After ordering and waiting a while, the "Braised Pork Belly Soba" was brought out. It looks simple, but it feels like a proper Okinawa soba. First, as always, I'll taste the broth 😋 The pork-bone-based broth has a gentle, light flavor. The not-too-strong pork bone flavor is pleasant, and it's finished as a homey taste that somehow feels nostalgic. Maybe because of its northernmost location, it has a gentle seasoning that tourists can easily take to.

Next, the noodles. They're wavy noodles with a softish texture. The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) topping is sliced thin like ramen chashu and is very tender. It's a different texture from thick braised pork belly, and it's tasty in its own way ✨ It's topped with plenty of finely shredded white leek too, and its crunchy texture is a nice accent.

The appeal of Maruhira Shokudo is, above all, the special feeling of it being "the northernmost tip of Okinawa's main island." Okinawa soba eaten after coming all the way to Kunigami Village, where an ordinary Okinawa soba tour rarely takes you, becomes an experience that stays in your memory even more than the taste 😊 Being a former community store, it has a warm atmosphere rooted in the community, with a charm different from Okinawa soba shops in urban areas. Eating Okinawa soba while time flows leisurely makes you forget the busyness of daily life.

Also, as the newspaper coverage shows, this shop is an important presence supporting the local food culture. It's a shop I'd definitely like you to drop by when you visit the northern region on a sightseeing trip 😄 The taste was decently good. It's not exceptionally outstanding in flavor, but I think the shop's big appeal is its northernmost location, its history as a former community store, and the warm atmosphere of a place that continues to be loved by the community ✨

Rating (9/15): When you come to sightsee in the northern region or enjoy the nature of Yanbaru, please be sure to try the Okinawa soba here at Maruhira Shokudo. You can have the special experience of "the northernmost Okinawa soba" that you can't usually taste 😊 Next time I'm also curious about the super-huge fried chicken of the "Genkotsu Set Meal," so if I get the chance I'd like to give it a try 😆
