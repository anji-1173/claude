Agarie Soba

東江そば
Jitchaku, UrasoeRating 10.5/15

The renowned long-established Urasoe main branch, on the second floor along the national highway. The signature Agarie Soba comes with braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), pork spare ribs (soki) and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) all in one, with a clear soup with a strong bonito broth and homemade medium-thick wavy noodles; there are many celebrity autographs too.

I went to eat here in January 2025😄 This time I visited the renowned long-established shop "Agarie Soba Urasoe Main Branch" in Jitchaku, Urasoe City 😊

The shop is on the second floor of a building along the national highway, and the words "Agarie Soba" on the big white sign really stand out 🏪 There are pink banners on the road too, so even first-timers shouldn't get lost. You go up the stairs to the second floor, and the well-worn building has the dignity of a long-established shop. It was lunchtime on a weekday, but there's plenty of parking, so access by car is good 🚗

When you go in, the first thing you see is the meal ticket machine 🎫 Looking at the menu, there's a wide variety, including Agarie soba, sanmai-niku soba, nankotsu soki soba, vegetable soba and hon-soki soba. Prices are fair, around ¥850 to ¥1,000 for a medium, and even a large is reasonable at ¥950 to ¥1,100. This time I ordered the signature "Agarie Soba (Medium)" for ¥1,000 💰

The seating is a tatami-floored raised area, with a very relaxed atmosphere 🏮 Lots of celebrity autographs hang on the walls, which makes it clear that this really is a popular shop. There's a TV too, making it a space where you can enjoy your meal at a leisurely pace. You can see the scenery outside through the windows, so it feels open too 🪟 The windows showed that it opens at 10:30 and closes at 16:00 on weekdays and 17:00 on weekends 🕐

The Agarie soba that was brought out after a while had an amazing volume of toppings just from the look of it!😋 It's a luxurious bowl with all of Agarie soba's signature toppings: braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), pork spare ribs (soki) and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). First I tasted the soup. It's a delicious, clear soup with a strong bonito broth 🐟

The noodles are homemade fresh noodles, a medium-thick wavy type 🍝 The firmness is so-so, cooked to an exquisite doneness that's neither too soft nor too hard. The waves cling well to the soup, creating a delicious sense of unity.

As for toppings, there were three kinds of pork: braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), pork spare ribs (soki) and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) 🥩 All were simmered to a moderate tenderness, and the flavor had soaked in well. However, the soki and sanmai-niku had some parts with a slight sinewy texture remaining. They weren't completely melt-in-your-mouth, but they were at a level you can enjoy well enough. The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) were especially tender, simmered to the point where the meat comes right off the bone, which was wonderful 👏

Agarie Soba was certainly a delicious Okinawa soba with the dignity of a long-established shop 🌟 The bonito broth in the soup was especially wonderful, and I can well understand why so many celebrities love it. The noodles are carefully made, as you'd expect from homemade noodles, and the volume of toppings is impeccable. However, if you ask whether it's perfect, the parts with a slight sinewy texture bother me a little, but even so, it's an Okinawa soba well within the "pretty tasty" level 😊

Rating (10.5/15): It's perfectly tasty. Access from the national highway in Jitchaku, Urasoe City is good. Agarie Soba Urasoe Main Branch was a shop where I could clearly see why it continues to be loved by so many people as a long-established name in the Okinawa soba world 🏆 It serves a highly satisfying bowl with a refined soup with a strong bonito broth and a hearty volume of toppings. It's a shop I'd like both Okinawa soba beginners and veterans to try at least once. When you're looking for Okinawa soba in the Urasoe area, please do pay a visit to Agarie Soba 👍
