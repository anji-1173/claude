Soba & Shuzen-dokoro Ipponmatsu

そば＆酒膳処一本松
Kuwae, Chatan TownUniqueRating 9.5/15

An izakaya at night that serves Okinawa soba lunches during the day. It's a treasure trove of unusual dishes like taco soba, tomato sauce soba, and tropical soba, and its signature is the soupless dandan-noodle-style "Okinawan-Style Mapo Soba." The chewy fresh noodles are delicious.

I ate here in November 2023. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (Oct 1, 2023 to Feb 29, 2024)," and I went here as my 12th of 34 shops 😊

I'd thought this shop was an ordinary izakaya, but thanks to this stamp rally I learned for the first time that they do Okinawa soba lunches at midday (;^ω^)

The interior does feel like an izakaya, but this time I decided to order the "Okinawan-Style Mapo Soba" that was featured in the stamp rally booklet.

This dish was also featured at the very beginning of the menu. Is it their top pick, I wonder?

After ordering, as I flipped through the menu, I noticed that Ipponmatsu has quite an array of unusual Okinawa soba dishes 😁

While I was enjoying looking at all the quirky dishes, the "Okinawan-Style Mapo Soba" I ordered arrived.

Usually I'd taste the broth, but this Okinawa soba is like soupless dandan noodles; instead of broth, it's mapo tofu sitting on top of Okinawa soba noodles.

So I decided to eat it by mixing the mapo tofu with the noodles 😊

The noodles seem to be fresh noodles, with a chewy texture, and they're delicious 😋 I'd imagined the mapo tofu would be a bit spicy, but it's not spicy at all; this is a flavor even kids would have no problem with 😁

The crunchy texture of the onions is a nice accent.

Rating (9.5/15): It was an Okinawa soba like a mapo tofu version of soupless dandan noodles 😊 The fresh noodles had a chewy texture and were delicious. Since it's mapo tofu, personally I'd have liked it a bit spicy.
