Honke Kame Soba Untama-ten

本家亀そば うん玉店
Ikeda, NishiharaRating 9/15

A Kame Soba group shop in Ikeda, Nishihara, bustling with local customers. Its feature is a fairly robust soup in which agu pork broth combines with the umami of stir-fried meat and vegetables, and the signature dish is the Agu Soba at ¥930. The menu is also extensive, with set meals, rice bowls, and more.

On this Okinawa soba journey, I went to "Honke Kame Soba Untama-ten" in Ikeda, Nishihara Town 😄 I ate here in May 2025 😆

The exterior is striking, with a well-worn sign standing under the blue sky and an entrance made of wood. A big sign along the road also had parking directions.

A one-item service coupon I received when I ate Okinawa soba before at "Isagawa Shokudo" in Nago City. When I looked at the back, it said it could also be used at "Honke Kame Soba Untama-ten," so this time I visited with that service coupon 😁

When I entered the shop, it was bustling with local customers. Inside there are table seats and tatami raised seating, and the atmosphere of being rooted in the community is nice. As expected of Kame Soba, it's bustling with local customers 😆

Looking at the ticket machine at the entrance, the menu is quite extensive, with not only Okinawa soba but also set meals, rice bowls, curry, and more. What I ordered this time was the "Agu Soba" for ¥930. And I used the service coupon to get jushi (Okinawan seasoned rice) as well. The combination of Okinawa soba and jushi is always welcome 👍

It was a little different from the bowl I imagined from the name "Agu Soba." What came out was a bowl like vegetable soba, topped with stir-fried bean sprouts, onions, and pork 😄

First, my usual sip of the soup.

The agu pork broth soup combines with the umami from the stir-fried meat and vegetables for a fairly robust flavor. The agu broth and the broth from the stir-fried pork and vegetables blend well. It's ordinarily delicious 👍

Next, the noodles. The noodles are the thin straight type with a slightly soft texture. They're easy to eat together with the stir-fried meat and vegetables and blend well with the robust soup.

The toppings are stir-fried bean sprouts, onions, and pork. You can eat the vegetables together, and the umami of the pork and vegetables adds to the soup. It wasn't what I pictured from the name "Agu Soba," but as a vegetable soba it's an ordinarily delicious bowl 😋

I also enjoyed the jushi that I got with the service coupon. A service coupon that links the earlier Isagawa Shokudo to this Untama-ten is fun and very much in the spirit of an Okinawa soba journey 😁

Rating (9/15): The robust soup, combining agu pork broth with the umami of stir-fried meat and vegetables, was memorable. The noodles are thin, straight, and slightly soft. It wasn't the "Agu Soba" I'd imagined and was more like a vegetable soba, but it had the steady reliability typical of Kame Soba and was ordinarily delicious. It's understandable why it's bustling with local customers 😄
