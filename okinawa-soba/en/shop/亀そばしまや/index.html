Kame Soba Shimaya

亀そばしまや
Uchidomari, GinowanRating 9/15

An independent Kame Soba shop with a striking red-tiled roof. The gentle Agu pork broth soup is the familiar Kame Soba flavor. The signature Honke Kame soba is topped with melt-in-your-mouth soft-bone pork ribs and braised pork belly, and there's an autograph from Yamakawa too.

I ate here in September 2024 😄 This shop in Uchidomari, Ginowan City, is an independent "Kame Soba" shop, so you can enjoy an atmosphere and flavor similar to the other Kame Soba affiliated shops. The exterior has a striking red-tiled roof, with a warm atmosphere that seems loved by tourists and locals alike.

I went inside and this time ordered the recommended "Honke Kame soba". It's the signature item, featured prominently on the menu too.

The soba that was served looked simple, yet I could sense it was carefully made.

First, the soup: the broth made with Agu pork had a very gentle flavor. You can sense the richness of the meat, but it's not heavy, finished to be easy to drink. A comforting flavor that seems likely to appeal to a wide range of ages. It's the same flavor as the other Kame Soba shops 😄

The noodles are thin and straight, finished on the soft side. The smooth, slippery feel going down is pleasant, and they clung well to the soup. Personally I'd be even happier with a bit more chewiness, but as it is, it's a classic flavor.

And the toppings are mainly soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku). Both are simmered until melt-in-your-mouth, and their tenderness, crumbling apart in your mouth, was appealing. The seasoning is just right too, with a perfectly balanced sweet-savory taste.

Overall, while not flashy, it was a bowl with a reliable flavor. What was especially memorable was the gentleness of the soup. I think it's something that a wide range of people, from Okinawa soba beginners to local regulars, can enjoy.

Rating (9/15): Simply tasty, and you can enjoy the same Kame Soba as at the other shops 😄
