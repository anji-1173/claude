Yanbaru Soba

山原そば
Izumi, Motobu TownOld Okinawan houseRating 9/15

A popular shop with lines, marked by an old house with cement tiles in Izumi on the Soba Highway. The distinctive dark-colored soup reminiscent of Tojin soba and the chewy, characterful pork spare ribs (soki) set it apart from other shops.

I ate here in December 2024 😆　This time I made my way to "Yanbaru Soba" in Izumi, Motobu Town! I'd actually been curious about this shop for a while, and I was finally able to visit ✨

As you can see, it has a very characterful exterior like an old house. It's a very Okinawan building with cement tiles, with an appearance that truly lets you feel the atmosphere of "Yanbaru" 🏠　The surroundings are peaceful country roads, which also add to its feel as a famous shop.

The sign in front of the shop says "Yanbaru Soba," and you get the sense that it's a soba shop rooted in the local community. There's a parking lot too, so access by car is easy 🚗

Inside, lots of handwritten menus are posted on the walls, and it has a very homey atmosphere.

This time I ordered the "Soki Soba," which you could call the signature item. Lots of tourists were coming in too, and the shop was quite busy 😄

After waiting a while, a steaming soki soba was brought out. It looks simple, but big pork spare ribs (soki) sit on top with a thud, and it's very voluminous!

First, as usual, the soup 🍜　Taking a sip... this is interesting! The soup's color is quite dark, and I feel it resembles "Tojin soba," the roots of Okinawa soba.

In terms of flavor, it has a distinctive taste a little different from the usual pork bone or bonito broth. You can taste some sweetness too, and my impression was that maybe it's soy sauce-based? It's quite a unique soup that I've never tasted at other shops. Opinions might be divided on this, but I found it interesting 😋

Next, I try the noodles. Medium-thick straight noodles with a softish texture.

And the pork spare ribs (soki) I was curious about... these were distinctive too! When I ate them, they were quite chewy, with a solid sinewy texture remaining. The texture was clearly different from the pork spare ribs (soki) at other shops; it was a type of pork spare ribs (soki) I'd never eaten before. Maybe the cooking method or cut is different 🤔

Honestly, the texture of these pork spare ribs (soki) was a first-time experience for me. It might be a bit surprising for people used to tenderly stewed pork spare ribs (soki), but I think this is interesting in its own way as part of "Yanbaru Soba's" personality.

Looking around the shop, lots of tourists were coming in, which shows how popular it is. Since it's in the tourist area of Motobu Town, I imagine many people stop by after sightseeing at places like the Churaumi Aquarium or the Bise Fukugi Tree Road 🐠

Also, JUNGLIA OKINAWA, which opened on July 25, 2025, is close by too 😄

It seems to be loved by the locals too, and I spotted a few customers who looked like regulars. You can really tell it's cherished as a shop rooted in the community.

My overall impression is that this shop serves a distinctive style of Okinawa soba that sets it apart from other shops. Both the soup and the pork spare ribs (soki) were characterful creations that defied expectations in a good way. If you love Okinawa soba, I think it's worth experiencing at least once 👍

Rating (9/15): Solidly tasty. If you have the chance to visit Motobu Town, be sure to drop by. It's not that far from the Churaumi Aquarium or JUNGLIA, so it's an easy location to stop by while sightseeing. However, it's a popular shop and can get crowded, so I recommend visiting with plenty of time to spare ⏰　I'm satisfied to have come across delicious Okinawa soba again this time!
