Meshiya Hiroshi

めし屋ひろし
Haebaru Town, TomoriRating 9/15

A "meshiya" (eatery) boasting an overwhelming variety across 14 pages of menus. Everything from set meals with free rice refills to all-you-can-drink at night. The jiggly, melt-in-your-mouth pig's feet (tebichi) soba pairs well with a generous helping of green onions.

I ate here in January 2024. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29)", and I went as my 32nd of 34 shops 😊

Once again I came here before my hobby of surfing (Itoman City: Suicide Cliff point). To match the 11:30 opening time, I waited in the parking lot in front of the shop from about 11:15 😄

Then a staff member noticed me and called out to me around 11:20, about 10 minutes before opening, and I went inside as the first customer of the day 😆

I had decided to order whatever was featured in the stamp rally booklet, so I ordered the "tebichi soba" without even looking at the menu in the shop.

While I was ordering I checked the menu, and it's quite extensive 😄

Wow, just as I finished photographing the menus, the "tebichi soba" I ordered arrived right away 😆 At "Warai Soba", where I ate yesterday, I was also the first customer, but the waiting time was completely different. You want to eat Okinawa soba quickly and casually, and here they were fully prepared and it came right away lol. Impressive.

First I taste the soup 😆 It's a relatively light pork bone broth with a slight pork bone kick. Simply tasty.

Next I try the noodles. The medium-thick wavy noodles have a soft texture, and since there's plenty of green onion, eating the crunchy onions together with the noodles works nicely 😆

The pig's feet (tebichi) topping is really jiggly and melt-in-your-mouth, and simply delicious. It melts in your mouth almost like a drink 😋

Rating (9/15): Simply tasty. True to the shop name "meshiya" (eatery), it's not just Okinawa soba — the other menu items are quite extensive too.
