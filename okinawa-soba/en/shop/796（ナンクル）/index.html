796 (Nankuru)

796（ナンクル）
Okinawa CityProvisional #6RecommendedRating 13.5/15

Provisional #6 (12/15, 3rd visit). The No.1 agu-broth shop, in Chuo, Okinawa City. A cloudy white broth that's pork-bone-based yet not too heavy, by far the creamiest and richest. It clings superbly to the thick, wavy noodles. There's also the famous soki soba and unusual monthly specials.

(3rd visit) This article was created with AI (Claude 4 Sonnet), with some additions of my own lol🍜✨ I ate here in December 2024.

This time I joined the Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally for the first time in a while, and revisited "796 (Nankuru)" in Chuo, Okinawa City, which I've already introduced among my recommended shops😊 This was my third visit here, and no matter how many times I eat it, it's reliably delicious; it's an Okinawa soba shop especially proud of its rich broth made with agu pork.

This time I ordered the "soki soba" with a soft-boiled egg topping. I've had it several times before, so I waited in the shop for a while expecting its reliable deliciousness. Looking at the bowl when it was brought out, the rich-looking broth really whets the appetite🤤

First, as usual, I taste the broth. The pork bone broth based on agu pork is by far the creamiest and richest of all the Okinawa soba shops I've eaten at😋 When you take a sip, a mellow umami spreads out, and you can also taste the refined sweetness unique to agu pork. Despite being this rich, it isn't too heavy, and the exquisite balance that makes you want to drink it all to the last drop is wonderful. As a pork-bone-based Okinawa soba shop, I definitely think it's the most delicious.

Next, the noodles. These also have their reliably chewy texture and cling superbly to the rich broth. The thick, wavy noodles grab hold of the broth, so you can savor the umami of the agu broth with every bite. The noodle texture is the same as last time, with firm springiness yet a gentle mouthfeel, and delicious✨

This time I ordered a soft-boiled egg as a topping, but this turned out a bit disappointing😅 The egg was served cold, so the temperature difference with the piping-hot broth bothered me. The soft-boiled doneness was good, but I wish it had been served a little warmer.

As for the soki, compared to previous visits, some sinewy texture remained, and it felt a little lacking in that melt-in-your-mouth tenderness. I had the impression it used to be simmered softer, so maybe it comes down to cooking timing or individual variation.

Actually, this shop recently started selling online through an e-commerce site, so I once ordered enough for my family online and tried it at home. However, since it's shipped chilled, it couldn't match the freshly made deliciousness of eating it at the shop, and it felt pricey too💦 Okinawa soba really is best enjoyed piping hot at the shop.

Even so, the quality of the broth and noodles is as high as ever, and the rich deliciousness of the agu broth is something special you can't taste anywhere else. Even on my third visit, that deliciousness hasn't changed, and I can well understand why people want to come back😊

In the stamp rally you end up going around all sorts of shops, but having a delicious shop as a benchmark makes it easier to compare with other shops. Nankuru is definitely one of those benchmark shops.

Rating (12/15): Nankuru delivered reliable deliciousness again this time. The rich agu broth is superb as always, and it pairs perfectly with the noodles. The soft-boiled egg and the condition of the soki were a little disappointing, but overall I think it maintains a high level. When you're in the Okinawa City area, it's a recommended Okinawa soba shop I'd love for you to try at least once✨

Noodles: ★★★★✬（4.5）

― 2nd visit (November 2023) ―

(2nd visit) I ate here in November 2023. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29)," and I went as my 14th shop out of 34😊

It's been about a year since I last ate here, but I think this "796 Nankuru" is the most delicious of the agu-broth Okinawa soba shops, so I'm looking forward to it😁

This time I decided to order the "Herb-Scented Butter Chicken Curry Soba" that was featured in the stamp rally booklet😊

My first time having curry soba at Nankuru. First, a gulp of the broth😁 You can faintly taste butter, and the agu-broth curry soup isn't spicy, but it's finished creamy and delicious😋

The noodles are thick, wavy noodles with a chewy texture, and these are tasty too. I really thought 796 (Nankuru) is high-level.

Rating (13/15): This is my second time, but as expected, among agu-broth Okinawa soba shops it's very high-level; I think it's the most delicious. The butter chicken curry soba this time is delicious too, but the regular agu-broth soba is also creamy and delicious, so please do try it.

― 1st visit (December 2022) ―

(1st visit) A shop that had just opened on September 30, 2022. I went to eat here in December 2022.

I've tried several Okinawa soba shops that use agu pork broth, but I think the deliciousness of this broth is No.1. It's finished incredibly creamy, and I think it's clearly different from other shops' agu broth.

You can choose between regular noodles and healthy noodles (whole wheat), and it's a system where you add toppings from the menu as you like. No other shop has this system either.

This time I had the "Okinawa soba, regular noodles (¥780)" with a "rafute (¥350)" topping.

Chewy noodles with creamy agu broth is the best lol. The rafute topping is generous too, well-seasoned and tasty. For agu-broth Okinawa soba, I recommend 796 Nankuru.

Rating (13.5/15): If you like agu-broth Okinawa soba, please do give it a try.
