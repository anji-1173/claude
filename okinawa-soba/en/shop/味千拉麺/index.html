Ajisen Ramen

味千拉麺
Nakagusuku Village (Nakagusuku SA, northbound/up line)Rating 9.5/15

A Kumamoto ramen shop at Nakagusuku SA (up line) on the Okinawa Expressway. Although it's a ramen shop, it serves authentic bonito-broth Okinawa soba, and the vegetable soba loaded with stir-fried vegetables is popular too. Good for a bowl during a drive.

― 2nd visit (January 2025) ―

*This article was created with "ChatGPT 5.1 (Thinking)" and partly added to by me. I went to eat here in January 2025 😄　This time I stopped by "Ajisen Ramen" at Nakagusuku SA on the up line of the Okinawa Expressway〜🚗💨

With its red exterior, big "RAMEN" lettering, and a red-tiled roof standing proudly, it catches your eye right away even within the service area. It's a shop with a strong image as a ramen place originating in Kumamoto, Kyushu, but here at the Nakagusuku SA location they quietly serve authentic Okinawa soba too.

First, I started by buying a meal ticket at the ticket machine beside the entrance 🎫　The buttons are packed in rows, with everything from dandan noodles and spicy miso ramen to fried rice sets, so you could easily agonize over it, but of course this is my "Okinawa soba tour" (lol), so I pressed the "Vegetable Soba" button.

I handed my meal ticket in at the counter, got a number tag, helped myself to water, and went to my seat. Inside, table seats and counter seats are nicely balanced, and the atmosphere is bright and clean ☀️　The walls are decorated with Okinawan photos and posters, making it a slightly relaxing space, just right for a rest stop during sightseeing. It was before noon on a weekday, so there weren't many customers, and time flowed at a leisurely pace.

After a while my number was called, and when I went to pick it up at the counter, I came face to face with a steaming "Vegetable Soba" ✨　The aroma rising from the dark bowl was a truly appetizing mix of savory stir-fried vegetables and bonito broth. On top was a heaping mound of stir-fried vegetables like cabbage, bean sprouts, and carrots, with bits of meat and fish cake (kamaboko) visible here and there. Bright red pickled red ginger sat neatly in the center, and the colors looked great too. Kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori) was properly on standby at the side as well—a visual that says everything is ready.

First, a sip of the soup. The base is a solid bonito broth, with the umami and oil of the stir-fried vegetables melted into it, giving it a flavor with a slowly spreading richness. It's surprisingly light on the palate, but it's the type where the umami slowly follows afterward. Since "Ajisen Ramen" is known for its signature Kumamoto pork bone ramen, I'd assumed it would lean more toward ramen, but I was pleasantly surprised. Rather than a "fake Okinawa soba" made by a ramen shop, it felt like a properly authentic Okinawa soba soup paired with stir-fried vegetables, and I thought, "Oh, I might like this direction" 😊

The noodles are a thin straight type, boiled slightly on the soft side, with a squishy mouthfeel.

The stir-fried vegetables are cooked through, with a nice balance between the sweetness of the cabbage and the crunchiness of the bean sprouts. The meat mixed in here and there has just the right presence without being too assertive. Mixing in the pickled red ginger little by little tightens up the richness of the fat and changes the flavor, which is fun too. Partway through I tried adding a few drops of kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori), and with its tingling heat and aroma, the soup transformed into something with yet another face 🌶

The Okinawa soba I recently ate at "Wazaya" in the National Theatre, also run by a ramen shop, had a strong ramen-leaning arrangement, but the "Vegetable Soba" at Nakagusuku SA leans quite orthodox. I found it a bit interesting that even ramen shops can go in such different directions 😄　It's not the type that hits you hard with personality; it's in the position of "a solidly tasty bowl you can eat with peace of mind." I think it's perfect when you want to stop by quickly during a drive and fill your stomach.

Personally, I do feel I'd have liked it even more if the noodles were just a little firmer, but you can clearly taste the bonito aroma in the soup, and with plenty of vegetables it was satisfying too.

Rating (9/15): Just like last time, I thought it was solidly tasty. I thought the Okinawa soba made by a ramen shop holds its own against specialty Okinawa soba shops. How about it when you stop by on a sightseeing sightseeing drive 😄

― 1st visit (June 2023) ―

In June 2023 I found it at the Nakagusuku service area on the airport-bound (up) line of the Okinawa Expressway and tried eating here 😄

While driving on the expressway, with no research as usual, I was planning to have lunch at some Okinawa soba shop, and wondering "Is there an Okinawa soba shop at the service area?" I tried Nakagusuku SA.

Then the outdoor sign I found said "Ajisen Ramen." I thought, "Ah, a ramen shop," but I wondered "Maybe they serve Okinawa soba too?" and headed to the shop entrance anyway 😁

And sure enough, on the sign at the shop entrance, the main items were of course lots of ramen dishes, but there were a few soba items too 😄

I thought "Lucky," but the ramen menu looked delicious too 🤤

Since it's a ramen shop, I hesitated for a moment about getting ramen, but I remembered my resolve when I decided on my Okinawa soba tour (wanting to conquer every Okinawa soba shop on the main island), and chose "Okinawa Soba" (^_^;)

First, my usual sip of the soup 😄　I thought it was delicious, with an authentic bonito broth base. The noodles are a thin straight type. Soft and solidly tasty.

The toppings are tender braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) that has soaked up the flavor, and fish cake (kamaboko). These too were solidly 😋

Rating (9.5/15): Solidly tasty. I thought the Okinawa soba made by a ramen shop holds its own against specialty Okinawa soba shops. When I googled this ramen shop, it seems to be Kumamoto pork bone ramen, but the Okinawa soba is bonito-based, not pork bone lol　It seems this is the only location in Okinawa, so how about it when you stop by on a drive 😄
