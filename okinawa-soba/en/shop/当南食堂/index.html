Tonan Shokudo

当南食堂
Noborikawa, Okinawa City (near Southeast Botanical Gardens)Rating 9/15

A well-worn local diner right next to the entrance of Southeast Botanical Gardens. Famous for its old-fashioned soba in a clear, golden bonito broth and its distinctive "sake" and "vinegar" condiments that hit hard with chili.

I ate here in November 2024😄 This time I went to "Tonan Shokudo" in Noborikawa, Okinawa City!🏮

The shop's exterior is well-worn and has the air of a place long loved by locals. There's a parking lot too, so it's convenient to visit by car🚗

When you open the glass door at the entrance, a fairly spacious interior with the warmth of wood opens up✨ The area around the counter feels a bit cluttered, but that has its own charm too. Handwritten menus line the walls, and there's a homey, very Okinawan atmosphere.

This time I ordered the "soki soba (large)" from the menu!🍜

The soba that was brought out after a while looks simple, like old-fashioned Okinawa soba. The broth is a clear golden color, bonito-based🐟 It's pretty ordinary.

The noodles are medium-thick and gently wavy. Cooked on the soft side. Personally I like a bit more firmness, but this too gives a feeling of old-fashioned Okinawa soba🍜 However, even though I ordered a large, the amount of noodles felt small compared to other Okinawa soba shops🤔

The soki topping has a light seasoning and is understated. But when I ate it, it was quite sinewy, and my impression was it was mostly bone with little meat to eat🥩 I'd have been happier if it were simmered until falling-apart tender.

On the table were condiments labeled "sake" and "vinegar," so I tried them🔥 These are quite distinctive! The chili heat hits harder than regular kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori), and the vinegar's sourness is strong too🌶️ Adding them to the broth gives it a punchy flavor, so opinions may be divided, but I recommend them to people who like spicy food.

The shop was bustling with customers, and I could tell it's a popular diner👪

Overall, my impression was "plainly tasty Okinawa soba." It doesn't feel especially special, but the old-fashioned diner atmosphere and gentle flavor are its charms🌺

Rating (9/15): Overall my impression is "plainly tasty Okinawa soba," but I recommend it to anyone who wants to experience the atmosphere of an Okinawan diner loved by its customers!😊
