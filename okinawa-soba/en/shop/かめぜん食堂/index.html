Kamezen Shokudo

かめぜん食堂
Mihama, Chatan Town (American Village)Rating 9/15

A shokudo with a Showa-retro atmosphere in a corner of American Village. The wall of soda bottles and tables remade from sewing machine stands are photogenic. The signature dish is the Kamezen soba with melt-in-your-mouth soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) in a light soup fragrant with bonito.

*This article was created with AI (Chat GPT 5) and partially edited by me 😆

I ate here in December 2024 😄　Mihama, Chatan Town: American Village, bustling with tourists. I visited “Kamezen Shokudo,” located in a corner of it.

As you climb the stairs, an orange neon sign and lanterns jump out at you, and it's an atmosphere as if you've slipped back in time to a Showa-era shokudo. The exterior alone is packed with “Okinawa feel,” and it seems even first-timers would get drawn right in.

When you go inside, nostalgic soda bottles line an entire wall, and the tables are actually remade from sewing machine stand legs. Little touches like this look great in photos~ 📸. It's an interior that would make tourists blurt out “So cute!”, and it's certainly a perfect place to drop by during a trip.

This time I ordered the “Kamezen soba,” which bears the shop's name, without hesitation. The bowl brought out had a clear, golden soup with thin wavy noodles. And on top, thin strips of yellow omelet and a large soft-bone pork rib (nankotsu soki) sit there boldly. It looks simple, but it's the classic Okinawa soba style.

First, a sip of the soup. The bonito broth is softly fragrant, a light type with a faint touch of saltiness.

The noodles are thin with a loose wave. A fairly good texture 😄

The soft-bone pork rib (nankotsu soki) is almost melt-in-your-mouth tender. It's so tender it comes apart in your mouth, but there's just a little sinewy texture.

Rating (9/15): It tastes just plain delicious. This shop, being located in Chatan Town, is close to American Village, in a convenient place to drop by between sightseeing. It's a shop I'd love people who want to enjoy Okinawa soba in the Chatan area to visit at least once 😄
