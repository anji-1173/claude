Kishimoto Shokudo Yaedake branch

きしもと食堂八重岳店
Nohara, Motobu TownRating 9.5/15

The Yaedake branch of the long-established "Kishimoto Shokudo", founded in Meiji 38 (1905). The menu has a clean-cut single item: "Kishimoto Soba" with wood-ash noodles. A solid flavor with authentic bonito broth; a line is a given, but turnover is fast.

It's a shop whose name often comes up when you search "Okinawa soba", so I went in March 2023 😀　This was my first time at an Okinawa soba shop in Motobu Town 😁

The menu is just one item: "Kishimoto Soba (extra-large) (large) (small)". Somehow that's cool.

I ordered "Kishimoto Soba (large)" from the meal ticket machine.

First, a sip of the broth. An authentic bonito broth with a solid flavor. It's delicious, but it felt a bit salty. I drank it all to the end, but I thought some people might find it salty.

The noodles are Okinawa soba made using wood ash, a slightly thick medium-thick type. They go well with the broth 😀　The toppings are green onions, fish cake (kamaboko), and lean pork and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), well seasoned, tender and delicious.

Rating (9.5/15): I thought the broth was a bit salty, but the noodles, broth and toppings were all well balanced and delicious overall.
