Maru Soba

まるそば
Maejima, Naha (Tomarin)Rating 9/15

An Okinawa soba eatery near Tomarin (Tomari Port). Its feature is a gentle, very clear broth that seems to be chicken-stock-based, and its signature is the ¥680 meat soba topped with two kinds of meat: braised pork belly and minced meat. There's curry and taco rice too.

*I had AI (Claude 3.5 Sonnet) help write this article too, and I've added and updated parts of it myself lol

I ate here in October 2024 😄

This time I'd like to introduce "Okinawa Soba-dokoro Maru Soba Tomarin Branch" in Maejima, Naha.

The words "Meat Soba" on the banner flags standing out front caught my eye, and curious, I went in 😆

Looking at the menu, besides regular Okinawa soba there's a wide range of dishes like curry and taco rice. This time I ordered the "Meat Soba" I'd come for 😊

The interior has a calm, wood-grain atmosphere with counter and table seats, making it a comfortable space.

The "Meat Soba" arrived 😄 First, as always, let's taste the broth. Its distinctive feature is a very clear broth, different from the pork bone or bonito broth you often see at other shops. It's a gentle flavor that makes me think, "Maybe it's chicken-stock-based?" and it's decently tasty 😋

The noodles are medium-thick, straight fresh noodles. They have moderate firmness and are satisfying to eat. For toppings, in addition to the usual braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), there's also minced meat, a feature you won't find at other shops. However, the braised pork belly still had a bit of sinewy texture, which bothered me.

Rating (9/15): The broth has a clear, gentle flavor different from other shops, and you can feel the creativity in things like using two kinds of meat. However, overall my impression was that it's decently tasty. When you visit Naha, why not stop by once? 😊
