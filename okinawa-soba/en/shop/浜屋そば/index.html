Hamaya Soba

浜屋そば
Miyagi, Chatan TownRating 9/15

A shop popular on social media with lines out the door, near Miyagi Coast (a surfing and diving spot). A light salt-based soup with bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki) and seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). It also runs the food truck "Tomoyan-kun."

This shop is popular on social media and elsewhere😊　I ate here for the first time in quite a while (May 2023)

I ordered the "Hamaya Soba," which has the same name as the shop

First, a sip of the soup. It's a light flavor with just a touch of pork bone, different from other shops' soups😊　The noodles are medium-thick, and the bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki) and seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) toppings also seem to be seasoned with salt, so it's on the light side.

Rating (9/15): Perfectly tasty. It's a popular shop with lines even on weekdays, but turnover is decent, so I think you'll get in after a short wait. There seem to be 2 parking spaces beside the shop and 7 a little way away, but this time I parked far down along the seawall on the coastal road^^;　This sea (Miyagi Coast) is popular for diving on days with no waves and as a surf point when there are waves, so there's a lot of street parking along the seawall too. They also seem to run a food truck, so you could probably eat there without lining up😊
