Kissa Jaan

喫茶ジャーン
Nakaza, Yaese TownUniqueRating 9/15

A retro café inside "Ishi no Yu Inomae-kan" along National Route 331. Here you can enjoy a new-style "Basil Soba": green homemade thin noodles kneaded with sweet basil, a bonito broth soup with lemongrass, and chicken smoked with cherry wood chips.

I went to eat here in January 2024 😊　It was during the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (Oct 1, 2023 – Feb 29, 2024)," and I had just one more shop to go to clear it, so I wanted to eat at a participating shop, but they were closed on holidays, so this time I decided to eat at this shop, which I learned about in the newspaper and which isn't part of the stamp rally.

The entrance had the name "Kissa Jaan" on it, so I went in thinking this was it, but the name of the building was "Ishi no Yu Inomae." Wondering "Huh?", I went inside and asked a staff member, "Is this where the Okinawa soba is?" and was told "Please come in" and shown to the back of the shop. I was glad I had it right 😄

At the top of the menu was the "Basil Soba" introduced in the newspaper. I ordered it 😄

While I was looking around the shop, the "Basil Soba" arrived 😄

First, I taste the soup 😋

As the menu describes, lemongrass seems to be added to the soup rich with bonito broth, and you can taste a faintly refreshing tartness. It's a new flavor you won't find at other shops. It works perfectly well—delicious 😋

Next, the noodles. The homemade noodles are thin and, because sweet basil is kneaded into them, they're a pretty green color 😄

The flavor is nice; these are solidly tasty too. It only shows a little in the photo, but the topping isn't the pork used in Okinawa soba but chicken smoked with cherry wood chips. This is nice too 😁

Rating (9/15): It's a new style of Okinawa soba. The inside also feels like a café with a retro vibe, and I think you can eat at a relaxed pace, so when you're nearby, why not give it a try at least once?
