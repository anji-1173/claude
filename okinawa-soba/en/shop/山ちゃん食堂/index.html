Yamachan Shokudo

山ちゃん食堂
Onaga, TomigusukuRating 8.5/15

A local diner serving Okinawan cuisine. Homestyle Okinawa soba with a blended pork bone × bonito broth where the pork bone comes through, and medium-thick straight noodles, reasonably priced at ¥600 for a large. The soki soba advertised on the banner being sold out is all part of the charm.

I went to eat here in November 2023 😊　It was during the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (Oct 1, 2023 – Feb 29, 2024)," so I wanted to eat at a participating shop, but there were no participating shops in the area where I had errands, so I tried eating at this diner I happened to find 😆

When I went inside, they had only just opened for the day, but several local customers were already seated at tables.

The menu on the wall had "Soki Soba," and the banner outside also said "Soki Soba," so I ordered the "Soki Soba," and the reply was "We don't have it" (^0^;)　"Huh?" I was surprised right off the bat, but this diner isn't an Okinawa soba specialty shop; it's an ordinary diner serving Okinawan cuisine, so I double-checked whether they had no "Okinawa soba"-related items at all, and they said they did have "Okinawa Soba," so I ordered that 😆　Anyway, that was a relief.

The Okinawa soba arrived 😆　First, a sip of the soup. It's a blended broth of pork bone and bonito, a soup where the pork bone comes through. It's ordinary.

Next, the noodles. The medium-thick straight noodles are soft. It's homestyle, very ordinary Okinawa soba. The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) had a bit of sinewy texture.

Rating (8.5/15): It was homestyle, ordinary Okinawa soba 😄　I ordered the large, but partly because the price was a reasonable ¥600, the portion didn't seem that big. I couldn't help laughing that even though the banner outside advertises "Soki Soba," they don't sell it 😆
