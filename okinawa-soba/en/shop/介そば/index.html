Kai Soba

介そば
Kakazu, GinowanRating 10/15

A shop with real skill on Pipeline Street. Proud of its broth from four kinds of dried fish (roasted dried sardines, bonito flakes, tuna flakes, and Spanish mackerel flakes) and its clear, smooth, chewy custom-made fresh noodles. A high level akin to Kinchichi Soba; a bowl that comes close to my recommended shops.

― 2nd visit (December 2023) ―

I ate here in December 2023. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29)," and I went as my 27th shop out of 34😊

I first tried this shop in September 2023, so I know the taste😁 The broth uses four kinds of dried fish (roasted dried sardines, bonito flakes, tuna flakes, and Spanish mackerel flakes), with the bonito broth coming through, and it's delicious.

The noodles are fresh noodles too, clear-looking with a smooth, chewy texture, and delicious.

This time I ordered the "soft-bone pork rib soba" featured in the stamp rally booklet, but after ordering I checked what I'd ordered last time, and it was the same "soft-bone pork rib soba" (^0^;) I regretted a little that I should have chosen a different dish (^0^;)

Let's leave that for another occasion😆

As I tasted before, the broth has bonito broth coming through as always and is just plain tasty😋 The noodles are clear, smooth, and chewy, and these are plainly tasty too.

The soft-bone pork ribs are jiggly and delicious too.

Rating (10.5/15): Just plain tasty. This is my 2nd time at this shop, and I felt it's close to the top tier (recommended shops, every bit as good as recommended shops). Why not try eating at this shop too?

Noodles: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Soup: ★★★✬☆（3.5）

― 1st visit (September 2023) ―

I went to eat here in September 2023😊

With no plan as usual, I was driving along Pipeline wondering, "Is there an Okinawa soba shop around?" when I suddenly found this place and went in lol

Inside, even though it was lunchtime, maybe because it was a Monday weekday, there were few customers and it was quiet.

From the menu, I decided to order the "soft-bone pork rib soba," which the staff told me is "often ordered."

While waiting for the soft-bone pork rib soba, I killed time looking at the menu🤗 It said they use fresh noodles and "noodles with strong firmness," so I imagined, maybe they're as chewy as Ishigufu's?

According to the description, the broth uses four kinds of dried fish. It looks delicious🤤 but only three are written: bonito flakes, tuna flakes, and Spanish mackerel flakes (;^ω^) I wonder if the other one is the roasted dried sardines?

The noodle description also says custom-made fresh noodles, smooth and chewy, which sounds delicious😊

It arrived while I was reading the menu lol The broth looks cloudy and could pass for pork-bone-based. The customary gulp of broth.

Sure enough, as the menu description said, the bonito broth comes through; just plain tasty😋

The fresh noodles look clear and are thin. As described, they're smooth and chewy, and these are plainly tasty too😋 However, it said "strong firmness," but they're not that firm. I felt both the broth and noodles might be similar to "Kinchichi Soba," which I ate in Onna Village. I wonder if there's some connection?

The complimentary jushi is nice too; this one tasted on the light, mild side. The soft-bone pork ribs are meltingly tender, and these are also finished with a light seasoning.

Rating (10/15): Just plain tasty. I thought the broth and noodles were similar to "Kinchichi Soba," which I ate in Onna Village, and found them delicious. Everyone, why not give it a try once? Payment was cash only, so it would be even better if they accepted cashless.

Noodles: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Soup: ★★★✬☆（3.5）
Toppings: ★★★☆☆（3）
