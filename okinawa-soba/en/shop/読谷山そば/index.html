Yuntanza Soba

A hidden gem in Furugen, Yomitan Village, loved by local customers. An elegant bonito broth soup, medium-thick wavy noodles, and light, tender soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). There's a wide menu, including sea lettuce (āsa) and fuchiba (mugwort) soba.

I went to eat here in January 2025 😁😊　At “Yuntanza Soba,” a hidden gem in Furugen, Yomitan Village, I had a bowl loved by local regulars. 📍

Looking only at the exterior, it has a slightly plain appearance, but when you go inside, a more comfortable space than you'd imagine spreads out before you. It's an Okinawa soba shop with the kind of atmosphere where local customers never stop coming. It's shops like this that serve truly delicious Okinawa soba.

What I ordered this time was the “soft-bone pork rib (nankotsu soki) soba.” Looking at the menu, there's quite a rich variety, from half soba to meat soba, soft-bone pork rib (nankotsu soki) soba, sea lettuce (āsa) and fuchiba (mugwort) soba, and so on. Being able to get a large serving of noodles for an extra ¥100 is a nice point for people with big appetites. 😋

First, a sip of the soup. A deep taste with plenty of bonito broth spreads in my mouth. It's never too strong; rather, it's an elegant finish that makes you feel the quality of the ingredients. With this soup, it's so delicious you'll want to drink it all.

The noodles are medium-thick and wavy. They look soft, but with one bite, the way they cling to the soup is the best. The balance of chewiness and softness is kept perfectly, and the texture when you slurp them is irresistible. You can enjoy the goodness of both the parts soaked with soup and the center that retains a little firmness at the same time. ✨

And above all, the “soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki).” Soki has a strong image of bone-in pork, but the soki here is finished on the light side. There's certainly a little sinewy texture, but they're mostly tender.

The soup, noodles, toppings, every element was beautifully balanced, a bowl that made me think again, “Ah, so this is Okinawa soba.” With the contrast to the look of the building, I can see why locals come here so often. Just plain delicious, and nothing more is needed. 🌟

The price is reasonable too, and if you can eat soba of this level, it's a shop I definitely want to drop by whenever I come to Yomitan Village. It's a shop I can truly recommend to Okinawa soba lovers.

Rating (12/15): I thought this shop is also a high-level Okinawa soba shop with a good balance of noodles, soup, and toppings. When you come to Yomitan Village, how about trying it at least once? It's recommended 😄
