Takaesu Soba

高江洲そば
Iso, UrasoeRating 9/15

An extremely popular shop that's sure to have a line, and the home of the "original yushi-dofu soba." A light, salty soup of pork bone, bonito, and kelp loaded with yushi-dofu (soft Okinawan tofu). You write your name on the whiteboard at the entrance and wait.

I ate here in September 2023, at this shop near the Pipeline Road 😁

The other day, on my way home from eating at "Suke Soba" along the same Pipeline Road (in Ginowan City), I found Takaesu Soba, so I decided "I'll go here next," and here I am lol

Go a little way in from Pipeline Road and there's the shop, with a parking lot, but although it was lunchtime, it was amazingly full and I couldn't park (;^ω^)

And there are lots of customers lined up near the entrance!! I could tell it's a popular shop.

"This might be high-level Okinawa soba!" My expectations went up 🤗

I waited in the parking lot with my hazard lights on for a parking space to open up. After a while, customers who had finished eating left, and I finally got parked 😊

But now, waiting in line (;^ω^)

There was a bench outside with a few seats, and elderly people were sitting there, and I couldn't tell whether they were people resting outside after eating or people waiting their turn, so I asked.

It turned out there was a whiteboard at the entrance where you write your name. So I went to the entrance and wrote my name.

After waiting in line for quite a while, my turn finally came to go inside. You buy a ticket at the ticket machine set up near the entrance.

I came here without any research again, but the entrance said "original yushi-dofu soba," so I decided to order the yushi-dofu soba.

The toppings are green onions, egg, shredded pork spare ribs (soki), kūbu (knotted kelp), and yushi-dofu

The kelp reminded me of "Miyazato Soba" in Nago City lol

I ordered the "large," but the bowl seemed on the small side (medium size?)

On the ticket machine, the yushi-dofu soba came in "extra large," "large," and "medium," so even though it says "large," it might be the "medium" size at other shops.

First I tasted the soup 😊 It was like "Hm? What's the base?" and I couldn't really tell the flavor (;^ω^) Sorry for my dull palate (;^ω^) I thought it was very gentle and light. When I googled it, it's said to be a "light, salty soup using pork bone, bonito, and kelp," but since it also contains yushi-dofu, I could taste the flavor of the yushi-dofu liquid too.

The noodles are medium-thick, wavy, and on the soft side. Like the light soup, the other toppings were also lightly seasoned.

Rating (9/15): It's ordinarily tasty, but seeing the line of customers raised my expectations and I set the bar too high ^^; It seems to be a hugely popular shop, so why not try the original yushi-dofu soba?
