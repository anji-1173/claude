Yone Shokudo

よね食堂
Zayasu, TomigusukuRating 9/15

A long-established diner with banners flying that read "Founded 1957". The signature Yone soba has a soup where you can taste both chicken and pork, medium-thin gently wavy noodles, and an everything-on-top lineup of soft-bone pork ribs, bone-in pork ribs, and braised pork belly.

I ate here in February 2024 😁

There was a "Yone soba", which shares the shop's name, so I ordered it. The toppings are soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) — an everything-on-top kind of bowl 😆

Inside, there were celebrity autographs on the wall, but I couldn't make out whose they were (^0^;) One says "All Japan Pro Wrestling", so it's from some pro wrestler, and below I can also see what is probably Yoko Gushiken's autograph 😆

The "Yone soba" has arrived. First, a sip of the soup. It's a soup where you can taste both chicken and pork.

Next I try the noodles. Medium-thin, gently wavy, soft noodles.

The toppings — braised pork belly, bone-in pork ribs, and soft-bone pork ribs — are tender too, but have a sinewy texture.

Rating (9/15): Simply tasty. It's relatively close to Naha Airport, so why not try this long-established flavor at least once 😁
