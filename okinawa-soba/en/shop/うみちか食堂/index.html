I ate here in October 2024 😄　This time I'm introducing "Umichika Shokudo" in Mashiki, Ginowan City.

The exterior is a cream-colored building, marked by a blue sign. Entering the shop, it was bustling with local customers and tourists 😄

Looking at the menu, starting with the basic "Umichika Soba", there's a rich lineup ranging from standard dishes like soft-bone soki soba and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) soba to unique items you don't see much at other shops, like squid ink soba and bean sprout and garlic chive stir-fry soba. In terms of price range, you can enjoy Okinawa soba reasonably 😆

This time I decided to order the signature "Umichika Soba (medium)" (¥700).

The "Umichika Soba" was brought out. First, the clear broth. The gently flavored bonito-stock-based broth comes through.

The noodles used are a thin straight type. Though the texture is on the soft side, they pair outstandingly with the broth 😋

The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) topping is slowly simmered to a melt-in-your-mouth texture. The fat has melted out just right and it's delicious, but a little sinewy texture remaining bothered me. The fish cake (kamaboko) has a moist texture, and the aroma of chopped green onions accents the whole thing.

The side menu is also plentiful, and you can enjoy standard Okinawan meals like jushi (Okinawan seasoned rice) and champon too. For dessert there's also "zenzai", unique to Okinawa, which I recommend to finish off 🍨

Rating (9/15): A gently flavored Okinawa soba with a well-balanced broth, plain tasty. The prices are reasonable too, and it's a shop you can drop into casually. When you come to Ginowan City, why not pay it a visit? It was a simple, warm Okinawa soba shop 😊
