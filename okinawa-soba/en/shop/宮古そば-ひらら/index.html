Miyako Soba Hirara

宮古そば　ひらら
Takayasu, TomigusukuRating 9/15

An unusual Miyako soba shop inside a golf driving range. Its selling point is the traditional-style, un-Instagrammable "Soba" with the toppings hidden under the noodles. A blended broth of pork bone and bonito, with the pork bone coming through strongly.

I ate here in May 2024 😁 I learned about this shop because it was introduced in a local newspaper 😁　But this road is one I often take to the surf point I go to every weekend (Suicide Cliff Point) (^0^;)

I had absolutely no idea there was an Okinawa soba shop at a golf driving range (^0^;)

From the menu, I somewhat randomly ordered the "Nankotsu Soba (regular)."

However, after I finished eating, I looked back at the newspaper article again and regretted it (^0^;)

It seems this shop's selling point was the "Soba" listed at the top of the menu.

Traditional Miyako soba is apparently characterized by the toppings being hidden at the bottom under the noodles, and the "Soba" at the top of the menu seems to be that.

At a glance it looks like just green onions and noodles; it makes "not Instagrammable" its selling point and seems to stick firmly to the old-fashioned traditional Miyako soba style.

This is my first Miyako soba since "Miyako Soba Ai" in Okinawa City, which I visited during the stamp rally, but Ai didn't have that style.

Since it just said "Soba," I mistakenly assumed it was simply plain soba (no toppings) (^0^;)　I should have ordered the "Soba."

This one has the toppings sitting on top of the noodles as usual (^0^;)

The soup is a blended broth of pork bone and bonito, with the pork bone coming through.

It's solidly tasty.

Next, the noodles. Medium-thin, roundish, and straight. A soft type. The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) are meltingly tender too, but there are spots where some sinewy texture remains.

Rating (9/15): Solidly tasty. I recommend the un-Instagrammable "Soba" with the toppings hidden under the noodles, which seems to be traditional for Miyako soba 😄　If you play golf, how about this driving range?
