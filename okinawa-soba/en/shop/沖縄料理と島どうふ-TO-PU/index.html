Okinawa Ryori to Shima-dofu TO-PU

沖縄料理と島どうふ TO-PU
Hamamoto, Motobu TownRating 9/15

Near Churaumi Aquarium, marked by a handmade sign on National Route 449. The shop name "Tōpu" means tofu shop in the Motobu dialect. Its signature dishes are handmade tofu prepared twice a day and the "Okinawa soba everything-on-top set meal" with an appetizer of three kinds of tofu.

I went to eat here in May 2024😄

I learned about this shop when it was featured in a local newspaper.

According to the newspaper, the shop name "Tōpu" means tofu shop in the Motobu dialect, and their biggest selling point is handmade tofu prepared twice a day😁

The signature dish was introduced as the "Okinawa soba everything-on-top set meal," so I decided to order it.

Inside was crowded at lunchtime, so I ate at a table outside.

The appetizer is handmade tofu in three kinds. I got to taste bite-size "okara," "yushi-dofu," and "shima-dofu." The staff explained that they're already seasoned, so please eat them without adding anything. Just plain tasty😆

The broth is a combined bonito and pork bone broth, with the pork bone coming through. Just plain tasty.

Next, the noodles. They're medium-thick and straight, with a cross-section like flat noodles, and soft.

The braised pork belly and soki toppings were a little dry, and some parts had a remaining sinewy texture.

The yushi-dofu (soft Okinawan tofu) on top of the Okinawa soba is handmade, and fluffy and soft😁

Rating (9/15): Just plain tasty😄 It's bustling with both local customers and tourists. It's close to Churaumi Aquarium, so why not stop by here too?
