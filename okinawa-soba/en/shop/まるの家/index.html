Maru no Ie

まるの家
Chatan, Chatan TownRating 9.5/15

A homemade-noodle shop in the amusing location of the second floor of a pachislot parlor. Its pride is chewy, firm, medium-thick straight noodles, and you can change up the flavor with its special homemade kōrēgūsu. A wide-ranging menu with set meals and curry too.

I went to eat here in September 2024 😄 Today's soba tour took me to "Maru no Ie" in Chatan, Chatan Town. This shop is, believe it or not, located on the second floor of a pachislot parlor! ✨ A bit of an amusing location, right? Actually, this atmosphere reminded me of "Garyu Soba" in Nago, which I visited before.

A big sign reading "Homemade Noodles" hangs at the shop's entrance, and you can feel their dedication to handmade noodles!

Right away I ordered the "Soft-Bone Soba" at the ticket machine. Looking at the menu, besides Okinawa soba there was a wide selection of set meals, curry, and more, and it felt like you could eat just about anything, but today I decided to go with the classic Okinawa soba after all 😊.

I sat down and looked at the soft-bone soba that was brought out; it looks substantial. The broth is clear, and it feels like both bonito and pork bone stocks are blended just right. It's a flavor where you can taste both bonito and pork bone.

And then the long-awaited homemade noodles! These were quite delicious 🍜. They're medium-thick straight noodles with a chewy texture and firmness, and you can enjoy a solid bite with every mouthful. This texture is irresistible. They really live up to the "Homemade Noodles" sign!

The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) in the toppings were thoroughly simmered and tender, and I liked how they fell apart in my mouth. However, a bit of sinewy texture remained; the tenderness is perfect, but it's a little disappointing that there's a lot of sinew 😅.

On the table there was also Maru no Ie's special kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori), and adding it instantly gave the flavor a sharp, spicy kick! It's a nice touch that lets you enjoy the second half without getting bored.

Overall, it was a bowl where the deliciousness of the noodles left the strongest impression. The broth and soft-bone pork ribs aren't bad either, but if anything they fall within the ordinary range. The shop's atmosphere was calm too, and I could relax and enjoy my meal 🍴.

Rating (9.5/15): It's decently tasty. The homemade noodles score pretty high 😄 It's in Chatan Town, a tourist area, so why not try it once while sightseeing 😆

Next time I'd like to try other dishes too, but I fully enjoyed this soft-bone soba as well! See you on my next Okinawa soba tour! 👋🍜
