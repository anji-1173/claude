STAND EIBUN

NahaTied provisional #1RecommendedRating 14/15

The second shop of provisional #1 “EIBUN” (14/15). The most popular item is the sea lettuce (āsa) soba. A hidden gem where you can enjoy a bowl of the same level without standing in the main shop's line.

“EIBUN” is the shop I think is the most delicious of all the Okinawa soba shops I've eaten at so far, but I recently found out they opened a second shop around April 2023(?), so I went to eat there in February 2024 😁

This second shop was really close to the main shop (^0^;)

On top of that, it was two doors down from “Okinawa Pork Soba,” which I visited the other day as the 6th of 34 shops in the 13th stamp rally (^0^;)　As usual, I go with no research and just wing it, so I didn't know (^0^;)

What surprised me even more is that on this day too the main shop had its usual line and didn't feel easy to drop into, but the second shop had no line in particular. When I stood in front of the entrance, a staff member guided me in, saying “Please, come in.”

The number of customers is completely different between the main shop and the second shop.

When I went inside, there was a meal ticket machine installed, so I ordered from the menu. I believe the main shop accepted cashless payment, but unfortunately this one seems to be cash only. The meal ticket machine is a new type too, so I hope they'll support cashless payment in the future.

In the staff member's explanation of the menu in front of the meal ticket machine, they said something like “the sea lettuce (āsa) soba is the most popular,” so this time I decided to order the “sea lettuce (āsa) soba” 😁

I was shown to a counter seat, and while I waited I looked around the stylish interior 😄

While I was observing the interior, the “sea lettuce (āsa) soba” arrived. It looks delicious 😋

The blue bowl is the same as at the main shop “EIBUN” 😄　I was about to taste the soup right away, but there were no chopsticks?

There were no chopsticks on the table, and thinking “Huh? Did the staff forget?”, I asked whether there were chopsticks, and the answer was “They're in the drawer” (°°)

When I checked the drawer, they were inside. Looking closely, there was a label-maker sticker in small letters on the counter wall saying that chopsticks and wet towels are in the drawer (^0^;)

I didn't notice (^0^;)　Starting over, I took a sip of the soup. It's a soup with the bonito broth coming through, and I thought, as expected, EIBUN is delicious.

Next, when I tasted the soup together with the sea lettuce (āsa), I could also feel the flavor of the seashore, and this was delicious too 😋　According to what I googled, before opening EIBUN the manager also trained at “Nakamura Soba” in Onna Village, and it reminded me of Nakamura Soba's sea lettuce (āsa) soba too 😆

Next, I have the noodles. The medium-thick fresh noodles have a chewy, springy feel, and they're delicious too 😄　The toppings, braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), still had a bit of sinewy texture, but they're tender and delicious. Moreover, unlike other shops, the soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) were finished with a fairly sweet flavor, rich and deep, and I found these delicious too.

Then, in the second half, I changed up the flavor of the soup with the “gōmāgūsu.” I could feel the nice aroma of sesame oil and the sourness of vinegar too, and it became a new soup. It goes well with it, and I thought this was delicious too 😄

Rating (14/15): Although it's the second shop, I had EIBUN's Okinawa soba for the first time in a while, and as expected I thought it was high-level and delicious. Looking at “noodles,” “soup,” and “toppings” individually, there are other shops that are just as delicious, but I think what's amazing about EIBUN is that the noodles, soup, and toppings are all in balance. Moreover, while the main shop has a line, this one maybe isn't well-known yet, so you can eat the same taste without getting caught in a line, which makes it recommended 😆　Also, it would be even better if they supported cashless payment soon 👍

Noodles: ★★★★✬
Soup: ★★★★★
Toppings: ★★★★✬
