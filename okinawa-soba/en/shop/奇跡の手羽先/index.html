Okinawa soba tour 🚶‍♂️ I ate here in October 2024 😁 I went to "Kiseki no Tebasaki" in Kumoji, Naha! ✨

It's a participating shop in the Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally, and a shop I'd been curious about for a long time. Even the shop name is unique, and I'm excited to see what kind of soba I can eat here! 😄

The exterior is marked by red lanterns, a design where you can tell at a glance, "This is it!"

When you go inside, graffiti art covers every wall, creating a playful space that's somehow nostalgic. Since it usually operates as an izakaya too, it has a homey, casual atmosphere. 🍻

What I had this time was the "Mixed Soba" (large size ¥1,100). It's one of the recommended items on the menu, so my expectations were high! The soba that arrived was loaded with toppings like soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), and chicken wings, and just by looking at it you can tell it's a very generous portion ✨

First, a sip of the soup. The seasoning has a strong bonito broth flavor, and it's really delicious! 😋 It has a punchy seasoning typical of an izakaya, and I can imagine it would be perfect for finishing off a night of drinking. However, it might be too strong for people who like a light taste.

Next, the noodles. They're a thin straight type, boiled on the soft side. They go well with the soup, and the way they slurp right down left an impression. However, if you're expecting the thick noodles and firmness characteristic of Okinawa soba, they might be a bit lacking 🤔 Personally, I would have preferred a bit more firmness.

And the toppings! The "chicken wings," which you could call the highlight this time, were a first for me, but they go surprisingly well with Okinawa soba! The soup has soaked in thoroughly, and their fall-apart tenderness is irresistible 😄 The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) were meltingly tender too, with an exquisitely soft texture that you barely need to chew. On the other hand, the braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) had a bit of sinewy texture left, and my impression was that it would be even better if that were improved.

The Okinawa soba at "Kiseki no Tebasaki" was well seasoned overall, as you'd expect from an izakaya menu, and it was filling. The unique topping of chicken wings gives it personality and a special feel you can't get at other shops. It was a memorable bowl among the stamp rally participating shops too! 🎉

Rating (9.5/15): Overall it was solidly tasty Okinawa soba. A bowl where you can enjoy unique chicken wing soba! Next time I'd like to try other menu items and the izakaya dishes too! 🍜✨😊
