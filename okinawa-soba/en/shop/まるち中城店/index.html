Maruchi Nakagusuku Branch

まるち中城店
Toma, Nakagusuku VillageOld Okinawan houseRating 9/15

A hugely popular shop in an old Okinawan folk house in the farmland area of Nakagusuku Village. A customizable style where you choose from 3 kinds each of broth (light, rich, salt) and noodles (thin, flat, wavy). Its signature is the Maruchi Soba topped with pig's feet, braised pork belly, and soft-bone pork ribs.

I ate here in January 2024 😁 I've conquered the 13th stamp rally, so I'll keep going, aiming to clear every Okinawa soba shop on the main island 👍

This shop is in a rural spot you reach by driving through the farmland area on the sea side of National Route 329 in Nakagusuku Village, but when I arrived, relying on Google Maps, I was stunned (°°)!

There were tons of cars parked, and I could tell there were lots of customers. It may be rude to call it the countryside, but it's an amazingly popular shop.

Inside the old folk house, it was full of customers. However, there were tables set up outdoors too, and I was told that at an outdoor table you could order right away without waiting, so I decided to eat outside 😁 It was a little chilly though (^0^;)

I decided to order the "Maruchi Soba," which shares the shop's name. Since you can choose the broth and noodles, I asked the staff for a recommendation. They said "light broth" and "thin noodles" are ordered a lot, so I ordered that.

The Maruchi Soba arrived 😁 Tasting the broth, the description says it's a blended stock of chicken, pork, and bonito, but it's a somewhat pork-bone-based broth.

Next, the noodles. The straight, thin noodles feel soft. The toppings, pig's feet (tebichi), braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), give it plenty of volume. The soft-bone pork ribs and braised pork belly are a bit dry and have a sinewy texture.

Rating (9/15): It feels average, but there are lots of tourists and it's a popular shop. If you want to experience an old Okinawan folk house, why not give it a try at least once 😆
