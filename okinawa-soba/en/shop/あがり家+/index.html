Agariya+

あがり家+
Iso, Urasoe CityRating 9/15

A locally rooted shop on the grounds of MaxValu. The signature is the "Agariya Special", topped with fragrant charcoal-seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) and a whole boiled egg. It's also fun that you can change up the flavor with wasabi as a condiment.

*This article was created based on AI (ChatGPT-4o) with some additions by me 😁

This time, in August 2024, I went to "Agariya+" in Iso, Urasoe City! The shop's atmosphere has a very local feel, with a scattering of customers who seemed to be regulars.

From the menu, what I had this time was the manager's recommendation, the "Agariya Special". Just from the name, it has a special feel, right ✨

The first thing that caught my attention was the broth's unique flavor. It's a blended stock of pork bone and bonito, but the pork bone comes forward gently, and it has more depth than I expected. You can taste the bonito flavor too, with the pork bone base gently working: that's the balance. It's a solid flavor, but the way it isn't too heavy is nice, and it feels ordinary 😊.

The noodles are medium-thin, finished on the soft side. I think I prefer a bit more chewiness. But the pairing of soft noodles and this broth is surprisingly good, and the way it spreads softly in your mouth feels nice. And something you don't see much at other shops is the boiled egg. A boiled egg on Okinawa soba was a bit of a surprise, but it works as an accent in its own way, and I thought it was interesting.

The seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), which you could call the star this time, give off the fragrant aroma of charcoal, and you can enjoy the aroma with every bite. The cartilage part is jiggly, soft and easy to eat. However, the meat part had a bit of a sinewy texture, and honestly I'd have liked it a bit more melt-in-your-mouth 😅. But people who like this level of searing will surely find it irresistible!

On top of that, the wasabi that came as a condiment was a slightly surprising accent. Wasabi on Okinawa soba…? I thought, but it goes surprisingly well! When you put a little dab of wasabi on the soki and eat it, the flavor changes to something refreshing, and it's delicious in its own way. It reminded me of "Ishigufu" and "Su~gi~ji", which has now moved to Onna Village and changed its name to "Sakihama Seimen". There was a nostalgic feeling, along with new discoveries, and it was a happy time 😌.

Overall, the Agariya Special at "Agariya+" is a well-balanced bowl throughout. But if you ask whether it has a standout point that makes it particularly special, maybe not really. Still, it's plenty for enjoying local Okinawa soba, and I felt it has a stability that makes you want to come back again and again. If I get the chance, I'd like to try other menu items too 🍜.

Rating (9/15): Everything is average and plain tasty. I have no complaints, but honestly I'd have liked a little more impact. This shop on the grounds of MaxValu has an unusual paid parking lot with no gate bar (cameras at the entrance/exit). The payment machine is on the shop side, so be careful. (You can get a free ticket from the shop, so parking is effectively free.)
