Udun

御殿（うどぅん）
Nakayama, NagoRating 9/15

An Okinawan restaurant on the Soba Kaido (Prefectural Route 84), across from Okashi Goten. Dishes include the signature "Udun Soba" and sea lettuce (āsa) soba served with jushi. A bonito-based broth with roundish thick noodles.

In July 2023 I went to eat at "Udun", across from Okashi Goten in Nago City, along Prefectural Route 84, which is known as the "Okinawa Soba Kaido (Okinawa Soba Road)"😄

The menu said the "Udun Soba," which shares the shop's name, was the No.1 recommendation, but I didn't feel like eating anything too heavy, so I ordered the "Āsa Soba"😄

Every soba on the menu came with "jushi (Okinawan seasoned rice)"😄

First, as is my custom, I take a sip of the soup to taste it😁　It's a bonito-based broth, and it's perfectly tasty. Next I try the noodles together with the āsa (seaweed)😄　The āsa has a nice briny aroma of the sea, and the noodles are roundish, thick and soft, unlike anything at other shops. The noodles feel a little on the soft side.

The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and the pork spare ribs (soki) are also tender and well-seasoned, and perfectly tasty.

Rating (9/15): I think it's perfectly tasty, but what was disappointing was that when I walked in, the place wasn't full, just a fair number of customers, yet nobody showed me to a table. All the staff were in the kitchen and no one guided me like at other shops, so I was a bit at a loss (^_^;)　I just sat down at an empty seat and waited a while, but even when they brought food to other customers' tables, they didn't notice at all that I had just come in (^_^;)

After delivering the other customers' food they hurried back into the kitchen; maybe they're short-handed, as there seemed to be few staff.

Unable to wait any longer, I peeked into the kitchen and ordered, and they said, "Oh, you hadn't ordered yet?" They hadn't noticed at all that I was a new arrival (^_^;)　I couldn't help feeling that they might not be able to compete on Prefectural Route 84, known as the "Okinawa Soba Kaido." I came away with a disappointing impression lol
