Akagawara Soba Maruya

赤瓦そば　まる屋
Heshiki, Nakijin VillageRating 10/15

The northernmost of the shops participating in the 13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally. A new building with a red-tile roof, serving a pork-bone-based soup with thin straight noodles. Its signature dish is the tsukemen-style "Kawara Soba."

I went to eat here in October 2023 😊 How did I find out about this shop? A few days earlier I learned from a local newspaper that the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally, 'The Road to Soba Mastery'" was being held from October 1, 2023 to February 29, 2024, and that this shop was a participant, so I came to eat here lol

I wanted to visit every Okinawa soba shop on the main island of Okinawa, and I've been going for about three years now, but I didn't know there was a stamp rally like this (;^ω^) As usual, my antenna is lacking (;^ω^)

I'm told this stamp rally has a record 34 shops entered. Anyway, aiming to complete all the shops within the period, I first came to this "Akagawara Soba Maruya." Among the shops entered this time, it's the northernmost Okinawa soba shop 😁

From the Okinawa soba section of the menu, I decided to order the "Maruya Soba, Large," which was at the very top. The thing at the top of the menu, called "Kawara Soba," which you eat like tsukemen, seems to be the main selling point. But since I'm aiming to eat ordinary Okinawa soba, I chose from the Okinawa soba section 😁

First, my usual sip of the soup. As introduced in the stamp rally, it's definitely pork-bone based 😋 The soup is ordinarily delicious. Next, the noodles.

The noodles are fairly soft, thin straight noodles. These are ordinarily delicious too.

Next, the toppings. Pickled red ginger, green onions, fish cake (kamaboko), bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). Quite a lot.

However, the toppings are generous and the meat is tender, but there's a lot of meat with a somewhat sinewy texture.

Rating (10/15): The pork-bone-based soup and straight thin noodles were ordinarily delicious 😄 However, the meat has a sinewy texture. Why not join the stamp rally yourselves?
