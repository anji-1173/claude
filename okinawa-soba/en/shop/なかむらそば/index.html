Nakamura Soba

なかむらそば
Seragaki, Onna VillageProvisional #2RecommendedRating 13.5/15

Provisional #2 (13.5/15). A popular shop along National Route 58 where you can enjoy your soba while gazing at the sea. The specialty "Asa Soba," with sea lettuce (āsa) kneaded into the noodles and fish cake, is a must-eat. The jumbo braised pork belly soba is limited to 7 servings a day.

At first I was touring Okinawa soba shops within Nago, but after seeing it introduced as delicious on social media, I went to "Nakamura Soba" in Onna Village along National Route 58 (November 2022).

This is also a popular shop where a line forms quickly, so I went early. I somehow managed to get inside without getting caught in the line lol

I ordered the "Asa Soba" recommended in the shop. Sea lettuce (āsa) is a staple in Okinawa. Sea lettuce miso soup, clear soup, and sea lettuce tempura are delicious too. To begin with, "āsa" is what Okinawans call the seaweed "hitoegusa." It clings to rocks in the shallows along Okinawa's coast, and it seems it's in season and delicious from February to May.

It's rich in dietary fiber, minerals, and calcium, and in particular contains plenty of beta-carotene, so it seems effective for anti-aging and cancer prevention. Apparently it has about 8 times the calcium of milk and about twice the folic acid of spinach (according to information in the shop). Maybe this is the source of the old-time Uchinanchu (Okinawans) being No. 1 in longevity? lol

First, a sip of the broth. This one also has plenty of bonito-based stock coming through and is tasty. The moment I drank it, it had a deliciousness unlike any shop I'd been to so far. The noodles are thin, and sea lettuce is kneaded into both the noodles and the fish cake (kamaboko), a creative touch and deliciousness not found at other shops. The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) was also tender and delicious. I thought the balance of broth, noodles, and toppings ranked near the top among all the Okinawa soba shops I'd eaten at so far.

I ate by the window, so it was Okinawa soba while looking at the sea ✌ When the weather is good, the sea in Onna Village turns a beautiful emerald green, so if your timing is right, both the taste and the view will be the best.

Rating (13.5/15): Well, for now I'm ranking each shop, but it's not like there's a huge difference in taste, and there's personal preference too, so I think it's only a hair behind #1. This time I had the "Asa Soba," but next time I want to try the "Jumbo Braised Pork Belly Soba, limited to 7 servings a day" that was on the sign outside 🤤 It seems you won't be able to get it unless you go early.
