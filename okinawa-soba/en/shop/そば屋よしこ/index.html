Sobaya Yoshiko

そば屋よしこ
Izumi, Motobu TownRating 9/15

A long-established shop with a yellow sign standing at the gateway to Soba Road (Prefectural Route 84). Featured on Horiemon's Teriyaki app, its specialty is a hearty pig's feet soba with three big pieces of pig's feet (tebichi) topped with lettuce.

I ate here in November 2024 😄 This time I'd like to introduce "Sobaya Yoshiko" in Izumi, Motobu Town. This shop was featured on Horiemon's Teriyaki app, and it's a place I'd been curious about for a while 🔍

Located along Prefectural Route 84, this is a long-established soba shop that has been loved by locals for many years. Its bright yellow sign is the landmark, and the noren (shop curtain) reading "Yoshiko Soba" flutters in the wind 🏮

Inside, there's an old-fashioned atmosphere, and many photos and autographs were posted on the walls. It was bustling with local customers and tourists, and lots of people came in that day too 😄

Looking at the menu, it lists classic dishes like soki soba, pig's feet (tebichi) soba, and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) soba. The menu has photos, so it's easy to understand 👍

This time I ordered the "Pig's Feet Soba" that was featured on Horiemon's Teriyaki app. Tebichi means pig's feet; it's packed with collagen and is a popular topping among women too 🐷

After waiting a while, the "Pig's Feet Soba" was brought out. First, the visual impact! Like "Oshiro Soba" in Okita, Nago, which I introduce among my "Okinawa soba shops that don't fall short of my recommended shops," it's distinctive for being topped with lettuce 🥬

As always, I start by tasting the broth. This shop's broth is a blended stock of pork bone, bonito, and kelp, with a gentle flavor where the bonito comes through a bit 😋

Next, the noodles. The medium-thick, straight, softish noodles cling well to the broth and are decently tasty 🍜

And as for the pig's feet (tebichi) topping, there are three big pieces, making it very voluminous! They're slowly simmered, so the meat is meltingly soft and tender. Packed with collagen, it seems good for your skin too ✨

After finishing, I was left with a warm feeling of nostalgia. It was a bowl that made me understand why it's been loved for so long by locals and tourists alike 😌

By the way, on Horiemon's Teriyaki app this shop's pig's feet soba is introduced as a recommendation, and it is decently tasty, but I thought the pig's feet soba at "Nanbu Soba" in Shiozaki, Itoman, was tastier than this one 🎵

Rating (9/15): It's decently tasty. It's a shop popular with locals and tourists, so why not stop by when you head toward Motobu Town? 😊
