Futami Soba

二見そば
Futami, NagoAs good as the top picksRating 12/15

A soba shop in a lone house standing along the mountains connecting the east and west of Nago City. Featured on the Nago Soba Map, it's particular about its chicken, pork, and bonito blended broth and homemade kōrēgūsu. The everything-on-top is loaded with toppings and superb. *There are reports it has been closed since around May 2024.

It was featured on the "Nago City Soba Map", and I tried it in September 2020 😀

*Please note that as of August 2024 there are reports that this shop is closed (since around May 2024?).

I ordered the "Futami soba everything-on-top" featured on the soba map.

Loaded with toppings — pork spare ribs (soki), braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), kombu, and yushi-dofu — it's the best 😁 The medium-thin noodles go well with the soup made from painstakingly prepared chicken, pork, and bonito broth, and it's delicious.

The kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori) is homemade too, and you can tell that everything is made carefully with a great deal of time and effort.

Rating (12/15): Futami Soba is particular about making things in-house. This is also recommended, so please try it.

PS: I don't have a photo, but the actress Meisa Kuroki is apparently from around here, and she seems to like this soba shop too — I think there was her autograph in the shop.
