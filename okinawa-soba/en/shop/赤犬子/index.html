Akainuko

赤犬子
Gusuku, NagoRating 9/15

A late-night Okinawa soba shop in the center of Nago, perfect for a finishing bowl after drinking. The menu is extensive, with plain soba, goat soba, offal soba and more, plus a soup-curry-style curry soba featured on the soba map.

― 2nd Visit (April 2023) ―

In April 2023 I tried again as a finishing meal after drinking ^^;

After last month, this time I took on the challenge while keeping my drunkenness in check 😄 The soup is a runny soup-curry-style broth and is ordinarily delicious 😋 The noodles are of ordinary thickness, the straight type, and the curry ingredients cling to them nicely.

Rating (9/15): This is a review from a drunk person's palate, but it's ordinarily tasty. How about it as a finishing meal after drinking in the center of Nago lol Be careful not to drink too much (｡>﹏<｡)
