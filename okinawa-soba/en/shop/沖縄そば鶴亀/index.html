I keep eating my way around every day to conquer all the Okinawa soba shops on the main island of Okinawa😄 I went to eat here in September 2024😄

This time I visited "Tsurukame" in Makiminato, Urasoe City.

The storefront has an old-fashioned look, and the characters "中縄 soba" on the white wall are striking😊 Inside, order tags hang on the wall, and you can tell they still cherish the old-fashioned way of doing business.

From the menu, I decided to order the classic "soki soba." When I took an order tag and tried to order, the manager said, "That's okay." In the end, the order tag wasn't needed, and I could order verbally (^0^;)

The soki soba is here😆　Tasting the soup first, it has a strong bonito broth, a perfectly tasty soup typical of Okinawa soba😋

The noodles are a medium-thick straight type with a softish texture. The pork spare ribs (soki) topping is seasoned on the sweet side, also a classic flavor. However, some parts had a bit of sinewy texture left.

Since I ordered the "large" this time, there's plenty of volume. On top of that, jushi came complimentary, lightly seasoned, and I enjoyed it as perfectly tasty too. I finished everything including the jushi and was stuffed😆

Rating (9/15): It's perfectly tasty. It's a shop where you can enjoy Okinawa soba at a leisurely pace while feeling the atmosphere of a long-established shop. When you come to Urasoe City, why not stop by once?😊
