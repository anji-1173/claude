Motobu no Soba-ya-san

もとぶのそば屋さん
Motobu Town (near Churaumi Aquarium)Rating 9/15

An Okinawa soba shop near Ocean Expo Park (Churaumi Aquarium). Fully equipped with self-checkout, in an almost contact-free style. The everything-on-top "MIX soba" comes with pig's feet (tebichi), bone-in pork ribs, braised pork belly, and fried tofu. The complimentary jushi is a nice touch too.

I went to eat here in March 2024😄

I came at lunchtime on a weekday, and it seemed quiet😁

When I entered, I was the first customer😄 There's also a self-checkout near the entrance, and I thought it was nice that it accepts cashless payment.

Up to this point I've had almost no contact with the staff😄 All the staff seem to stay holed up in the kitchen, and everything up to ordering and choosing a seat is completely self-service.

From the menu, I decided to order the "Okinawa soba (MIX soba)," listed at the very top and seemingly the top recommendation😆

The MIX soba arrived😁 It's an everything-on-top Okinawa soba with braised pork belly, bone-in pork ribs, pig's feet, knotted kelp, fried tofu, and fish cake lol

First, I taste the broth. The light pork bone broth base comes through.

Each topping is also light, with a gentle flavor😄

Next, the noodles. The noodles are medium-thick and straight. They feel soft😄

This time I ordered the MIX soba on its own, but a staff member asked, "Would you like some jushi too, if you'd like?" so I answered, "Thank you😄"

The jushi is on the light side too.

Rating (9/15): An Okinawa soba shop near Ocean Expo Park (Churaumi Aquarium). When you come to the northern region or the Motobu Peninsula for sightseeing, why not give it a try once? Just plain tasty.
