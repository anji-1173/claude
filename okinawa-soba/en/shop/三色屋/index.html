Sanshoku-ya

三色屋
Aichi, Ginowan CityAs good as the top picksRating 12/15

An Okinawa soba specialty shop with the motto “to become number one in Ginowan.” Its pride is soup you can choose from 3 kinds (chicken, pork, or blend), house-made fresh noodles, and melt-in-your-mouth seared soki served on a separate plate. Changing up the flavor with bonito oil is fun too.

I went to eat here in October 2024 😁😊　This time I'll introduce “Sanshoku-ya” in Aichi, Ginowan City. This shop is a participating shop in the 14th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally, and this is my 2nd visit.

The shop is on the 1st floor of a reddish-brown building, and the white noren (shop curtain) is the landmark 😄　There's a handwritten sign at the entrance, with a notice that the “extra-large seared soki” is sold out. It must be a popular menu item.

The interior has a retro atmosphere, with red sofa seats and counter seats. Brick-style tiles are used on the walls, giving it a calm atmosphere 🏮

Looking at the menu, standard Okinawa soba items like “braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) soba” and “seared soki soba” are all there. And what's distinctive is that you can choose the soup from 3 kinds (chicken broth, pork broth, blend). Last time I chose chicken broth, so this time I tried ordering the “seared soki soba” with pork broth.

The Okinawa soba that was brought out after a while has a striking clear soup. First, a sip of the soup. A smooth, pork-bone-based mouthfeel, and I can feel a deep taste a cut above other shops. The depth of the soup is wonderful 😋　However, I also feel the saltiness is a bit strong.

The noodles are medium-thick with a gentle wave, and apparently they use house-made fresh noodles. Along with a chewy texture, they cling to the soup excellently. You can taste the flavor unique to fresh noodles too, and they're delicious 🍜

The topping, the seared soki, is served on a separate plate, a trick to keep its flavor from transferring to the soup. It's simmered melt-in-your-mouth tender, searing adds toastiness, and it's very satisfying to eat. Partway through, when I added the shop's original bonito oil to the soup, I could enjoy a different, deep taste.

Rating (12/15): A wonderful Okinawa soba where every element is finished at a high level. The soup especially has a deep taste with concentrated pork bone umami, and it goes perfectly with the noodles. The balance of the seared soki's tenderness and toastiness is exquisite too. The idea of letting you enjoy a flavor change with the original bonito oil is wonderful. When you come to Ginowan City, how about dropping by? 😊

― 1st Visit (July 2024) ―

I went to eat here in July 2024 😁

This time I'll introduce “Sanshoku-ya” in Aichi, Ginowan City. On my journey touring Okinawa soba shops on the main island of Okinawa, I've visited quite a number of shops, but I'm still far from conquering them all. But I'll do my best, one shop at a time, aiming to conquer them all lol

Now, “Sanshoku-ya,” which I visited this time. Actually, it's a shop I'd been curious about for a while. Why? Because of the words on the sign that catches my eye every time I pass along National Route 330. I'd been drawn in by the catchphrase “Okinawa soba that will become number one in Ginowan,” which is just overflowing with confidence.

When you turn onto the road with the sign from National Route 330, the shop is right there. There was also a dedicated parking lot diagonally across.

When you go inside, there are counter seats and table seats; the interior is built like a former snack bar. (Sorry, I didn't take photos)

Looking at the menu, besides Okinawa soba, there are also a few rice dish items. But the purpose this time was, of course, “Okinawa soba that will become number one in Ginowan.” So I decided to order the signature Okinawa soba.

When ordering, I learned that you can choose the soup. You can choose from 3 kinds: chicken broth, pork broth, or blend. When I asked the staff, they said the most popular is chicken broth. So this time I tried choosing chicken broth.

While I was looking for a while at the menu, which even includes the profile of a manager who seems to have quite a character? (lol), the Okinawa soba I ordered was brought out.

First, the soup. When I take a sip... oh, this is unexpected! It's a slightly different taste from other Okinawa soba shops. Maybe because it's based on chicken broth, I sense a taste somewhat close to ramen soup. But it's unmistakably the taste of Okinawa soba. Rich and mellow. Preferences might be divided on this, but personally it was a taste I liked a lot.

Next, I have the noodles. A chewy texture is their characteristic. They have chewiness / bite too and cling well to the soup. I have the impression that many Okinawa soba noodles are on the soft side, but the noodles here have a moderate springiness and are very satisfying to eat. Delicious.

Pay attention to the toppings too. The seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) were especially memorable. A tender texture that falls apart. A deliciousness that melts in your mouth. Besides the regular seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), there was also salt-seared soki.

According to the profile of the owner, Mr. Ishikawa, on the menu, he also trained at the famous Okinawa soba shops “Chinchiki Soba” and “Ishigufu.” I see, now that you mention it, there's something similar in the taste, I thought, convinced.

According to Mr. Ishikawa, in July 2023 he temporarily closed Sanshoku-ya (at the time, Sanshokudon-ya), and reopened it renewed in 2024. At that time, he's also taking on the challenge of making noodles using house-made wheat. A story where you truly feel his passion for Okinawa soba.

Rating (12/15): Even while the owner says he's raising the bar, I felt he must also have a fair amount of confidence. No lie in the catchphrase “Okinawa soba that will become number one in Ginowan”! Preferences may vary by person, but I thought it might be the most delicious of the Okinawa soba shops in Ginowan City I've eaten at so far~. How about trying it once? It's recommended 😄
