Oshiro Soba

Okita, Nago CityAs good as the top picksRating 12/15

Okita, Nago City (moved in 2022). A shop with real skill that many people around me call "the most delicious". It competes with just large, medium and small, with a lettuce topping not found at other shops and a bonito broth, and you can choose flat noodles too. The first shop I visited on my Nago Soba Map tour.

It's a shop that people around me call "the most delicious", and it's one of my favorite soba shops too ☺　It's the first shop I visited after deciding to go on a soba tour with the "Nago City Soba Map" lol

It was originally in Gusuku, Nago City, but on November 24, 2022 it moved and reopened in Okita, Nago City, so I went for the first time in a while (December 2022).

They compete with just soba (large), (medium) and (small), and I ordered a large ☺

You can choose regular noodles or flat noodles, and I chose "flat noodles". With a lettuce topping not found at other shops, the bonito broth is as tasty as before the move ✌　The toppings are big-volume too, very satisfying. You can't go wrong.

Rating (12/15): Bonito-based broth is the standard at soba shops in Nago City, but I think Oshiro Soba is delicious, so please give it a try. Before the move it was street parking or a nearby coin parking lot, but now a certain amount of parking (9 cars) is secured, so I think it's easier to drop in than before.
