Ishikawa-san Chi

石川さん家
Biimata, NagoRating 9/15

An open-air diner loved by students, near Meio University and a nursing school. Tacos and taco rice are the main draw, but there's also "nankotsu soba," topped with hefty soft-bone pork ribs in a rich, sweetish seasoning. A community-rooted shop where the proprietress runs everything solo.

This time I'm introducing "Shokuji-dokoro Ishikawa-san Chi" in Biimata, Nago🏠 I ate here in March 2025😄

It's right in front of the nursing school in Nago, and Meio University is nearby too, so the shop is in an area bustling with students and young people.

As for the exterior, a signboard with an eye-catching yellow band is mounted on the ground floor of a mostly white building, with the words "Shokuji-dokoro - Tacos, Taco Rice, Soba - Ishikawa-san Chi" written big and bold inside an orange round logo.

It feels less like an Okinawa soba shop and more like a diner where tacos and taco rice are the main thing, and sure enough, looking at the menu, there's a rich variety of dishes besides Okinawa soba, like taco rice, taco rice sets, cheese curry rice, salted tongue set meals, and pig's feet (tebichi) set meals😄

Rather than an interior, it's more like tables and chairs lined up in a semi-outdoor open space with a roof over the parking area; a so-called open-air diner style. Menus with photos are posted all along the wall, and it's fun just to look at them. There's an unpretentious, very Okinawan local feel, and it has the atmosphere of a truly community-rooted diner🌺

When I visited at lunchtime, there were lots of young students, probably from the nearby schools. The proprietress running the place solo looked extremely busy handling the customers who kept coming one after another. It took a while from ordering until the food came out, so I waited😅

What I ordered this time was the "nankotsu soba (large)" for ¥900🍜

First, a sip of the broth. It's a combined pork bone and bonito broth, with the pork bone flavor coming clearly to the front. It doesn't have any especially standout character, but it's just plain tasty😋

Next, the noodles. They're medium-thick and straight, with an overall softish texture. Personally I'd be happier with a bit more firmness and springiness, but they blended well with the broth and were easy to eat.

And the highlight this time, the soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki)!🥩 Big chunky pieces were piled in generously, and visually the volume is quite something. The sweetish, rich flavor had soaked in thoroughly, and in that respect they were satisfying. However, some sinewy texture remained, and rather than being finished meltingly tender, they were finished with a chew. My impression was I'd appreciate it if they were simmered a bit longer🤔

Rating (9/15): It was a bowl that felt like "just plain tasty~." Given its location near the nursing school and Meio University, I could really see that it's loved as an everyday eatery for students. I was also curious about the other dishes like tacos and taco rice, so it might be fun to come back for those. Please consider it as one option when you're looking for an Okinawa soba shop in the Nago area😊
