Okinawa Pork Soba

沖縄ポークそば
Tsuboya, NahaAs good as the top picksClosedRating 12/15

A shop that was in the fiercely competitive Tsuboya area of Naha, near EIBUN. Its pork bone-based rich soki soba features exquisite charcoal-seared soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki), with high-level toppings and noodles. *There are reports that it closed around May 2024.

*Please note that as of May 2024 there are reports that it has closed. It's a shame, since it was one of the Okinawa soba shops I really thought was delicious (;｡;)

I ate here in October 2023. This shop is a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1 to 2024.2.29)," and I went as my 6th of 34 shops😊

It was lunchtime, and EIBUN, my provisional #1 recommendation, had its usual line of customers, but this place was empty. I felt that word about the shop hadn't spread yet.

A meal ticket machine is installed at the shop entrance. I'd decided to order the soba introduced in the stamp rally booklet, so I selected the "Rich Soki Soba."

This ticket machine supports smartphone payment, so I paid with "Rakuten Pay." I earn Rakuten points while paying, which effectively gives me cashback, so cashless payment really is great✌　I hope all Okinawa soba shops go cashless soon🙏

Apparently the thick fresh noodles take a little while to boil, so even though there were few customers, it seemed to take a bit of time.

They said they could give me a small bowl of jushi for free, so I asked for jushi (Okinawan seasoned rice) too😊

After waiting a while, the rich soki soba arrived lol　Imagining something very rich from the dish name and the color of the soup, I took my customary sip of the soup.

However, it wasn't the rich taste I expected, but a light pork bone-based soup (;^ω^)　I'd been imagining a rich soup like that of "796 (Nankuru)," my provisional #4 recommendation, whose agu broth soup I personally consider the provisional No.1, so I felt a bit let down (;^ω^)

But the toppings, spinach and a seasoned soft-boiled egg (runny yolk), are delicious😋　It feels like they were inspired by ramen toppings. Also, the soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) that came on a separate plate were seared over charcoal, and you could taste the charcoal in both aroma and flavor, which was the best. The toppings are of a higher level than at the other shops I've eaten at so far😊

The thick fresh noodles are nicely chewy and delicious too. I thought they were high-level, just like the toppings.

Rating (12/15): I thought the toppings and noodles were high-level. Along with EIBUN, the delicious Okinawa soba shops in the Tsuboya area of Naha City are really pushing each other to improve. The soup is lighter than it looks, but adding the green chili condiment changed the flavor and boosted the umami of the soup, making it even more delicious. Please do try this shop too.
