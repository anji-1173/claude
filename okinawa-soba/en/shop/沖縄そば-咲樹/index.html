Okinawa Soba Saki

沖縄そば 咲樹
Ganeko, Ginowan CityRating 9/15

A popular local shop in Ganeko, Ginowan City. Its signature is the hearty Saki Soba, with a rather pork-bone-forward blended broth, medium-thick wavy noodles, and toppings of braised pork belly, bone-in pork ribs, and pork. A solid flavor loved by construction workers too.

* This article was created with AI (Chat GPT 4o) and partly supplemented by me 😆

I ate here in November 2024 😄 This time I went to “Okinawa Soba Saki” in Ganeko, Ginowan City 🌺. There was some construction in front of the shop with scaffolding set up, but a large banner saying it was open was clearly hung, so I could walk in without hesitation! I like this kind of thing; it feels loved by the local people 😊.

Inside, signed shikishi boards were lined up, and I realized “this is a famous shop even locally” ✨.

Now, this time I ordered the “Saki Soba,” which is also the name of the shop! The menu had various kinds of Okinawa soba and I hesitated, but you do get curious about the item bearing the shop's name 😋.

The Saki Soba that came out, large size at ¥950. Topped with a big slab of braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), and tender-looking pork, it's full of volume! Pickled red ginger, green onions, and shredded egg add color, and it already looks delicious just by sight… 🤤.

When I took a sip of the broth, the blended stock of bonito and pork bone let me taste both. But the pork bone feels rather strong. No wonder many local construction workers visit. The flavor is solid, so it may be just right for those who want to eat heartily 👍.

The noodles are medium-thick wavy noodles, boiled on the soft side. Personally I'd like a bit more firmness, but the broth clings to them easily, so it's not bad. They go down with a slurp.

The braised pork belly topping is well-seasoned and finished on the sweet side. But it was a bit sinewy and firm, I think. It may be good for people who like a chewy bite, but I prefer it a bit softer 😉. The bone-in pork ribs seemed seared, with a slight smoky aroma, and were delicious! These too are sweetly seasoned, but simmered tender and easy to eat.

The shop was bustling with local customers, and that kind of atmosphere is nice. There's an air where you can chat a little and take your time, and you can feel it's loved not only by tourists but by locals too 😊.

Rating (9/15): Overall my impression is “just plain tasty!” The flavor is on the strong side, so eating it with rice or jushi would probably raise the satisfaction further ✨. Why not try this shop at least once too? 😆
