Uema Okinawa Tempura-ten

上間沖縄天ぷら店
Minami-Tōbaru, Okinawa CityRating 9/15

A shop with a long display of tempura, a “tempura shop where you can also eat soba.” The Uema way is to top the soupy ¥590 braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) soba with fish tempura and sausage tempura. The free tempura bits are a nice touch too, a shop rooted in the local community.

*This article was created with “Chat gpt 5.2 (Thinking)” and partially edited by me. I went to eat here in February 2025 😄

On my Okinawa soba tour, this time I went to “Uema Okinawa Tempura-ten” in Minami-Tōbaru, Okinawa City~ 😊　As the name says, the “tempura-first” vibe is already coming out from outside, and there are banners up too, so it's a perfect landmark. There were a lot of customers coming and going, and even before going in I felt, “Ah, this is the kind of place rooted in the local community” 🍤

When you enter the shop, the first thing you see is the tempura display. It's lined up in a long row, and honestly it gets me excited ✨　There's fish tempura, sausage tempura, and vegetable ones too; it feels like Okinawa's “tempura culture” turned straight into a shelf. The interior is bright and clean, and I also like how casual it is, where you can eat quickly and leave quickly. In fact, customers never stopped coming, and seeing what looked like regulars buying with practiced ease, I thought, shops like this are strong~ 😌

My order this time was the “braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) soba” topped with fish tempura and sausage tempura. Rather than an Okinawa soba shop, the feeling of “a tempura shop where you can also eat soba” might fit better. It was served fairly quickly, and the moment I received it, it was the kind where you go, “Oh, it's soupy.” The visual volume was pretty impressive, and the sense of satisfaction comes first, as soon as it's set on the table 😂

The soup is a combined broth with a light touch of pork bone. It has richness, but the overall impression is that the saltiness is a bit strong. Opinions may be divided, but on days when you've sweated or days when you want something strong, it seems like it would hit the spot for a lot of people 👍

The noodles are medium-thick flat noodles with a loose wave. The texture is on the soft side. Personally, my honest feeling is that I'd be happy if they had just one more level of springiness~ 😅　However, the soupy broth clings to them well. I think people who like soft noodles can get hooked without worry.

And the tempura toppings. The fish tempura has a fluffy batter, and when you dip it in the soup a little, the batter gets moist and becomes a different kind of delicious. The sausage tempura has a junky feel that needs no explanation, and when something like this goes in, the “Uema-ness” comes out all at once 😋　Being able to add tempura bits for free is a quietly nice touch too.

Rating (9/15): Honestly, my overall verdict lands on “Yeah, it's ordinary.” There's no sharp edge like “Super moving!” or “One of a kind!”, but precisely because of that, there's a stability that makes it usable every day. It makes sense that there are so many customers, and I think the “convenient strength” of being able to enjoy tempura along with it is this shop's appeal 😊　Next time I'd like to go after other tempura and have fun from the tempura side 🍤✨
