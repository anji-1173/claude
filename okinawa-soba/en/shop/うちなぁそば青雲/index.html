Uchinaa Soba Seiun

うちなぁそば青雲
Takushi, UrasoeRating 9/15

A community-rooted shop in Takushi, Urasoe. Its biggest distinguishing feature is soki soba piled so high with green onions that you can't see the noodles, with the onion flavor spreading through a light bonito soup. The menu is admirably minimal: braised pork belly soba, soki soba, and jushi only. The model airplanes inside the shop are fun too.

I ate here in April 2025 😄 This time I visited "Uchinaa Soba Seiun" in Takushi, Urasoe City 🍜

A yellow "soba" banner stands at the entrance. A tasteful wooden sign reads "Uchinaa Soba Seiun," giving the place a charming look 😊 The exterior has an open feel, letting you see inside through the glass.

There's also the word "soba" beside the entrance. You can really feel the community-rooted atmosphere 🌺

Inside is a compact space, mainly counter seats. You can feel that it's a shop that has long been loved by locals 😄 Through the big windows you can see the street outside, and it has a bright, open atmosphere.

Over the counter you can see the kitchen, and the owner working hard making soba. Photos and models of airplanes are displayed on the walls, making it a slightly fun space ✈️

The menu is very simple, with an admirably minimal lineup: "Braised pork belly soba ¥750," "Soki soba ¥850," and "Jushi ¥150" 😄 Not having to hesitate is actually welcome. This time I decided to order the "soki soba" 🍖

The tabletop is stocked with condiments such as ichimi chili pepper, yuzu kosho, and pipāchi (island pepper) 😋

After a short wait, the "soki soba" appeared ✨ Wow, this is a surprise 😳 It's so loaded with green onions that you can't see the noodles! I've never seen another shop with this many green onions on top 😆 The green of the chopped onions spreads across the whole bowl, vivid to look at 🌿

First, the soup. It's a light, bonito-based soup with a gentle flavor 😊 With the onion flavor added, it's a refreshing, easy-to-drink bowl.

Pushing aside the onions and pulling up the noodles, out came medium-thin straight noodles. The texture is on the soft side and they pair well with the light soup 🍜 Since it's loaded with onions, every time you slurp the noodles you enjoy the onion texture and flavor together, which is this shop's special charm.

The soki topping has a moderately soaked-in flavor and is ordinarily tasty 😄 However, some parts had a bit of sinewy texture.

Overall, being able to feel the texture and flavor of the onions at all times is distinctive, and it was an ordinarily tasty bowl 😊

Rating (9/15): A light bonito-based soup, soft medium-thin straight noodles, and moderately flavor-soaked soki made for an ordinarily tasty Okinawa soba 😄 Above all, the onion-loaded bowl where you can't see the noodles is a distinctiveness you won't find at other shops 🌿 The atmosphere of being loved by the community is nice too, so when you come to Takushi, Urasoe City, why not stop by once? 🍜
