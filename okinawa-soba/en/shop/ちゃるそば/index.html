Charu Soba

ちゃるそば
Asato, Naha (Sakaemachi Market)Rating 9/15

A locally rooted shop in an alley of Sakaemachi Market. Soft-bone pork rib soba for ¥750, with medium-thin straight noodles in a gentle bonito broth. A bowl to enjoy together with the retro atmosphere of the quiet market at midday.

I went to eat here in October 2024 😄 I visited "Charu Soba" in Sakaemachi Market in Asato, Naha! ✨ It had been a while since I'd been to Sakaemachi Market, and the daytime quiet left an impression. When you picture the nighttime bustle, the daytime atmosphere feels like a completely different world. Enjoying Okinawa soba in such a calm atmosphere is a luxury in itself 🍜.

As you walk down the narrow alleys inside the market, the "Charu Soba" noren (shop curtain) leaps into view. The exterior feels somehow nostalgic, and you get the sense that it's a shop rooted in the local community. The orange sign combined with the retro atmosphere of the market slowly builds your anticipation 🌟.

Inside, a warm space with a wood-based design opens up. The walls are decorated with menus and celebrity autographs, and you're enveloped in a homey, very Okinawan atmosphere. The tables and chairs have a design somehow reminiscent of a nostalgic school classroom, and when I sat down I felt I could take a breather 🍃.

What I ordered this time was the "Soft-Bone Pork Rib Soba" (¥750). It's one of the popular dishes among Okinawa soba, isn't it! The soba that was served was simple yet beautifully arranged, and it looked delicious.

When I took a sip of the broth, the gentle flavor of the bonito broth spread through my mouth.

The noodles are medium-thin, straight, and finished on the soft side. They're not especially firm, but they cling well to the broth, and you can enjoy how they come together in your mouth. You could say this softness is typical of Okinawa soba, but I thought I'd be even happier if there were a little more variety in texture 🤔.

The soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) are tender, but in places some sinewy texture remains. Still, overall they're well seasoned, and they went well with the broth and noodles. The chopped green onions and kelp served alongside were also just the right accent.

Okinawa soba eaten inside a market really is something special. Eating soba among the locals while enveloped in the quiet midday atmosphere gives an indescribably special feeling. This combination of a retro market scene and Okinawa soba is an experience I can recommend to both tourists and locals 🌿.

Rating (9/15): Overall my impression was "decently tasty," but I think enjoying it together with the market atmosphere makes this bowl even more appealing 🎵. Its charm isn't special luxury, but a homey flavor that somehow puts you at ease 🌺
