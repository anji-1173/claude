Hanamura Soba

花村そば
Seragaki, Onna VillageRating 9/15

A shop in Seragaki, Onna Village, along National Route 58. The signature dish is the ¥800 Hanamura Soba with a blended broth of pork bone, chicken, bonito and kelp. Next door is the attached pottery studio "Unna-yaki", where you can enjoy picking out souvenirs like yachimun pottery and shisa. A shop overflowing with Okinawan vibes, where customers on their way back from diving also stop by.

I ate here in June 2024 😄

This time I'm introducing "Hanamura Soba" in Seragaki, Onna Village. At this spot with a superb view surrounded by blue sky and sea, I had an encounter with Okinawa soba that soothed both body and soul. 🌴

The first thing that jumps out at you is the "Hanamura Soba" sign standing out against the blue sky. Combined with the tropical palm trees, for tourists, it seems the Okinawan mood will already be at its peak right here 🌺

Entering the shop, there's a calm atmosphere where you can feel the warmth of wood. Looking at the menu, I just couldn't help ordering the "Hanamura Soba", which shares its name with the shop and seems to be the signature dish. 🤤

And now, the long-awaited "Hanamura Soba" makes its appearance! 🎉

First, let's taste the broth. The stock, combining pork bone, chicken, bonito, kelp and more, has a gentle pork bone flavor coming through. Though it's a complex flavor, it's a gentle taste that somehow feels nostalgic. It wraps you in an indescribable sense of happiness. 😌

Next, let's taste the noodles. The medium-thick straight noodles are just moderately soft. It's a texture typical of Okinawa soba, and they go perfectly with the broth. The glossiness of the noodles lifted with chopsticks is enough to make you swoon. 😍

And pay attention to the toppings too. Both the braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and pork spare ribs (soki) are simmered until tender. The savory flavor of the meat fills your whole mouth. With fish cake (kamaboko) and chopped green onions added, the colors are vivid too. 🥩🥕

To be honest, you might say the taste is "very, very ordinary". But the great location in the tourist area of Onna Village multiplies the appeal of "Hanamura Soba" many times over.

Looking around the shop, it's bustling with tourists. Interestingly, there were also customers still wearing wetsuits, apparently on their way back from diving, enjoying Okinawa soba at the outdoor tables. The ocean side of the national highway is the sea, and because there's planting along the road, you can't see the sea directly from the shop, but the very Okinawan scene made me smile without thinking. 🏊‍♂️😄

Actually, "Hanamura Soba" has one more attraction. Next to the shop, there's an attached workshop dealing in traditional Okinawan crafts. 🏺

Even though it's a soba shop, very Okinawan items like shisa and ceramics are lined up crammed into every corner. After enjoying your Okinawa soba to the fullest, I also recommend taking your time to enjoy choosing souvenirs. 🛍️

Rating (9/15): So, how was the Okinawa soba at "Hanamura Soba"? It's a lovely shop where you can enjoy a superb location and Okinawan culture. 😊　Overall, the taste is ordinary, but the great location and the attached workshop are a big draw. 🌊🏺　When you visit on a trip, please do stop by! Nearby there's "Nakamura Soba", which I recommend and have introduced, but if that one is crowded, how about this one instead? I think you can leisurely soak up the Okinawan mood 😄
