Uchina Italian SOLA

うちなーイタリアンSOLA
Ganeko, Ginowan CityAs good as the top picksRating 12/15

An Italian restaurant near the Ganeko intersection on National Route 330. An oddball place that uses fresh Okinawa soba noodles even for its pasta. The specialty "Seared Meat Soba" is superb, with seared pork loin like thick-cut ham and smooth, chewy homemade noodles. *There is information that they stopped serving Okinawa soba around May 2024.

*As of May 2024, there is information that they no longer serve Okinawa soba on the lunch menu, so please be careful. It was honestly one of the Okinawa sobas I thought was delicious, so it's a shame (;｡;)

I ate here in October 2023. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29)", and I went as my 8th of 34 shops 😊

Wow lol　I'd often seen this shop while driving on the national highway, but I'd never seen an Okinawa soba banner standing like this at lunchtime, and from the shop name I'd never thought it was an Okinawa soba shop (;^ω^)　I'd thought it was just an ordinary Italian restaurant (;^ω^)

The business hours seem relatively short, 12:00 to 14:30, so maybe that's why I'd never seen the Okinawa soba banner flag lol

Entering the shop, it did indeed have an Italian feel, as in the photo above 😁　On top of that, I don't know if it's for the pasta or pizza, but there was a nice smell of cheese in the shop 🤗

The "Seared Meat Soba" was introduced in the stamp rally booklet, so I ordered it.

There seem to be 2 kinds of noodles, and you choose from "eggless noodles" or "egg noodles". When I asked the staff for their recommendation, they said "egg noodles", so I chose those. Also, the Seared Meat Soba was relatively reasonable at ¥850 even for a large, so I ordered a large 😁　Among the stamp rally shops I'd eaten at so far, my impression was that many were in the ¥1,000-or-so price range, so I thought ¥850 for a large at this shop was reasonable.

While I was playing around looking at the menu, the Seared Meat Soba came lol　The meat somehow has a look not found at other shops.

First, the broth. The stamp rally introduction said "pork and bonito stock", and it's a blended stock where the bonito really comes through. Tasty 😋

Next, I'll have the noodles 😋

As written on the menu, they're thick-ish straight noodles. The smooth, chewy homemade fresh noodles are delicious too 😋

Next, I'll have the meat. This topping is different from other shops; it seems to be seared pork loin, but it's like thick-cut ham, and this too feels fresh and is delicious 😋.

Rating (12/15): I thought the toppings, noodles and broth were all high-level. The smooth, chewy homemade fresh noodles and the seared pork loin like thick-cut ham are especially delicious. The inside of the shop has an Italian feel and a nice atmosphere. I'd really like you to try it once, I recommend it.
