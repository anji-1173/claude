Sanchome Soba

三丁目そば
Kumoji, NahaRating 10/15

A shop full of local customers that I happened upon on a back street off Kokusai-dori. Pork bone × bonito blended soup with jiggly soft-bone pork ribs, and amazingly jushi included, all for ¥650 — great value. A pretty high-level bowl.

I ate here in June 2023 😄

I had an errand at "Pallet Kumoji", at the end of Kokusai-dori, so I parked in a paid parking lot in a nearby building and walked toward Asato looking for a soba shop, and when I searched the streets behind Kokusai-dori, I found this shop by chance 😄

It's nice, kind of a once-in-a-lifetime encounter (^_^;)

Okinawa had entered the rainy season, but this time there was little rain, and that day was super hot too, and since I'd walked a bit from Pallet Kumoji, I went into the shop drenched in sweat. The shop was cooled pretty strongly, which was the best 😁

Looking at the menu, I thought that apart from the "Sanchome soba", things were relatively reasonable compared to other shops. The clientele also seems to be mostly locals.

I ordered the "nankotsu soki soba".

Looking at the menu, I thought it was just the soba, but amazingly it also came with jushi (Okinawan seasoned rice) — a happy surprise 😆

I felt like starting with the rice, but first, the ritual sip of the soup. The soup looks a bit cloudy, but it's a delicious soup that seems to be a blend with pork bone broth, with a bonito base coming through a little 😋

The noodles are thin and straight, on the soft side. The soft-bone pork ribs are jiggly and infused with flavor — can't go wrong. Delicious 😄 I put them on top of the jushi like a pork bowl and ate it that way, and it was the best lol

Rating (10/15): Simply tasty. I thought it was a pretty high level 😄 It's close to Kokusai-dori, so how about it when you come shopping or sightseeing? I think it's reasonable and delicious Okinawa soba.

*The shop has no parking lot, so you'll need to park at a coin parking lot in the area.
