Yonehachi Soba

A popular shop in Awase, Okinawa City. A famous shop that even displays Prime Minister Kishida's autograph, and it supports smartphone ordering via QR code. Unusual offerings like the green curry-flavored "Soy Milk Curry Soba" and the mysterious "Jokki Soba (mug soba)" are fun too.

In July 2024 I went to "Yonehachi Soba" in Awase, Okinawa City!

When I arrived in front of the shop, there was a sign firmly planted in the parking lot saying "Yonehachi Soba Parking Only," and looking closely at the text, it says "Unauthorized parking will be immediately reported to the police (110), and you will be cursed for generations to come"😆　Kind of scary😱 lol

The exterior is a simple building with an eye-catching red sign, and somehow it felt warm.

When I went inside, there was a homey atmosphere. Believe it or not, on the wall were the autographs of Prime Minister Kishida and the comedy duo Kamaitachi! I really felt it's a popular shop that all kinds of celebrities visit. What's more, when I went in, an actual RBC TV program was being filmed. My expectations just kept rising even before eating📈😄

What's more, when I sat down and looked at the menu, it supports QR codes. It's great that you can order with your smartphone📱😁.

However, my phone was overheating, so it couldn't read the QR code, and in the end I had to order verbally from the staff (^0^;)

The menu is out of focus, but I was somehow drawn to the "Soy Milk Curry Soba," so although it's an unusual item, I decided to order it.

The soy milk curry soba I ordered was brought out.

Time to dig in. Taking a sip of the soup, it's different from regular Okinawa soba soup: a green curry flavor, with the soy milk making it mellow and creamy, and delicious😋　The taste is different from its pure white appearance.

This exquisite balance is the best.

Next, the noodles. It pairs perfectly with the thin straight Okinawa soba noodles, the soup clinging well and bringing out the noodles. This is truly a new frontier for Okinawa soba! It feels new🌟

What's more, drizzling a little of the original kōrēgūsu vinegar on the meat topping really brings out the meat's umami. The balance of sourness and spiciness is just right, and I was hooked after one bite😆

Looking around, the shop was bustling with locals and tourists, showing how popular it is. I found myself completely wanting to try the regular Okinawa soba on my next visit too😋.

There are plenty of desserts too, with zenzai with brown sugar and zenzai with taimo (water taro) on the menu. This time I was full from the soy milk curry soba, so I'll save them for next time🍧.

Oh yes, the "Jokki Soba (mug soba)" that caught my eye on the menu. I think I'll save this for my next challenge. Just the name gets me excited; I'm already looking forward to what kind of taste awaits🎉.

And so, this visit to "Yonehachi Soba" was very satisfying. This shop, where you can enjoy a new side of Okinawa soba, is full of charm that makes you want to come back again and again. Everyone, please do experience this deliciousness. Next time I'll try the classic Okinawa soba, and I'm looking forward to new discoveries again.

Rating (12/15): This time I ordered an unusual menu item, but I thought it was high-level, so I'm introducing it as a recommended shop😄　Next time I'd like to try the regular Okinawa soba too. Everyone, why not give it a try when you come to Okinawa City?
