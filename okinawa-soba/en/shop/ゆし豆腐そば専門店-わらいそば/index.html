Yushi-dofu Soba Senmonten Warai Soba

ゆし豆腐そば専門店 わらいそば
Nezashibu, Tomigusuku CityRating 9/15

A two-faced shop: yushi-dofu soba specialist by day, izakaya (pub) by night. Fresh noodles from Terukina Noodle Factory, yushi-dofu from Unashi Tofu, and a ginger condiment that feels fresh. A memorable shop where I was able to use the ¥500 coupon earned by completing the stamp rally.

― 2nd Visit (July 2024) ―

I came to eat here in July 2024 😄 Actually, the other day I completed the Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally and got a ¥500 coupon. Since I had it, I thought I’d use it, so I chose this place this time 😆

Last time I had the yushi-dofu soba, but honestly I couldn’t really tell the taste of the broth (^_^;) So this time I went with a regular Okinawa soba.

Here it comes! It looks like an ordinary Okinawa soba. What will it taste like?!

First the broth. Yeah, last time the yushi-dofu was mixed in and I couldn’t tell the broth’s flavor well, but this one has a pork-bone-forward taste. It seems to be a blended broth, but the pork-bone flavor comes through clearly.

The noodles seem to be Terukina Noodle Factory’s wavy noodles. They’re just plain tasty.

The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) is tender! But there are also a few slightly sinewy parts, I think.

Honestly, the Okinawa soba at Warai Soba is just plain tasty. They’re not doing anything particularly unusual, but the flavor is stable. I thought it’s the kind of shop where local people come to eat on a daily basis.

I was glad I could use the stamp rally’s ¥500 coupon too 😁

Rating (9/15): It’s just plain tasty. When you’re stopping by near Nezashibu in Tomigusuku City, why not give it a try? 😄

Noodles: ★★★☆☆（3）
Soup: ★★★☆☆（3）
Toppings: ★★★☆☆（3）

― 1st Visit (January 2024) ―

I ate here in January 2024. This is my first Okinawa soba outing of the new year 😄 This shop is a participating shop in the “13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29),” and I went as the 31st of 34 shops 😊

This time I planned to eat before going surfing, my hobby (Itoman City: Suicide Cliff Point), so I waited in the parking lot from about 15 minutes before the 11:00 opening 😄 At exactly 11:00 I walked in as the very first customer 😆

The menu I would order was the “yushi-dofu soba with braised pork belly topping” 👍 introduced in the stamp rally booklet.

There was a QR code for cashless payment (PayPay) at what looked like a checkout counter next to the ticket machine, so I told the staff “I’d like to pay with PayPay,” and the answer was “Sorry, we can’t use PayPay” (^0^;)

In the end I ordered the “yushi-dofu soba with braised pork belly topping” from the ticket machine with cash (^0^;) I wish every Okinawa soba shop would go cashless soon 😁

After ordering I looked at the menu for a while, but even though I was the first customer of the day, I thought it was taking a little while.

Okinawa soba, the soul food of Okinawans, is something I want to eat quickly (^0^;)

The photo above shows the situation inside the shop, and I thought “this feels like an izakaya.”

Then, looking closely at the sign outside, it seems they are an Okinawa soba shop by day and an izakaya at night (^0^;) That explains the interior (^0^;)

I was killing time looking at the menu, thinking “it’s taking long,” and it finally came? (^0^;)

As usual I start with the broth 😄 The menu’s explanation said “a blend of stock from ‘bonito + pork bone,’ ‘chicken bones,’ ‘kelp,’ and ‘5 kinds of vegetables,’” but my impression when drinking it was that of yushi-dofu soup (^0^;) Honestly I couldn’t tell what it was based on. Sorry 🙏

But it has a gentle flavor and is just plain tasty.

Next, the noodles. These are Terukina Noodle Factory’s medium-thick wavy fresh noodles, I was told. They’re chewy as expected, but to me they didn’t seem to cling to the broth, and the broth and noodles felt separate.

There was a little ginger as a condiment, and mixing it into the broth gave a refreshing taste that felt new. Ginger with Okinawa soba broth may be surprisingly good 😄

The braised pork belly is melt-in-your-mouth tender and just plain tasty.

Rating (9/15): It’s just plain tasty. However, the broth tasted more like yushi-dofu soup than Okinawa soba broth, and it was hard to tell what the broth was based on. Also, the noodles are chewy and just plain tasty, but I couldn’t feel them mingling with the broth, and the broth and noodles felt separate. The ginger accent isn’t found at other shops, which I thought was nice, and the braised pork belly was melt-in-your-mouth tender and just plain tasty.
