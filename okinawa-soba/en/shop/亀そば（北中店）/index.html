Kame Soba (Kitanaka Branch)

亀そば（北中店）
Zukeran, Kitanakagusuku VillageRating 9/15

The Kame Soba Kitanaka branch along Route 330. A surprisingly light Agu broth considering how it looks, medium-thin noodles, and tender braised pork belly and rafute. There are also locations in Nishihara Town, Roadside Station Kadena, and Minatomachi in Naha City.

I ate here in March 2019 😊

I ordered the "Kame soba". From the color of the soup it looks like it would be strongly flavored, but the Agu broth is very light. Medium-thin noodles, and the braised pork belly and rafute are tender and delicious 😋

Rating (9/15): Simply tasty. It seems popular with locals, and at lunchtime it was full of customers. There are also Kame Soba shops in Nishihara Town, Roadside Station Kadena, and Minatomachi in Naha City, so how about trying it once?
