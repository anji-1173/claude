Okinawa Soba Etchan

沖縄そばエッチャン
Uebaru, NishiharaRating 10/15

An Okinawa soba shop up the stairs on the second floor, near the south gate of the University of the Ryukyus in Uebaru, Nishihara. A pork-bone-forward blended broth with soft, thin straight noodles. The "Mix Soba" (¥950) comes with meltingly tender tebichi and soft-bone pork ribs whose texture varies between soy sauce and salt versions. Bustling with locals and construction workers.

On this Okinawa soba journey, I visited "Okinawa Soba Etchan" in Uebaru, Nishihara Town 🍜 I ate here in July 2025 😄

Unfortunately it was raining that day. The photo ended up being taken through a window glass, but the landmarks are the "Etchan" sign and the stairs with red handrails ☔

Along the road there were banners for "Open" and "Seared Salted Soft-Bone Pork Rib Soba." Salted soft-bone pork ribs are intriguing 🤤

The entrance is at the top of the stairs.

You order at the ticket machine at the entrance. The Mix Soba is ¥950, and the set with jushi is ¥1,000. There's a wide variety of soba too: soft-bone pork rib soba, seared salted pork rib soba, braised pork belly soba, tebichi soba, yushi-dofu soba, and green onion soba. They're open from 11:00 to 16:00 and closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.

The shop is self-service. Pickled red ginger and kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori) are on the table.

The interior has counter seats and table seats, and when I visited it was bustling with local customers and construction workers. Shops with a lot of customers like this seem to rarely disappoint 😊

Outside the window it was rainy. From the window seat you can see the road and greenery, which gives a relaxed feeling.

What I ordered this time was the "Mix Soba." It's a loaded bowl topped with tebichi, braised pork belly, and soft-bone pork ribs ✨

First, the soup. A blended broth with a pork-bone-forward flavor 😋

The noodles are thin and straight, boiled on the soft side. The noodles and soup go well together, and it's fairly tasty.

The tebichi topping is meltingly tender. What's interesting is the soft-bone pork ribs: the soy-sauce-based ones have firmer cartilage, while the salted soft-bone pork ribs have meltingly tender cartilage, so they vary the textures. It's quite a fun touch 😄 However, I felt a sinewy texture in the braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs ^^;

Rating (10/15): A pork-bone-forward blended broth with soft, thin straight noodles. The noodles and soup pair fairly well. There was thoughtfulness in the toppings too, with meltingly tender tebichi and soft-bone pork ribs whose texture changes between soy sauce and salt. The sinewy texture of the braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs bothered me, but the level was higher than I expected, and it was a fairly satisfying bowl 👌
