Minoya Ameku Ryubo Rakuichi Branch

みの家 天久りうぼう楽市店
Ameku, NahaRating 9/15

A long-established Okinawan cuisine restaurant inside the "Ameku Ryubo Rakuichi" commercial complex in Naha Shintoshin. An overwhelming selection from soba to champuru, simmered dishes, soups, set meals, and side dishes. The "Soki Soba" (¥950) has a light pork-bone-based soup with soft, medium-thick straight noodles.

On this Okinawa soba journey, I visited "Minoya Ameku Ryubo Rakuichi Branch" in Ameku, Naha City 🍜 I ate here in June 2025 😄

It's a shop inside "Ameku Ryubo Rakuichi," a commercial complex in Naha Shintoshin. The sign lined with "Okinawa soba" and "goya champuru" is the landmark, and it's nice how casually you can drop by while shopping 😊

Opening the menu, the first thing that catches my eye is "40 years of commitment to flavor." It's apparently a long-established Okinawan cuisine restaurant ✨ The soba lineup is Okinawa soba ¥800, soki soba ¥950, braised pork belly soba ¥900, tebichi soba ¥900, offal (nakami) soba ¥900, and sea lettuce (āsa) soba ¥950 (all tax included).

And with every turn of the page, I'm overwhelmed by the sheer size of the menu. There are soba sets, takana fried rice sets, yakisoba sets, even island Napolitan, and Okinawan dishes are lined up: goya champuru, tofu champuru, fu champuru, liver and chives, and miso-simmered eggplant and loofah sets 😆

Soki simmered sets, tebichi simmered sets, Minoya Special, Minoya set meal, āsa soup set meal, nakami soup and tebichi soup sets, jushi, yushi-dofu, even inamuruchi. It feels like nearly all of Okinawan home cooking is covered, and I don't think you'd ever tire of it no matter how often you come 🤤

In addition, there are fried foods and rice bowls such as tonkatsu sets, katsudon, karaage sets, and chicken tempura sets.

Side dishes such as kūbu irichii, mimigā, and yushi-dofu, a demi-glace hamburger steak set, and drinks — there really is everything 😄

From 16:00 there's also a "great-value light drinking set," with either a draft beer set or a sour set at ¥1,200. You can reportedly pick one item from fried gyoza, karaage, fried tacos, and more, and I feel the breadth of an Okinawan casual diner rather than a soba shop 🍺

The takeout-only side dish menu is rich too.

The interior is an unpretentious diner with table seats lined up. It looks like you could relax even by yourself 😊

At the shop front there's also a showcase lined with bento boxes and side dishes, and looking at just this part it looks like a deli.

Now, what I ordered this time was the "Soki Soba." This is what was brought out ✨ An orthodox bowl, topped with soki, kelp, and green onions.

First, the soup. A gentle pork-bone-based flavor 😋

The noodles are medium-thick straight noodles, boiled on the soft side.

The soki is moderately seasoned, with the flavor soaked in just right. However, I did feel a fair amount of sinewy texture ^^;

Rating (9/15): A light pork-bone-based soup with soft, medium-thick straight noodles. The soki is moderately seasoned but has a sinewy texture. For better or worse, it was an ordinary Okinawa soba 😊 However, the sheer breadth of the menu lined with Okinawan dishes had the dignity of a long-established shop.
