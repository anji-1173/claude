Bar Shisa-tei

Barシーサー亭
Goya, Okinawa CityRating 9/15

Authentic Okinawa soba served by a bar in Goya, Okinawa City, in the heart of Koza. A light pork-bone broth soup, with a choice of wavy noodles or locally milled flour noodles. The signature "Shisa Soba" (¥950) is a loaded bowl topped with smooth yushi-dofu from the long-established Kawakami Tofu, braised pork belly, soft-bone pork ribs, and even a soft-fried egg.

On this Okinawa soba journey, I visited "Bar Shisa-tei" in Goya, Okinawa City 🍜 I ate here in June 2025 😄

When I was passing by I noticed an "Okinawa soba" banner standing out, and I couldn't help but drop in. The combination of a bar and Okinawa soba is so intriguing, isn't it? 😆

A wooden "Bar" sign hangs at the entrance, and it really does look completely like a bar. I doubt many people go in thinking it's an Okinawa soba shop (lol)

Beside it is a splendid yellow "Shisa-tei" sign adorned with a shisa lion-dog. Seeing it makes me feel it's an Okinawan shop and warms me up to it 😊

Inside, bottles of liquor line the entire wall, truly the interior of a bar. Honestly, I was a little on guard thinking, "What kind of soba will come out of here?" ^^;

But when they showed me the menu, it was surprisingly authentic. And you can choose the noodles from "wavy noodles" and "locally milled flour noodles (+¥50)" 🤤

The toppings are well stocked too: soft-bone pork ribs, braised pork belly, and yushi-dofu are each +¥200, and extra noodles are +¥150.

There's also the line "All the meat uses prefectural pork! Choose your prefectural noodles!" The soup is based on a light broth, and they say to let them know if you prefer rich. Such solid commitment, you wouldn't think it's a bar ✨

Shisa Soba ¥950, soft-bone pork rib soba ¥880, braised pork belly soba ¥880, yushi-dofu soba ¥800, vegetable soba ¥900. I hesitated, but naturally I decided to have the "Shisa Soba" named after the shop.

After placing my order and looking around the shop, there's even a billiards table so you can play while drinking at the bar 😄

And here's what was brought out. Loaded with yushi-dofu, braised pork belly, soft-bone pork ribs, and even a soft-fried egg. It's a more authentic bowl than you'd expect from Okinawa soba made by a bar ✨

First, the soup. A light pork-bone broth flavor, a gentle bowl that goes down easily 😋

What stood out among the toppings was the yushi-dofu. It's apparently from the long-established "Kawakami Tofu" in Okinawa City, and its smooth texture is irresistible 😊 The braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs were both tender, and their seasoning is light as well. However, I did feel a bit of sinewy texture ^^;

And something you rarely see at other shops is the soft-fried egg. This is a welcome touch.

The noodles are medium-thick wavy noodles, boiled on the soft side. They went well with the light soup 🍜

Rating (9/15): A light pork-bone broth soup with soft medium-thick wavy noodles. It's a loaded bowl topped with smooth yushi-dofu from Kawakami Tofu, tender braised pork belly and soft-bone pork ribs, and even a soft-fried egg. It was an ordinarily delicious bowl, hard to believe it came from a bar 😊
