Shimanchu Soba

島人そば
Yogi, Okinawa CityRating 9/15

A sister shop of the tacos-famous “Gate One.” Its signature is the Shimanchu Soba, with a broth of Agu pork stock scented with bonito, and it was also selected as one of the “Tabearuki Kyushu-Okinawa 100 Taste Shops.” Its longer hours, until 20:30, are convenient too.

I went to eat here in August 2024 😆

Incidentally, I'm told this is a sister shop of the shop called “Gate One,” famous for taco rice and tacos. As usual I went in with no research, so I learned this for the first time (^0^;) The tacos looked famous and delicious too, but this time, since I aim to conquer every Okinawa soba shop, I ordered the “Shimanchu Soba” 😆

Furthermore, the menu says it was selected as one of the “Tabearuki Kyushu-Okinawa 100 Taste Shops.” I don't really know what that is, but maybe I can expect something 😄

When you enter the shop, a calm, Okinawan atmosphere spreads out first. Autograph boards of celebrities are lined up along the wall, giving the impression of a shop loved by locals and tourists alike. It's also nice that you can get water and tea self-service, so casual. Wrapped in a relaxed atmosphere, I looked at the menu right away, and what caught my eye was, as expected, the “Shimanchu Soba.” A soba with the same name as the shop — I have to order it!

After ordering and waiting a while, the “Shimanchu Soba” finally arrived. In a big bowl, the generous broth and medium-thick noodles whet the appetite. The broth, said to use “Agu stock,” gives a distinct pork flavor on the first sip, but at the same time the aroma of bonito comes through too.

The noodles are medium-thin, with a characteristically soft texture. I felt they were slightly lacking in firmness and thought a bit more bite would be good~ But they cling easily to the broth and were perfect for the light, easy-to-drink broth.

The toppings include braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). The braised pork belly is lightly seasoned. The soft-bone pork ribs are also lightly seasoned, a bit firm, with some sinewy texture remaining. This may divide opinions, but for those who want to enjoy a solid texture, it might be a plus. I prefer them a bit softer, but those who like this meat's chew might like it.

Rating (9/15): Overall, the “Shimanchu Soba” didn't have strong surprise or individuality, and my impression was of a so-called “just plain tasty” soba. The shop's hours are 11:00 to 20:30, which I think is longer than the hours of other Okinawa soba shops. If you get a chance to come to Yogi in Okinawa City, please do stop by “Shimanchu Soba.” Next time I'd like to try the tacos too 😆
