Miyako Soba Nakayoshi

宮古そば なかよし
Shimashi, GinowanRating 9/15

An old-fashioned diner run single-handedly by a grandma. You can enjoy traditional-style Miyako soba, with a distinctive, slightly sweet soy-sauce-based broth and the toppings hidden under the noodles. A wide-ranging menu that also includes goat soba and curry.

This time I went to "Miyako Soba Nakayoshi" in Shimashi, Ginowan! I ate here in November 2024😄

"Nakayoshi" is on the ground floor of an apartment building, and its bright exterior in orange and yellow catches the eye. The noren (shop curtain) at the entrance reads "Miyako Soba Nakayoshi," and an "Open" sign was hanging too. Beer crates were stacked in front of the shop, giving it a somehow nostalgic look😌

Step inside and you'll find an old-fashioned diner-style interior. All kinds of menu placards are posted on the walls, and I really got the feeling that this is a shop long loved by the locals🎫 It seems a grandma runs the place by herself, so you may need to be prepared to wait a little when it's busy.

Looking at the menu posted on the wall, there's plenty of variety, from standard dishes like "goat soba," "soki soba," and "vegetable soba" to "katsu curry" and "curry rice." Prices are reasonable too, in the ¥600 to ¥900 range👍 This time I decided to order the "Miyako soba" that's in the shop's name!

After a short wait, the "Miyako soba" arrived. At first glance it looks simple, like just noodles topped with green onion, but that's the hallmark of Miyako soba😄 The toppings are hidden at the bottom; digging around, I found braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and fish cake (kamaboko).

First, a sip of the broth. The color is a little dark, and it looks like the broth of Tojin soba. It gives a different impression from the bonito broth or pork bone broth of typical Okinawa soba. If anything, it's soy-sauce-based with a bit of sweetness, with a distinctive flavor a bit like chicken broth. This is Miyako soba with a unique broth🤔

Next, the noodles. They're the thin, straight noodles characteristic of Miyako soba, with a softish texture😋 The braised pork belly hidden at the bottom had a firm chew, with a somewhat strong sinewy texture. The fish cake, though simple, makes a nice accent too.

I think this is a shop where you can enjoy Miyako soba in its true form. I was able to savor a flavor different from the Okinawa soba at other shops. Since the grandma runs it alone, things look a little hard when it's crowded, but I think it's a good shop where you can feel the local taste, all of that included. If you want to experience Miyako's food culture, please stop by😊

Rating (9/15): Just plain tasty. Everyone, please do pay a visit to "Miyako Soba Nakayoshi"! Next time I'll introduce yet another Okinawa soba shop, so stay tuned~🍜✨
