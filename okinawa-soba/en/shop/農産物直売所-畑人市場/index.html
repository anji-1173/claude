Nosanbutsu Chokubaijo Hatabito Ichiba

農産物直売所 畑人市場
Zanami, ItomanRating 9/15

A bowl you can enjoy inside a farm produce market in Zanami, Itoman. The only menu item is Okinawa soba, at a one-coin ¥500. A pork-bone broth with a salty touch and soft, thin straight noodles. It has a warm atmosphere like a gathering place for the local grandmas.

On this Okinawa soba journey, I visited the "Farm Produce Market Hatabito Ichiba" in Zanami, Itoman City 🍜

I ate here in May 2025 😄

A vivid yellow building is the landmark, and inside the shop, local fresh vegetables and specialty products are lined up. It has a wonderfully warm atmosphere, like a gathering place for the local grandmas 😊

Seeing people chatting (yuntaku) while shopping, I feel this is a place loved by the community.

Now, the only Okinawa soba on the menu you can eat here is "Okinawa soba"! No need to hesitate, an admirably minimal competition 😄

The price is, amazingly, ¥500. Okinawa soba prices have been rising recently, so being able to eat it for one coin is nice.

First, the soup. It's a pork-bone broth soup, the type where the saltiness comes through firmly.

The noodles are the thin straight type, finished on the soft side. It has a gentle, old-fashioned texture.

The toppings are a simple composition of braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), fish cake, and pickled red ginger. The braised pork belly has a moderately soaked-in flavor, which is nice, but the sinewy texture was really strong ^^;

The portion is average, so it may be unsatisfying for people who want to eat heartily. It has just the right volume for a light snack while shopping. Considering the ¥500 price, I think the value is good 😄

Rating (9/15): The bowl was very, very ordinary in taste, but the good thing about a produce-market eatery like this is that you can taste the local atmosphere along with it. When driving toward Itoman, why not try a one-coin bowl while shopping for fresh vegetables? 👋
