Tsukikuruchi

月黒木（つきくるち）
Enobi, UrumaRating 9/15

An Okinawa soba specialty shop in Enobi, Uruma City, opened in 2022. The cloudy, rich pork bone soup, slowly simmered from Okinawa-raised pork bones, is smooth with no gamey smell. Melt-in-your-mouth soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) and thinnish straight noodles. A hideaway-like shop with a low-key sign, so be careful not to drive past it.

I went to eat here in November 2023😊　Since it was during the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1 to 2024.2.29)," I wanted to eat at a participating shop, but I had already eaten at and cleared the participating shops around Uruma City, where I had errands, so even though it's not part of the stamp rally, I decided to eat at this shop since I'd been curious about it for a while.

Tsukikuruchi is about one door down from "Shima Soba," where I ate before, but the road in front of the shop isn't very congested, so cars tend to go fairly fast and it's easy to drive past it (;^ω^)　It's not so much that there's no sign as that it doesn't stand out; Shima Soba is easier to spot.

Inside, a meal ticket machine was installed near the entrance. This shop is cash only too lol　I wish all Okinawa soba shops would hurry up and go cashless; I think it would help with efficiency (lol)

This time I ordered the "Soki Soba (Medium? Regular?)".

According to what I'd googled beforehand, the soup is a "rich pork bone broth." The color is a bit cloudy too, and it certainly looks pork bone-based.

First, as is my custom, a sip of the soup. It's definitely a pork bone broth. What's more, there's no gamey smell, and it's finished quite smooth. I thought it was tastier than the soup at "Kame Soba" with its agu pork broth, which I ate before, but I felt it didn't have the rich creaminess of "796 (Nankuru)," which I've introduced as my provisional #4 recommendation.

Next, the noodles. They're a thinnish straight type. On the soft side. The topping is soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). They're very tender, practically melting, and perfectly tasty, but the flavor is on the light side and wasn't quite enough for me.

In the latter half I added kōrēgūsu (chili-infused awamori) to the soup to change up the flavor. I enjoyed it that way too, but the richness of the pork bone disappears and it becomes spicy, so if you want to savor the smoothness, it might be better not to add it.

Rating (9/15): Perfectly tasty. The pork bone soup is reasonably smooth and delicious. It opened in 2022, and the inside is clean too. Why not give this shop a try once?
