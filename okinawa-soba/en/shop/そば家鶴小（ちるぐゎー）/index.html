Soba-ya Chiruguwa

そば家鶴小（ちるぐゎー）
Chatan, Chatan TownOld Okinawan houseRating 9/15

The Chatan branch of Chiruguwa, an old-folk-house-style shop with a beautiful red-tiled roof. Its unusual beef soba is a bowl where the savory flavor of stir-fried beef and vegetables blends into the pork bone broth. Even the tissue holder doubles as a menu, which is a charming touch. It's also close to American Village.

I ate here in December 2024 😄 This time I was exploring roads I don't usually take, hoping to discover a new Okinawa soba shop, and I found this place in Chatan, Chatan Town! It's "Soba-ya Chiruguwa Chatan Branch," with a beautiful exterior topped by a classic Okinawan red-tiled roof ✨

When you step inside, the interior is styled like an old Okinawan folk house, with a very calm atmosphere. On the tables are menus with Okinawan-style patterns, and just looking at them gets me excited 🎵

What I ordered this time was the "Beef Soba"! A beef dish is rare at an Okinawa soba shop, and it reminded me of the beef soba at Maeda Shokudo. My expectations rose as I wondered what it would taste like 🤤

First, I start with the broth. The pork-bone-based broth is mixed with the juices from the stir-fried beef and vegetables. It's a broth with a flavor a little different from regular Okinawa soba 😋

Next, the noodles. They're thin, straight noodles with a very soft texture 👍

And now the star of the show, the stir-fried beef and vegetables! The generous heap of beef and vegetables looks very voluminous and filling. The beef is simmered until tender, and the vegetables keep their crunch while soaking up plenty of flavor 🥬

When you eat the beef, vegetables, and noodles together, the savory flavors of each ingredient harmonize in your mouth, making it a very satisfying bowl. It's not as big as the beef soba at Maeda Shokudo, but it's still enough to fill you up 😊

Rating (9/15): It's decently tasty. This shop is located in Chatan Town, close to American Village, so it's a convenient place to drop by between sightseeing stops. Being able to enjoy beef soba while soaking up the atmosphere of an Okinawan old folk house is a real draw 🏠 If you want to enjoy Okinawa soba in the Chatan area, I'd definitely like you to give this shop a try 😄
