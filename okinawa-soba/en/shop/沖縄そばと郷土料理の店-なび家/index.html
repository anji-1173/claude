Okinawa Soba and Local Cuisine　Nabiya

沖縄そばと郷土料理の店　なび家
Gusukuma, Urasoe (Urasoe SC)Rating 9/15

A local cuisine chain inside Urasoe Shopping Center. Its signature is the Yokubari Soba with three toppings: bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), pig's feet (tebichi) and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku). It also has a good selection of Okinawan snacks to take out, such as sata andagi and popo.

I went to eat here in March 2025😁😊　This time I went to "Nabiya Urasoe Shopping Center branch" in Gusukuma, Urasoe City😄

Speaking of "Nabiya," it's a chain that features Okinawa soba and local cuisine, and besides the Urasoe Shopping Center branch, it has several locations, including the Barclays Court branch and the AEON Haebaru branch.

True to its concept of "freshly made local flavors," Okinawan home-style treats such as sata andagi, jushi, chinbin and popo are also sold for takeout, lined up all around the entrance🌺　Even though it's inside a shopping center, it's packed with so much Okinawan character that my mood lifted as soon as I approached the shop.

When I went in, it being lunchtime on a weekday, the place was bustling with local customers. You can gradually feel that it's a shop rooted in the community. The menu has not only Okinawa soba but a wide range of Okinawan home cooking, such as the bitter melon (goya) champuru set meal, the fu champuru set meal, simmered pig's feet (tebichi) and the tuna and marlin set meal, so it seems you can't go wrong with whatever you order😁

What I ordered this time was the "Yokubari Soba (¥950)," marked "Recommended" on the menu. It's a truly greedy bowl that lets you enjoy three toppings at once: bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), pig's feet (tebichi) and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku)🍜

Looking at the bowl as soon as it arrived, it's heaped with leafy greens and green onions, so it looks lively. First, as is my custom, a sip of the soup. It seems to be a blended broth of pork bone and bonito, but none of the broths really asserts itself. There are no sharp edges; it's an "ordinary taste."

The noodles are medium-thick and slightly wavy, with a pretty soft texture. Personally I'd be happier if they had a bit more chewiness.

As for toppings, having the three kinds, bone-in pork ribs (hon-soki), pig's feet (tebichi) and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), is the real appeal of the "Yokubari Soba"✨　However, to be honest, all three still had a somewhat sinewy texture, and I got the impression they fell a little short in terms of tenderness and how well the flavor had soaked in😅　There's plenty of knotted kombu and leafy greens too, so the volume of toppings itself is ample.

What I feel throughout the shop is that it's established itself as "a place for everyday local meals." It's a shop that plays the role of somewhere people in the area can quickly drop in for lunch. Seeing the wide range of local cuisine on the menu and the abundance of takeout sweets, that feeling comes across well🌺

Rating (9/15): As you'd expect from a chain, "Nabiya" is consistent, but it doesn't really have an outstanding personality, so I thought it was an ordinary bowl. That said, it's an easy-to-eat, easy-to-visit shop, and it's no wonder it's bustling with locals😊　When you come to the Urasoe area and suddenly feel like eating Okinawa soba, why not stop by?
