Okinawa Soba Niibunja~

沖縄そば にいぶんじゃ～
Noborikawa, Okinawa CityRating 9/15

A popular shop in Noborikawa with a cute pink noren. You can choose between fresh noodles kneaded with bonito powder and wavy noodles, and the blended broth of bonito, pork, and chicken is unique. Changing the flavor with the special garlic condiment is fun too.

I ate here in October 2024 😄 This time I’ll introduce my experience at “Okinawa Soba Niibunja~,” which is also listed as a stamp rally participating shop.

From the moment I arrived at the shop, I felt a comforting atmosphere. The exterior's pink noren is striking, and its cute design catches the eye ✨.

A warm appearance that conveys how loved it is by the locals ❤️. Inside, the wooden interior feels warm and clean, and a homey atmosphere spreads further.

It was a weekday lunchtime, but the shop was almost full of what seemed to be local regulars! It makes me think this is a really popular shop, and my expectations grew ☺️.

What I ordered this time was the “Mixed Soba” introduced in the stamp rally booklet. At this shop you can choose the noodles from two types, “straight fresh noodles” and “wavy noodles,” and I chose the “straight fresh noodles.” Looking at the menu, it said bonito powder is kneaded into the noodles, which is a somewhat unusual feature ⚡️. When the soba was brought out, the noodles had a faint brownish tint, indeed a different look from ordinary Okinawa soba.

As for the broth, it blends three kinds of stock: bonito, pork, and chicken. No single stock stands out, and it has a mysterious flavor. It’s a unique broth you can’t easily find at other shops, and I felt this is the individuality of “Niibunja~.”

Next, when I took a bite of the noodles, the chewiness and decent firmness were irresistible ❗️. However, although they said bonito powder was kneaded in, honestly I didn't feel much of its flavor. But the quality of the noodles themselves is quite high, and it's a satisfying bowl.

And the toppings are generously piled with braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) ✨. First, the braised pork belly is seasoned very lightly. The soft-bone pork ribs are simmered until tender, and the umami spreads gently with each bite. There's no heaviness at all, so you can eat it refreshingly to the end ♥️.

In addition, a “special garlic” condiment was on the table, and thinking “like ramen,” I added a little. Garlic in Okinawa soba? I wondered, but when I tried it, it was surprisingly good‼️ It offered another way to enjoy it. This condiment seems to be a touch unique to “Niibunja~.”

All in all, the Okinawa soba at “Niibunja~” cherishes the local flavor while adding its own touches, and what impressed me especially was the chewiness of the noodles and the condiment arrangement ✨. I can well understand why it's loved by the locals. It's a shop I can confidently recommend even to those visiting as tourists.

Rating (9/15): It's just plain tasty. The meal at “Niibunja~” made me appreciate again the depth of Okinawa soba. Next time I'd like to try the “wavy noodles”! I'd also love to try the other menu items ✨✨. Why not give this shop a try too? 😄❤️!
