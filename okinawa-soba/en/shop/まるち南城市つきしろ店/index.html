Maruchi Nanjo Tsukishiro Branch

まるち南城市つきしろ店
Tsukishiro, NanjoRating 9/15

Maruchi's Tsukishiro branch, where you can choose from 3 broths (light/rich/salt) and 4 noodles. You can even choose superfood moringa noodles, and its signature is the Maruchi Soba with everything on top: braised pork belly, pig's feet, and soft-bone pork ribs. Complimentary jushi too.

I ate here in July 2024 😁 This time I visited "Maruchi," an Okinawa soba specialty shop in Tsukishiro, Nanjo. I had the "Maruchi Soba," which shares the shop's name, so I'll give you my thoughts in full (?)!

First, Maruchi's exterior. The red sign is the landmark. Inside the bright, clean shop, locals were enjoying their meals in a friendly, convivial atmosphere.

What I ordered from the menu this time was the "Maruchi Soba," which shares the shop's name. For the broth, on the staff's recommendation I chose "light," the most popular.

After a while, the "Maruchi Soba" arrived 😄

First, a sip of the broth. It's a blended stock of chicken, pork, and bonito, with the pork bone flavor standing out.

Next, the noodles. I tried choosing the moringa noodles. They're noodles made with a superfood vegetable called moringa, and apparently they're rich in nutrients important for human activity, such as vitamins, minerals, and amino acids. It feels like it makes things a little healthier (^0^;)

But to be honest, eating them, I couldn't really taste much difference from ordinary thin straight noodles. The texture wasn't anything special either; they were just ordinary soft noodles. Since I'd had high hopes, it was maybe a bit disappointing.

The toppings are a so-called "everything on top" combo: braised pork belly (sanmai-niku), pig's feet (tebichi), and soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). They're all tender, but the braised pork belly still had a bit of sinew, and the texture bothered me in places.

Actually, I'd eaten "Maruchi Soba" before at the old-folk-house-style Nakagusuku branch, and I only realized afterward that it's the same chain as the "Maruchi" I visited this time.

Rating (9/15): It feels average. The Nakagusuku branch I visited before had lots of tourists, but this one seemed to have more local customers. The sinewy texture of the braised pork belly and the somewhat weak impact of the moringa noodles' flavor led me to this rating. It's near the Costco scheduled to open around August 24, 2024, so why not try a meal here at least once on your way back from Costco 😄
