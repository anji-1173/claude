Nari Soba

なりそば
Futenma, GinowanRating 9/15

A self-service Okinawa soba shop operating unstaffed 24 hours a day. An automatic door that closes using plastic-bottle weights, a broth dispenser, and complimentary all-you-can-eat jushi or curry; its one-of-a-kind system feels fresh. A shop that runs on trust.

I ate here in October 2023. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29)," and I went as my 11th shop out of 34😊

The stamp rally booklet described it as "operating unstaffed 24h," so the system had me thinking "?", but once I went inside, I figured out a lot😊

It's quite novel lol What first made me laugh was the shop door (lol) After you open the sliding door, it closes automatically, and believe it or not, the power comes from the gravity of weights in plastic bottles lol The person who came up with it is amazing (lol)

First, a gulp of the Okinawa soba broth. Is this broth strongly pork-bone-based? As expected, it's like the Okinawa soba side dishes sold at bento shops.

The noodles are a loosely wavy type, and the braised pork belly is soft too; just plain tasty. As in the earlier photo it's stored refrigerated, but with the hot broth it feels freshly made lol

Rating (9/15): Just plain tasty. I imagine there are security cameras, but operating unstaffed 24h is a system that works because of trust with customers lol It's my first Okinawa soba shop with a system like this, so why not give it a try once?
