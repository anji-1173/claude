I ate here in February 2025😄　I went on my Okinawa soba tour in Oyama, Ginowan City🍜　This time I visited "Okinawa Soba Shokudo Yonehama."

From the outside it has a real "diner" feel, and it's located next to a pachinko parlor, which reminds me a little of "Garyu Soba" in Nago~😊

The yellow sign at the entrance stands out, so it's easy to find even on your first visit, which is great. When you enter, it's ticket machine style first, and there are so many buttons that you go "Whoa, that's a lot of menu items!"😂　There are all kinds of soba alone, plus yakisoba, curry dishes, and even rice bowls and set meal-type dishes, so it's very extensive.

From the many menu items, I pressed the button for the "Salt Nankotsu Soki Soba," which was billed as the No.1 most popular.

What's interesting here is that you can choose your soup from "bonito broth" or "pork bone broth." As usual, I chose the bonito broth.

First, a sip of the soup. The moment I drank it, imagining my usual bonito broth, I went, "Oh, this isn't the 100% bonito I expected." There's definitely bonito flavor, but the "blended broth" aspect was stronger than I expected, and I thought it was a soup where you can taste pork bone broth too.

The noodles are thin and straight, with a pretty soft texture.

And then the star, the soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki). Big chunky ones on top got me excited, but the seasoning is on the light side.

The atmosphere inside is that of an unpretentious diner, so it's easy to walk into. Customers come and go at a decent pace, and when I happened to look around, everyone was ordering the pork bone broth...! That bothered me a little😂　I honestly found myself thinking, maybe the real star is the pork bone broth? Even choosing bonito I could sense pork bone, so if you choose pork bone, maybe what they're "pushing" is easier to understand. Next time I go, I'll want to go with pork bone and try a different menu item.

Rating (9/15): My honest opinion is that this time's bonito broth salt nankotsu soki soba was less "totally hits the spot!" and more "a perfectly tasty Okinawa soba"😊　Some people might be wary when they hear it's attached to a pachinko parlor, but it's easy to pop into~. Next time I want to try the pork bone broth everyone was ordering🍜✨
