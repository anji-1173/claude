Ichiran

壱蘭
Nishizaki, ItomanRating 9/15

A shop with a black noren (shop curtain) like a ramen shop. A light bonito-based soup with homemade fresh medium-thick wavy noodles. Its distinctive feature is changing up the flavor with "Shimanero," an original habanero-based seasoning. A rare Okinawa soba shop that's also open in the evening.

*This article was created with AI (Claude 3.5 Sonnet) and partly added to by me.

I ate here in September 2024 😄 This time I'm introducing "Ichiran" in Nishizaki, Itoman!

It's not a shop name that makes you think of "Uchinā soba," is it. It gives off a completely ramen-shop vibe 😄 Perhaps to match the shop name, the black noren (shop curtain) with gold lettering is striking.

Inside there are counter seats and table seats, with a cozy atmosphere. On the tables was a seasoning called "Shimanero" that I've never seen at other Okinawa soba shops, which made me curious 😲

Looking at the menu, there are spicy items, but this time I decided to stick to the basics and order the "Nankotsu Soba."

After waiting a few minutes, the "Nankotsu Soba" came served in a black bowl with beautiful kintsugi, and it looks great too! 😋

First, the soup. It's a light soup based on bonito broth, with a gentle flavor.

Next, the noodles. They're said to be homemade fresh noodles—medium-thick and wavy. They're chewy, but honestly my impression was that they're just average for homemade fresh noodles 🤔

As for the soft-bone pork ribs (nankotsu soki) topping, the cartilage parts are stewed until meltingly tender, but parts of the meat still had some sinewiness left.

I tried changing up the flavor with the "Shimanero" I was curious about. It's said to be a seasoning based on habanero; about three drops add a tingling heat and let you enjoy a different flavor 😆

The tourist sitting next to me kept saying "So good! So good!", but I couldn't help thinking there are many more delicious Okinawa soba shops out there 😅

Rating (9/15): It's solidly tasty Okinawa soba, but its distinctive feature is that you can enjoy changing up the flavor with the original seasoning "Shimanero." If you love super-spicy food, why not take on the super-spicy menu? 😊 This shop is also open in the evening, which is rare for an Okinawa soba shop.
