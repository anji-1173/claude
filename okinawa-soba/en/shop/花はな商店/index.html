Hanahana Shoten

花はな商店
Makishi, Naha CityRating 9/15

A shop particular even about its water, with special fresh noodles kneaded with fish powder and aged 48 hours for a firm texture. The signature Mixed Soba, with low-temperature-cooked whitish meat and 2 quail eggs, has a character found at no other shop.

I ate here in November 2023. This shop is a participating shop in the “13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29),” and I went as the 16th of 34 shops 😊

Inside the shop, a note explaining their commitment to the soba was posted. Also, when I googled it, there was an explanation saying “Specially made fresh noodles with an original blend of wheat flour and fish powder kneaded in and aged for 48 hours. Firm and smooth going down,” which sounds delicious 😁

There was also an explanation about the water: “With a water purifier using 2 types of antibacterial activated carbon, free residual chlorine is removed while leaving the mineral content in the raw water. Furthermore, with a water softener with high filtration capacity, advanced chlorine is removed. Safe and secure ‘delicious water’ is used and served for the broth, boiling the noodles, and drinking water, all.” They're particular about water too, which sounds delicious.

The broth is said to be extracted over 5 hours from domestic pork bones, Hidaka kelp, and domestic bonito flakes with aromatic vegetables. That sounds delicious too 😊

The meat is also cooked at low temperature for a gentle flavor, they say. This too is different from other shops, so I'm looking forward to it, lol

At the ticket machine, I decided to order the “Mixed Soba” introduced in the stamp rally booklet.

First, a sip of the blended broth of pork, kelp, and bonito, for which they're even particular about the water. A gentle flavor with the bonito stock of the blended broth coming through strongly. Just plain tasty 🙂

Next I tried the medium-thin straight special fresh noodles with fish powder. The noodles feel a little firm and hard.

They reminded me of the fresh noodles at “Ishigufu” and “Chodegwa.” They're similarly firm and hard, so preferences may divide depending on the person.

The braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and pork spare ribs (soki) toppings are a whitish color not found at other shops, perhaps because of low-temperature cooking. They were tender and lightly seasoned. Only a little shows in the photo, but there were 2 quail eggs, and this topping isn't found at other shops.

Rating (9/15): It's just plain tasty. The noodles are firm and hard, so preferences may differ depending on the person.

　* The shop has no parking lot, so you will need to park at a nearby coin parking lot.
