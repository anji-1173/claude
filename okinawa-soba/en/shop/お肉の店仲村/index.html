Oniku no Mise Nakamura

お肉の店仲村
Nakanishi, Urasoe CityRating 8/15

An Okinawa soba shop run by a pork specialty store. It uses Terukina Soba's handmade noodles, and the Special Soba topped with spare-rib-style soki, pig's feet (tebichi) and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) has big impact. Carefully selected pork and beef are also sold at the shop front.

I ate here in December 2023. This shop was a participant in the "13th Okinawa Soba Stamp Rally (2023.10.1–2024.2.29)", and I went as my 25th of 34 shops 😊

With this shop, it's hard to realize it's an Okinawa soba shop from the name alone (^0^;) But looking carefully, the cute pig on the sign is promoting Okinawa soba (lol)

*You can park about 4 cars in the parking lot out back.

I went straight into the shop and checked the menu. First there's an explanation of the noodles. It says, "We use Terukina Soba's handmade noodles." On top of that, these noodles "can't be bought at the supermarket", which gave me a sense of anticipation that they'd be delicious 😄

This time I decided to order the "Special Soba (medium)" introduced in the stamp rally booklet.

Being a pork specialty store, they have a lineup of rice bowls too 😁　In the photo of the bone soup, the toppings are out of control (^0^;)

True to a meat specialty store, they sell meat too lol　They handle not only pork but beef too 😆

Apparently carefully selected pork is butchered at this shop. I'm looking forward to the meat too 😄

It came 😁　The toppings have impact; as expected of a pork specialty store, there's soki like spare ribs, and pig's feet (tebichi) (pork trotters), which I haven't had in a while lol　Also thinly sliced braised pork belly (sanmai-niku).

But first, as usual, the broth. Thinking "Hm?", I tasted the broth several times with my spoon. It looks rich in color, but it's a quite light seasoning with a faint pork-bone-based stock.

Next, the braised pork belly (sanmai-niku). This too has a surprisingly light seasoning, and it feels like a little sinewy texture remains.

Next, I have the "Terukina Soba handmade noodles". These noodles are medium-thick and a straight type. On the soft side. Continuing with the toppings, the pig's feet (tebichi). These are tender and full of collagen, but the seasoning feels weak. The spare-rib-style soki also has tender meat, but the flavor feels light.

It wasn't placed on the table where I sat, but on the next table there was fuchiba (mugwort). In the second half I ate just a little with the noodles, and the faint bitterness makes a nice accent. I wish it had been placed on my table too.

Rating (8/15): Well, hearing it was Okinawa soba from a pork specialty store, I got my hopes up, but the broth felt weak in flavor, and while the meat had visual impact, I ended up thinking other shops' meat is just plain tastier.
