This time I'd like to introduce "Byakko Gaiden" in Minatogawa, Urasoe City, which I learned about from a local newspaper. I ate here in July 2024. An Okinawa soba specialty shop run by an owner who switched over from running a ramen shop, makes you curious, right?😉

"Byakko Gaiden," in Minatogawa Stateside Town, catches the eye with the stylish exterior of a renovated former foreigners' house. The light blue window frames accent the white walls, giving it a full-on Okinawan atmosphere!🏠✨ The surrounding foreigners' houses are also an area lined with stylish restaurants😄

When you go in, an interior based on pink and white welcomes you. The contrast with Okinawa's blue sky is lovely, and it makes you want to take photos📸

So, what I ordered this time was the "Nikumori Soba" (meat-loaded soba), which was also introduced in the newspaper article. As the ticket machine's description says, it seems to be the everything-on-top version with pork spare ribs (soki), pig's feet (tebichi) and braised pork belly (sanmai-niku).

A bowl topped with luxurious toppings was brought out.

The soup is a well-balanced flavor combining a seafood broth of bonito and mackerel with pork bone broth. I can feel a personality a cut above other Okinawa soba shops. Neither the seafood nor the pork bone flavor is too assertive; they harmonize gently, making it easy to drink😌

Next, the noodles😄　Actually, these noodles are really unique. They're homemade noodles using four kinds of wheat, and believe it or not, a few pink noodles kneaded with hibiscus are mixed in as an accent. Totally Okinawan, right?🌺 They're firm, and the texture is fun too.

The toppings are the classic Okinawa soba trio: pig's feet (tebichi), braised pork belly (sanmai-niku) and pork spare ribs (soki). They're lightly seasoned and don't get in the way of the soup and noodles. However, I thought they could have had a bit more presence.

The defining feature of "Byakko Gaiden" is, above all, its originality. You can sense an approach that makes use of skills honed at a ramen shop while also valuing the traditions of Okinawa soba. I think it's a bowl packed with the owner's passion and ingenuity💪

Rating (9.5/15): Overall, the distinctive taste and beauty of the noodles were impressive. The soup also has a flavor in a class apart from other shops, making me sense new possibilities for Okinawa soba. There may be some room for improvement in the toppings, but as a whole I think it's a well-balanced bowl.

"Byakko Gaiden" is a shop that values the traditions of Okinawa soba while trying to bring in a fresh breeze. I recommend it not only to Okinawa soba lovers, of course, but also to anyone interested in Okinawan cuisine😊

If you want to enjoy a stylish exterior and interior along with original Okinawa soba, please do visit "Byakko Gaiden." I'm sure you'll discover a new charm of Okinawa soba!
